## Job Title
Entry Level UX Designer

## Location
Chennai, India

## Client Industry
Manufacturing

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead end-to-end UX design for web and mobile products.
- Conduct user research including interviews, surveys, and usability testing.
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs using Figma.
- Collaborate with product managers and engineers to translate requirements into designs.
- Maintain and evolve the company's design system and component library.
- Present design concepts to stakeholders and incorporate feedback iteratively.
- Champion accessibility and inclusive design principles across all products.
- Analyse user behaviour data and heatmaps to inform design decisions.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Design Systems.
- User Research.
- Prototyping.
- Figma.
- Wireframing.

**Good to Have:**
- Adobe XD.
- HTML/CSS.
- Accessibility (WCAG).
- Motion Design.
- Usability Testing.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Design, HCI, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 0-1 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- Portfolio demonstrating strong UX process and outcomes required.
- Experience designing for mobile-first experiences.
- Familiarity with front-end development concepts.
