## Job Title
Lead Customer Success Manager

## Location
Dublin, Ireland

## Client Industry
Agriculture & AgriTech

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Manage a portfolio of enterprise and mid-market accounts to drive retention and growth.
- Lead customer onboarding, training, and adoption programmes.
- Conduct regular business reviews with customer stakeholders to demonstrate ROI.
- Proactively identify at-risk accounts and develop remediation plans.
- Partner with sales on renewal negotiations and expansion opportunities.
- Gather customer feedback and relay product insights to the product team.
- Track and report on customer health scores, NPS, and CSAT metrics.
- Develop and maintain customer success playbooks and best practices.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Stakeholder Communication.
- Account Management.
- Customer Onboarding.
- Churn Management.
- SaaS.

**Good to Have:**
- Product Adoption.
- Upselling.
- NPS/CSAT.
- Gainsight.
- Salesforce.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Communications, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 6-9 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- Experience using Gainsight or similar CS platforms.
- Background in SaaS or B2B technology preferred.
- Strong presentation and executive communication skills.
