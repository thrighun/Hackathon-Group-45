## Job Title
Data Scientist

## Location
Austin, USA

## Client Industry
Government & Public Sector

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Build and deploy machine learning models to solve complex business problems.
- Conduct exploratory data analysis and derive actionable insights from large datasets.
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams to integrate ML solutions into products.
- Design A/B experiments and interpret results to guide business decisions.
- Develop data pipelines and feature engineering workflows.
- Present findings and recommendations to senior stakeholders.
- Monitor model performance and implement retraining strategies.
- Stay current with advances in ML research and evaluate applicability to business needs.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- scikit-learn.
- Python.
- Machine Learning.
- Pandas.
- Statistics.

**Good to Have:**
- PyTorch.
- Spark.
- Tableau.
- NLP.
- TensorFlow.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Master's or PhD in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 2-4 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with cloud ML platforms (SageMaker, Vertex AI).
- Publications or kaggle competition track record is a plus.
- Familiarity with MLOps practices.
