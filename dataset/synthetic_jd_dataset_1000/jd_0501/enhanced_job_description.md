## Job Title
Staff Software Engineer

## Location
San Francisco, USA

## Client Industry
Information Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services and APIs.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define and ship product features.
- Write clean, well-tested code following best practices and code review standards.
- Participate in sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives.
- Debug production issues and implement performance optimizations.
- Contribute to technical documentation and internal knowledge base.
- Mentor junior engineers and support their professional growth.
- Evaluate and introduce new tools and frameworks to improve development velocity.

**Must Have:**
- Git.
- SQL.
- Agile/Scrum.
- Python.
- REST APIs.

**Good to Have:**
- Docker.
- AWS.
- Kubernetes.
- Redis.
- GraphQL.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or related field.

**Experience:**
- 8-12 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with microservices architecture.
- Exposure to CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitHub Actions).
- Knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS/GCP/Azure).
