## Job Title
Director QA Engineer

## Location
Pune, India

## Client Industry
Information Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and maintain automated test suites for web and mobile applications.
- Perform functional, regression, integration, and performance testing.
- Collaborate with developers to identify bugs early in the development cycle.
- Write clear and detailed bug reports and track resolution in JIRA.
- Participate in sprint ceremonies and contribute to Definition of Done.
- Develop and maintain test plans, test cases, and test data.
- Implement API testing strategies using Postman or RestAssured.
- Champion quality practices and shift-left testing approach across the team.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- API Testing.
- JIRA.
- Selenium/Cypress.
- Test Automation.
- SQL.

**Good to Have:**
- BDD/TDD.
- CI/CD.
- Performance Testing.
- Postman.
- Playwright.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 10+ years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- ISTQB certification preferred.
- Experience with performance testing tools (JMeter, Gatling).
- Knowledge of mobile testing (Appium, XCUITest) is a plus.
