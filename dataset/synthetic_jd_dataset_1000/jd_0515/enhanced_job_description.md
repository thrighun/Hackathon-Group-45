## Job Title
Principal Business Analyst

## Location
Chennai, India

## Client Industry
Pharmaceuticals

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Elicit, document, and validate business requirements from stakeholders.
- Analyse as-is processes and design optimised to-be workflows.
- Create detailed functional specifications, user stories, and acceptance criteria.
- Facilitate workshops and JAD sessions with business and technology teams.
- Support UAT planning and execution to ensure solutions meet requirements.
- Develop dashboards and reports to provide business insights to leadership.
- Track project milestones and communicate progress to stakeholders.
- Identify opportunities for process automation and digital transformation.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Requirements Gathering.
- JIRA/Confluence.
- Stakeholder Management.
- SQL.
- Process Mapping.

**Good to Have:**
- Tableau.
- Power BI.
- Agile.
- BPMN.
- Data Modelling.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Information Systems, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 8-12 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- CBAP or PMI-PBA certification preferred.
- Experience in Agile and waterfall delivery methodologies.
- Knowledge of ERP systems (SAP, Oracle) is advantageous.
