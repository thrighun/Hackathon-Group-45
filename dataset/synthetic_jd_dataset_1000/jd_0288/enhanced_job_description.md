## Job Title
Lead Financial Analyst

## Location
Toronto, Canada

## Client Industry
Hospitality & Tourism

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports and forecasts.
- Build and maintain financial models to support business decision-making.
- Conduct variance analysis and provide commentary on business performance.
- Partner with business units to develop annual budgets and rolling forecasts.
- Support the CFO and finance leadership with ad hoc analysis and presentations.
- Monitor key financial metrics and flag risks and opportunities proactively.
- Ensure accuracy and integrity of financial data across systems.
- Assist in month-end and year-end close processes.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Financial Modelling.
- Budgeting.
- FP&A.
- Variance Analysis.
- Excel.

**Good to Have:**
- SAP.
- IFRS/GAAP.
- DCF Valuation.
- SQL.
- Power BI.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 6-9 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- CFA, CPA, or CA qualification preferred.
- Experience with ERP systems (SAP, Oracle).
- Advanced Excel and financial modelling skills required.
