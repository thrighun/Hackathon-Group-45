## Job Title
Manager HR Business Partner

## Location
São Paulo, Brazil

## Client Industry
Healthcare & Life Sciences

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Partner with business leaders to develop and execute people strategies.
- Manage the full employee lifecycle including onboarding, development, and exits.
- Lead talent acquisition initiatives in collaboration with the recruitment team.
- Drive performance management cycles including goal-setting and reviews.
- Resolve complex employee relations issues with fairness and legal compliance.
- Analyse HR metrics and provide data-driven insights to leadership.
- Support organisational change management and restructuring initiatives.
- Design and deliver training programmes on leadership and HR topics.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Talent Management.
- Performance Management.
- Employee Relations.
- HR Policy.
- HRBP.

**Good to Have:**
- Succession Planning.
- Labour Law.
- HRIS (Workday/SAP).
- Org Design.
- Compensation & Benefits.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 5-10 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- SHRM-CP/SCP or CIPD certification preferred.
- Experience with HRIS platforms (Workday, SAP SuccessFactors).
- Knowledge of local labour laws and compliance requirements.
