## Job Title
Junior Operations Manager

## Location
San Francisco, USA

## Client Industry
Education & EdTech

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Oversee day-to-day operations and ensure service delivery meets SLAs.
- Identify and implement process improvements to drive efficiency and reduce costs.
- Manage and develop a team of operations specialists and analysts.
- Build and monitor operational KPIs and dashboards for leadership reporting.
- Collaborate with technology teams to automate manual processes.
- Manage vendor relationships and ensure contract compliance.
- Lead root cause analysis for operational failures and implement corrective actions.
- Drive a culture of continuous improvement across the operations function.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Operations Management.
- KPI Management.
- Lean/Six Sigma.
- Cross-functional Collaboration.
- Process Improvement.

**Good to Have:**
- Automation.
- Supply Chain.
- Vendor Management.
- ISO 9001.
- ERP Systems.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Engineering, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 1-3 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- Lean Six Sigma Green Belt or Black Belt certification preferred.
- Experience with ERP platforms (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite).
- PMP certification is an advantage.
