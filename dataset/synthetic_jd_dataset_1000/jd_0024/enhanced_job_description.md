## Job Title
Junior Sales Executive

## Location
Zurich, Switzerland

## Client Industry
Cybersecurity

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Identify, prospect, and close new business opportunities within assigned territory.
- Build and maintain a robust sales pipeline through outbound and inbound efforts.
- Conduct product demos and presentations to C-level and senior decision-makers.
- Negotiate contracts and pricing to close deals while maintaining healthy margins.
- Manage and grow existing client accounts to drive upsell and cross-sell revenue.
- Collaborate with marketing on lead generation campaigns and events.
- Maintain accurate records in CRM and provide weekly pipeline forecasts.
- Meet or exceed quarterly and annual sales quotas.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Negotiation.
- Prospecting.
- Pipeline Management.
- B2B Sales.
- CRM (Salesforce).

**Good to Have:**
- SaaS Sales.
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator.
- Cold Calling.
- Consultative Selling.
- Account Management.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Marketing, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 1-3 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- Experience selling enterprise SaaS solutions.
- Strong network in the target industry vertical.
- Ability to travel 30-40% as required.
