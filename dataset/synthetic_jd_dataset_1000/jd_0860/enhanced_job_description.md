## Job Title
Research Scientist

## Location
Amsterdam, Netherlands

## Client Industry
Media & Entertainment

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and execute research experiments to advance product or scientific objectives.
- Analyse complex datasets and publish findings in peer-reviewed journals.
- Collaborate with internal teams and external research partners on joint projects.
- Develop hypotheses, experimental protocols, and evaluation frameworks.
- Present research outcomes at conferences and to senior leadership.
- Maintain up-to-date knowledge of relevant scientific literature.
- Mentor junior researchers and oversee research integrity practices.
- Apply for grants and manage research budgets and timelines.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Scientific Writing.
- Data Analysis.
- Python/R.
- Research Methodology.
- Literature Review.

**Good to Have:**
- Statistical Modelling.
- Bioinformatics.
- Lab techniques.
- Grant Writing.
- Machine Learning.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- PhD in a relevant scientific discipline; post-doctoral experience preferred.

**Experience:**
- 2-4 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- Publication record in peer-reviewed journals required.
- Experience with industry-academia collaboration.
- Familiarity with ethical research guidelines and IRB processes.
