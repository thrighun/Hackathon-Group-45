## Job Title
Entry Level Cloud Architect

## Location
Zurich, Switzerland

## Client Industry
Automotive

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and own the cloud architecture strategy across multiple products and teams.
- Evaluate and select cloud services and technologies aligned to business needs.
- Define cloud security, compliance, and governance standards.
- Lead cloud migration projects from on-premise to cloud-native architectures.
- Conduct architecture reviews and provide technical guidance to engineering teams.
- Optimise cloud spend through FinOps practices and right-sizing initiatives.
- Develop reference architectures, playbooks, and technical documentation.
- Mentor engineers on cloud best practices and emerging technologies.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Infrastructure as Code.
- Microservices.
- AWS/Azure/GCP.
- Security & Compliance.
- Cloud Architecture.

**Good to Have:**
- Serverless.
- Multi-cloud.
- FinOps.
- Kubernetes.
- Cost Optimisation.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 0-1 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- AWS Solutions Architect Professional / Azure Solutions Architect Expert preferred.
- Experience designing multi-cloud or hybrid cloud solutions.
- Familiarity with SOC 2, ISO 27001, or HIPAA compliance.
