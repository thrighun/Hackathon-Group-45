## Job Title
Staff Marketing Manager

## Location
Noida, India

## Client Industry
Energy & Utilities

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and execute integrated marketing campaigns across digital and offline channels.
- Manage the marketing calendar, budget, and resource allocation.
- Lead content strategy including blogs, whitepapers, case studies, and social media.
- Drive SEO/SEM initiatives to increase organic and paid traffic.
- Collaborate with sales to align on pipeline goals and lead quality.
- Analyse campaign performance and optimise ROI using data-driven insights.
- Manage relationships with external agencies and vendors.
- Build and maintain brand guidelines and ensure consistent brand voice.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Google Analytics.
- SEO/SEM.
- Campaign Management.
- Content Strategy.
- Digital Marketing.

**Good to Have:**
- Social Media.
- HubSpot.
- Marketing Automation.
- Brand Management.
- Copywriting.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 8-12 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with marketing automation tools (HubSpot, Marketo).
- Google Analytics and Google Ads certification is a plus.
- B2B SaaS marketing experience preferred.
