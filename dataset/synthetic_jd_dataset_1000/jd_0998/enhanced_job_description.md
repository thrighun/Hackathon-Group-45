## Job Title
VP Project Manager

## Location
Ahmedabad, India

## Client Industry
Gaming

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead end-to-end delivery of complex projects across multiple workstreams.
- Define project scope, schedule, budget, and resource plans.
- Identify, assess, and mitigate project risks and issues.
- Facilitate daily standups, steering committee meetings, and governance forums.
- Manage project budgets and provide accurate financial forecasting.
- Communicate project status, risks, and decisions to executive stakeholders.
- Drive change management activities to ensure smooth adoption of deliverables.
- Conduct project retrospectives and embed lessons learned into future delivery.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Stakeholder Communication.
- Risk Management.
- MS Project/JIRA.
- Project Management.
- PMP/Prince2.

**Good to Have:**
- PMO.
- Change Management.
- Resource Planning.
- Agile/Scrum.
- Budget Management.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Engineering, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 12+ years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- PMP, Prince2, or CAPM certification required.
- Experience delivering digital transformation or technology projects.
- Proficiency in MS Project, JIRA, or Smartsheet.
