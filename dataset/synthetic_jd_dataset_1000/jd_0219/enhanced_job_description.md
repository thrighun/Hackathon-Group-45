## Job Title
Fresher Legal Counsel

## Location
Noida, India

## Client Industry
Aerospace & Defence

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Draft, review, and negotiate a wide range of commercial contracts.
- Provide legal advice to business units on regulatory and compliance matters.
- Manage relationships with external law firms and control legal spend.
- Monitor changes in applicable laws and regulations and advise leadership.
- Support M&A transactions including due diligence and deal structuring.
- Develop and maintain standard contract templates and legal playbooks.
- Conduct legal training for employees on compliance and ethics topics.
- Represent the company in dispute resolution and litigation proceedings.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Corporate Law.
- Legal Research.
- Regulatory Affairs.
- Compliance.
- Contract Drafting.

**Good to Have:**
- GDPR.
- IP Law.
- Litigation Management.
- M&A.
- Employment Law.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- LLB or LLM degree; admission to the Bar in applicable jurisdiction required.

**Experience:**
- 0-1 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in a reputable law firm or in-house legal team.
- Knowledge of GDPR, data protection, and privacy laws.
- Exposure to intellectual property and technology law is advantageous.
