## Job Title
Lead Data Engineer

## Location
Mumbai, India

## Client Industry
Government & Public Sector

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and build robust, scalable data pipelines for batch and streaming workloads.
- Develop and maintain data models in the cloud data warehouse.
- Collaborate with data scientists and analysts to understand data requirements.
- Ensure data quality, lineage, and governance across the data platform.
- Optimise query performance and data processing costs.
- Implement data cataloguing and metadata management practices.
- Support migration of on-premise data systems to cloud-native architectures.
- Document data architecture, pipelines, and data dictionaries.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Data Warehousing.
- SQL.
- ETL/ELT.
- Apache Spark.
- Python.

**Good to Have:**
- dbt.
- Airflow.
- Kafka.
- Snowflake.
- AWS Glue.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 6-9 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with modern data stack (dbt, Airflow, Snowflake/BigQuery/Redshift).
- AWS or GCP Data Engineer certification preferred.
- Knowledge of data governance frameworks.
