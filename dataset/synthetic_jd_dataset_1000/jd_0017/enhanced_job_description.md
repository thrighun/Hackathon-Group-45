## Job Title
Fresher Supply Chain Manager

## Location
Sydney, Australia

## Client Industry
Government & Public Sector

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Manage end-to-end supply chain operations from procurement to last-mile delivery.
- Develop demand forecasts and collaborate with commercial teams on S&OP.
- Manage supplier relationships and conduct regular performance reviews.
- Optimise inventory levels to balance service levels and working capital.
- Identify and mitigate supply chain risks including geopolitical and capacity risks.
- Lead continuous improvement initiatives to reduce costs and lead times.
- Ensure compliance with import/export regulations and trade compliance.
- Develop supply chain KPIs and report performance to senior leadership.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- Supply Chain Management.
- ERP Systems.
- Demand Planning.
- Inventory Control.
- Vendor Management.

**Good to Have:**
- SAP SCM.
- APICS/CSCP.
- S&OP.
- Logistics.
- Lean Manufacturing.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain Management, Engineering, or related field.

**Experience:**
- 0-1 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- APICS CSCP or CPIM certification preferred.
- Experience with SAP SCM or Oracle SCM.
- Knowledge of customs, trade regulations, and 3PL management.
