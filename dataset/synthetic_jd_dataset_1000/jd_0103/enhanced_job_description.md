## Job Title
Staff Product Manager

## Location
Singapore, Singapore

## Client Industry
Finance & Banking

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Define and own the product roadmap aligned to company strategy and customer needs.
- Gather and prioritize product requirements through user interviews and data analysis.
- Work closely with engineering, design, and marketing to deliver high-quality features.
- Define success metrics and track KPIs for all product initiatives.
- Communicate product vision and strategy to leadership and stakeholders.
- Conduct competitive analysis and market research to identify opportunities.
- Lead sprint planning and backlog grooming sessions.
- Drive go-to-market strategy for new product launches.

## Skill Requirements
**Must Have:**
- User Research.
- Product Roadmapping.
- Data Analysis.
- Agile.
- Stakeholder Management.

**Good to Have:**
- A/B Testing.
- JIRA.
- OKRs.
- SQL.
- Figma.

**Educational Qualifications:**
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Engineering, or related field; MBA preferred.

**Experience:**
- 8-12 years of relevant professional experience.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with SaaS or consumer product development.
- Familiarity with UX design principles.
- Prior experience in a technical or analytical role is advantageous.
