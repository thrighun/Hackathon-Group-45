## Job Title
Senior Software Engineer - Java

## Location
Bangalore or Hyderabad

## Client Industry
Software Development Firm

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and maintain scalable enterprise-level applications in Java, utilizing the Spring Framework and RESTful APIs to meet evolving business requirements.
- Conduct rigorous testing including unit, integration, and performance testing to ensure application reliability and robustness.
- Collaborate closely with product managers, designers, and other developers to gather detailed requirements and contribute to defining the project roadmap.
- Troubleshoot and debug complex application issues, providing efficient and innovative solutions to enhance system performance.
- Foster a culture of collaboration and continuous improvement within the development team, participating in code reviews and knowledge sharing sessions.
- Implement best practices in software development, emphasizing clean coding standards, documentation, and version control.
- Stay abreast of industry trends and emerging technologies to infuse cutting-edge solutions into projects.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Minimum of 3+ years of professional experience in Java application development.
- Strong knowledge of Spring Framework and RESTful service design.
- Familiarity with relational databases (like MySQL or PostgreSQL) as well as NoSQL databases (like MongoDB or Cassandra).
- Excellent analytical skills with a strong attention to detail.
- Ability to work effectively in a team-oriented environment, demonstrating strong interpersonal and collaborative skills.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with CI/CD tools like Jenkins, GitLab CI, or CircleCI.
- Understanding of reactive programming principles and frameworks.
- Experience with Agile methodologies and tools like JIRA.
- Certifications in Java or related frameworks would be a plus.
