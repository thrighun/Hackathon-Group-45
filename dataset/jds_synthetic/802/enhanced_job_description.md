## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop predictive models and advanced analytics solutions to address business challenges.
- Conduct exploratory data analysis to identify trends, patterns, and anomalies in large datasets.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics.
- Utilize machine learning techniques to enhance data-driven decision-making processes.
- Communicate findings and recommendations effectively through visualizations and reports.
- Research and implement new methodologies and algorithms to improve data analysis processes.
- Maintain and update existing models to ensure accuracy and relevance over time.
- Participate in data governance initiatives to ensure data integrity and quality across the organization.
- Provide mentorship to junior data professionals and assist in their professional growth.

- Expertise in programming languages such as R, Python, or SQL for data manipulation and analysis.
- Strong understanding of statistical methods and their application in business contexts.
- Experience with machine learning libraries (e.g., Scikit-learn, TensorFlow) and data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI).
- Familiarity with big data technologies such as Spark and distributed computing environments.
- Knowledge of cloud-based analytics platforms (e.g., AWS Redshift, Google BigQuery).
- Strong problem-solving and analytical thinking abilities.
- Excellent communication skills to convey complex data findings to non-technical stakeholders.
- Master's or Ph.D. degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related field.
