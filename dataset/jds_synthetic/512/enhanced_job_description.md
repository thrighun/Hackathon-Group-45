- Lead the design and architecture of innovative ServiceNow solutions tailored to meet complex business requirements.
- Collaborate closely with business analysts, project managers, and IT teams to gather and document detailed solution requirements.
- Mentor and guide junior technical staff in ServiceNow best practices, fostering skill development and knowledge sharing.

- Minimum of 3 years of experience in ServiceNow architecture or solution design.

- Expertise in ServiceNow platform architecture, integration methodologies, and security protocols.
- Strong leadership, project management, and communication skills for cross-functional collaboration.
