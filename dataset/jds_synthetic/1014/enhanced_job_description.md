- Lead the architecture and design of ServiceNow solutions to meet business requirements effectively.
- Conduct workshops and meetings with key stakeholders to understand their requirements and translate them into functional specifications.
- Oversee the implementation of ServiceNow capabilities, ensuring best practices are followed throughout the lifecycle.
- Collaborate with development teams to guide the technical implementation and integration of various ServiceNow modules.
- Create and maintain architecture documentation that includes solution designs, integration patterns, and data flow diagrams.
- Evaluate and recommend new ServiceNow features and applications that could enhance operational effectiveness.
- Monitor system performance and provide recommendations for optimizations based on usage and feedback.
- Mentor junior architects and developers, fostering a culture of continuous improvement and learning within the team.

- Deep understanding of the ServiceNow platform and experience in designing scalable architectures.
- Proficiency in REST APIs and integration methodologies applicable to ServiceNow.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills to assess and address complex business requirements.
- Experience in leading cross-functional teams in the implementation of ServiceNow solutions.
- Minimum of 10 years of experience in IT service management and solution architecture.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- ServiceNow certification (Architect level preferred) is a significant advantage.
