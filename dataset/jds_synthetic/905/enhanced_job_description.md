## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and maintain scalable machine learning infrastructure for deploying and managing models in production.
- Monitor model performance and reliability, implementing automated alerts and reporting systems.
- Collaborate closely with data scientists to ensure smooth handoff and integration of ML models into production systems.
- Automate data ingestion and preprocessing workflows to enhance efficiency and reduce manual intervention.
- Develop and maintain comprehensive documentation for model operations, best practices, and troubleshooting procedures.
- Conduct regular model performance evaluations and contribute to model retraining initiatives as necessary.
- Stay informed about industry trends and emerging technologies to enhance operational capabilities.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Extensive experience in deploying machine learning models in cloud environments (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud).
- Proficiency in containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Strong skills in programming with Python and familiarity with ML frameworks (TensorFlow, Scikit-learn).
- Experience with CI/CD tools and version control systems (Git).
- Knowledge of monitoring tools (e.g., Prometheus, Grafana) for maintaining model performance.
- Excellent analytical skills with attention to detail and the ability to troubleshoot complex systems.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in designing data pipelines using ETL tools or frameworks (Apache Airflow, Luigi).
- Understanding of data security and compliance regulations in machine learning.
- Familiarity with serverless architectures and microservices.
