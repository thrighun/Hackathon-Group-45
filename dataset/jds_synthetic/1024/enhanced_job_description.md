## Job Title
AI/ML Model Evaluator

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Evaluate the performance of AI and machine learning models in production.
- Collaborate with data scientists to identify areas of improvement and implement model enhancements.
- Design and implement data validation pipelines to ensure data integrity and quality.
- Conduct A/B testing to compare model performance and effectiveness.
- Develop anomaly detection methods to monitor for unexpected behaviors in AI applications.
- Ensure compliance with regulatory standards applicable to AI technologies.
- Provide insights based on model evaluations to guide strategic decisions.

- Strong background in AI/ML model evaluation and optimization techniques.
- Experienced in A/B testing methodologies and statistical analysis.
- Proficient in designing data validation frameworks and processes.
- Familiarity with machine learning interpretability tools and techniques.
- Ability to work collaboratively in a team-oriented environment.
- Educational qualifications in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field.
- 3-10 years of relevant experience.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of industry regulations and standards for AI applications.
- Familiarity with monitoring tools and technologies for AI systems.
- Excellent analytical skills and attention to detail.
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills.
