## Detailed Responsibilities
- Conduct systematic product inspections to verify adherence to quality standards and specifications.
- Document findings and create reports detailing quality levels and issues identified during inspections.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including production, development, and project management to address quality concerns.
- Evaluate manufacturing and software processes to recommend improvements for quality assurance.
- Implement quality control measures and develop quality specifications to guide production.
- Participate in audits and prepare for regulatory inspections to ensure compliance with industry standards.
- Continuously seek opportunities to enhance product quality and operational efficiency.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Quality Assurance, Engineering, or a related field.
- Proven experience as a QC Analyst or similar position.
- Knowledge of quality control methodologies, tools, and processes.
- Familiarity with industry standards for quality assurance and regulatory compliance.
- Strong analytical skills to evaluate inspection results and suggest improvements.
- Excellent communication skills for effective teamwork and reporting.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with statistical quality control techniques.
- Knowledge of software quality assurance principles is a plus.
- Certification in Quality Control (e.g., Six Sigma) is preferred.
