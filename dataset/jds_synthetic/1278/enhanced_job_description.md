## Job Title
Senior Software Engineer - Embedded Systems

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop embedded software solutions for various hardware platforms, ensuring high reliability and performance.
- Collaborate closely with hardware engineers to integrate and optimize software for specific embedded applications.
- Perform system-level analysis and debugging to identify and resolve software and hardware issues.
- Lead efforts in the optimization of code for constrained environments, focusing on memory and processing efficiency.
- Mentor junior engineers and provide technical leadership within the team.
- Create and maintain comprehensive technical documentation related to systems design, architecture, and user manuals.
- Stay informed about emerging technologies and advancements in embedded systems engineering.

## Skill Requirements
- 6-12 years of experience in embedded systems software development, specifically using C/C++.
- Strong knowledge of real-time operating systems (RTOS) and low-level hardware interfacing.
- Familiarity with embedded development tools, debuggers, and simulators.
- Experience with communication protocols such as SPI, I2C, and UART.
- Excellent analytical and troubleshooting skills, with the ability to work proactively in a team environment.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with scripting languages like Python or Shell scripting is advantageous.
- Knowledge of hardware design principles and PCB layout design is a plus.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills to effectively convey technical concepts.
