## Job Title
Frontend Developer

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop dynamic web applications using Angular framework.
- Collaborate with UX/UI teams to translate design wireframes into high-quality code.
- Ensure high performance and responsiveness of applications by implementing optimizations.
- Conduct thorough code reviews to maintain code quality and share knowledge with the team.
- Integrate third-party APIs and backend services to enhance application functionality.
- Actively participate in agile ceremonies, including daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and retrospectives.

## Skill Requirements
- At least 4 years of experience in frontend development with a focus on Angular.
- Strong understanding of TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS3.
- Familiarity with state management libraries such as NgRx or Redux.
- Experience with build tools and task runners like Gulp or Grunt.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Solid analytical and troubleshooting skills.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of responsive design principles and mobile-first development.
- Familiarity with RESTful services and JSON for data interchange.
- Understanding of version control systems, preferably Git.
- Capability to work independently and in a team-oriented, collaborative environment.
