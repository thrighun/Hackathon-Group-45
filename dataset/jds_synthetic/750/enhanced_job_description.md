## Job Title
Backend Software Engineer

## Location
Remote or On-site

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement scalable backend services to support diverse applications.
- Develop and maintain RESTful APIs, ensuring high performance and responsiveness.
- Collaborate with DevOps teams to automate deployment and monitoring processes.
- Manage database systems, including schema design, optimization, and query performance tuning.
- Work closely with cross-functional teams, including frontend developers and product managers, to define clear requirements and deliver high-quality software.
- Debug and resolve issues in production environments, ensuring minimal disruption to users.
- Conduct regular security assessments and optimization of backend applications.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong experience in backend programming languages such as Go, Ruby, or PHP.
- Proficiency in designing and implementing database solutions with SQL and NoSQL databases.
- Hands-on experience with cloud services (AWS, Azure, GCP) and serverless architectures.
- Familiarity with containerization technologies like Docker and orchestration tools like Kubernetes.
- Strong understanding of API design and microservices architecture.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related technical field.
- Excellent analytical skills and a methodical approach to problem-solving.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with version control systems such as Git.
- Knowledge of unit testing and test-driven development practices.
- Familiarity with agile methodologies and participation in sprint planning and reviews.
- Ability to mentor junior team members and contribute to a learning culture.
