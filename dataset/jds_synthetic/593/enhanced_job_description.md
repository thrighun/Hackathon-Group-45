## Job Title
Node.js Developer with Express.js

## Location
Remote or On-site

## Client Industry
Web Development Agency

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Build and maintain server-side applications using Node.js and Express.js.
- Design and implement RESTful APIs for front-end consumption.
- Work closely with front-end developers to integrate user-facing elements with server-side logic.
- Optimize application performance and ensure the high availability of services.
- Conduct rigorous testing and debugging processes to enhance code reliability.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to gather requirements and develop solutions that meet business needs.
- Stay informed on emerging technologies and best practices in server-side development.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field.
- 4-6 years of experience in Node.js and Express.js development.
- Proficient in JavaScript and have a solid understanding of asynchronous programming.
- Strong experience with MongoDB or other NoSQL databases.
- Familiarity with version control systems, especially Git.
- Excellent analytical and troubleshooting skills with attention to detail.
- Proven ability to work effectively in a team-oriented environment.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of authentication and authorization mechanisms in web applications.
- Experience with testing frameworks such as Mocha or Jest.
- Understanding of deployment processes, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud services.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills to convey technical information to various stakeholders.
