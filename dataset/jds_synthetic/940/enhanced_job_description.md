## Job Title
AI/ML Solutions Architect

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design scalable AI-driven architectures for data analysis and intelligent automation.
- Supervise the deployment and integration of machine learning models in production settings.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to gather and define technical and business requirements.
- Drive technical best practices in AI solution development and deployment.
- Manage data workflows and ensure data quality for AI applications.
- Facilitate the agile development process across cross-functional teams.
- Monitor and evaluate the performance of AI models, making recommendations for improvements.
- Ensure compliance with regulatory standards and industry best practices in AI development.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field.
- 5-10 years of experience in AI/ML architecture and solutions design.
- Proven experience in MLOps and deploying machine learning models at scale.
- Strong understanding of cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python or Java, and familiarity with AI/ML libraries.
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Solid understanding of software architecture principles and agile methodologies.
- Insight into data governance and ethical AI practices.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with containerization and orchestration tools (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes).
- Knowledge of data visualization tools and techniques.
- Familiarity with machine learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Certifications in AI/ML architecture or related fields.
