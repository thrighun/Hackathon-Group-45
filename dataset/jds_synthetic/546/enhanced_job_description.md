## Detailed Responsibilities
- **Solution Development**: Create and implement machine learning models for various NLP tasks aimed at enhancing user experiences and extracting valuable business insights.
- **Model Training and Evaluation**: Train and meticulously evaluate NLP models using extensive datasets, focusing on achieving high accuracy in tasks like sentiment analysis and intent recognition.
- **Chatbot Development**: Design and develop sophisticated conversational agents utilizing frameworks such as Rasa or Dialogflow, facilitating seamless automated customer interactions.
- **Data Preprocessing and Annotation**: Manage the preprocessing of text data and collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure effective data annotation, contributing to improved model performance.
- **Collaboration and Reporting**: Partner with product and engineering teams to integrate cutting-edge NLP features into applications, providing regular updates on project progress and outcomes.

## Skill Requirements
- **Technical Skills**: Expertise in Python and machine learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch, with a strong focus on NLP techniques.
- **Experience**: 5-7 years of relevant experience in AI and machine learning, particularly within natural language processing, with demonstrable success in developing and deploying models.
- **Educational Background**: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, or a related field.

## Other Requirements
- Strong analytical skills and proficiency in data visualization tools such as Tableau or Power BI.
- Ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced environment and communicate complex ideas to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
