## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement comprehensive test strategies and systems for software applications.
- Conduct thorough manual and automated testing to validate software functionality.
- Collaborate closely with development teams during the development lifecycle to ensure seamless quality integration.
- Lead test case reviews and troubleshooting efforts for software issues.
- Generate clear and actionable testing documentation for the quality assurance process.
- Engage in continuous learning to enhance testing techniques and tools.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Minimum of 3 years of experience in software quality assurance or related field.
- Expertise in both manual and automated testing frameworks.
- Familiarity with continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) practices.
- Strong analytical skills with a meticulous eye for detail.
- Proven ability to communicate effectively within teams and with stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with performance testing tools and methodologies.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Java or JavaScript.
- Relevant certification (e.g., ISTQB or equivalent) is a plus.
