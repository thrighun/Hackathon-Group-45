## Detailed Responsibilities
- Oversee the configuration and maintenance of document management systems to ensure accessibility and security.
- Develop, implement, and manage efficient workflow processes for document creation, review, approval, and storage.
- Provide expert technical assistance regarding document handling and workflow systems to users across departments.
- Troubleshoot issues related to document retrieval, editing, and workflow automation, ensuring minimal disruption.
- Manage user permissions, access controls, and compliance requirements for document management systems.
- Create and update operational documents, user manuals, and training materials for document management best practices.
- Work closely with IT, compliance, and various business units to align document practices with organizational goals.
- Ensure that all document management practices comply with relevant regulations and organizational policies.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Business Administration, or a related field (or equivalent experience).
- Prior experience in document management or workflow optimization roles, preferably in a corporate environment.
- Strong understanding of document management systems and workflow automation tools.
- Excellent analytical and troubleshooting skills to resolve complex document and workflow issues.
- Proficiency with compliance standards related to document management (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills for effective collaboration with various departments.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with cloud-based document management solutions and electronic signature tools is a plus.
- Experience in project management and process improvement methodologies (e.g., Lean, Six Sigma) can be advantageous.
