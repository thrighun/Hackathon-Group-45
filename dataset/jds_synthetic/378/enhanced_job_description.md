## Detailed Responsibilities
- Collaborate with product management and engineering teams to gather user insights and develop design specifications that drive user experience improvements.
- Create and iterate wireframes, mockups, and prototypes using CAD tools and front-end technologies to visualize design concepts.
- Conduct user research and usability testing, analyzing feedback to iteratively enhance design solutions that meet user needs and expectations.
- Implement design best practices to create a cohesive and user-friendly interface, leading to increased user satisfaction and engagement.
- Develop and maintain a comprehensive design documentation repository that aids in project tracking and stakeholder communication.
- Mentor junior designers, sharing knowledge and best practices to foster professional growth within the design team.

## Skill Requirements
- Proven experience in UX design with a strong portfolio showcasing user-centered design projects.
- Proficiency in CAD software to integrate design solutions effectively within technical contexts.
- Solid front-end development skills, including HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript knowledge to communicate effectively with development teams.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to approach design challenges strategically.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal abilities to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams.
- A keen understanding of design systems and methodologies, with attention to detail to ensure design quality.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with UX research methodologies and tools.
- Ability to work in agile project environments and adapt to changing priorities.
- Experience in creating accessible and inclusive design solutions.
- Bachelor's degree in Design, Human-Computer Interaction, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience).
