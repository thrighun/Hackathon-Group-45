## Job Title
Quality Assurance Engineer

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement automated testing solutions to improve overall software quality.
- Conduct performance testing using JMeter to identify system bottlenecks.
- Utilize Selenium WebDriver for functional testing of web applications.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to establish testing requirements and ensure alignment with project objectives.
- Create and maintain detailed documentation of testing processes, including automation scripts and test results.
- Analyze test results and communicate findings to stakeholders to facilitate timely issue resolution.
- Act as a key contributor in continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) initiatives to streamline testing processes.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong knowledge of automation testing frameworks and tools.
- Proficient in Selenium WebDriver, JMeter, and Postman for API testing.
- Experience with performance testing methodologies and tools.
- Excellent troubleshooting skills and attention to detail.
- Strong communication and teamwork capabilities.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in a quality assurance role.

## Other Requirements
- ISTQB certification or equivalent certification is preferred.
- Familiarity with Agile and DevOps practices is a plus.
- Experience with version control systems, such as Git.
