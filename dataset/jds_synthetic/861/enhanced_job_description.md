## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the design and development of innovative mobile applications that leverage AI technologies to deliver high-impact user experiences.
- Collaborate with product managers and designers to conceptualize and implement features that drive customer engagement.
- Utilize Agile methodologies to manage projects, ensuring timely delivery and adherence to quality standards.

- Engage in big data management strategies to handle and analyze large datasets, enabling data-driven decision-making.

- Experience with AI technologies and machine learning libraries, and how they can be applied in mobile solutions.
- Familiarity with Agile development practices and project management tools (e.g., JIRA, Trello).
- Strong knowledge of RESTful APIs and microservices architecture.
- Understanding of big data technologies (Hadoop, Spark) and data visualization tools.
- Exceptional problem-solving skills and the ability to communicate complex technical concepts effectively.
