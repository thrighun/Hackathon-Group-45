## Detailed Responsibilities
- Evaluate and validate automotive components through rigorous testing methodologies in areas such as Electrical Systems, Safety Components, Thermal Management, Transmission Systems, and Body Structures.
- Develop comprehensive test plans and execute performance evaluations to ascertain the reliability and safety of automotive products.
- Utilize simulation software like ANSYS or MATLAB to predict component behavior under various conditions.
- Analyze test results, prepare detailed reports, and present findings to cross-functional teams to inform design improvements.
- Ensure compliance with automotive regulatory standards and quality assurance protocols throughout the testing process.
- Collaborate with design engineers to provide feedback and drive enhancements in product design based on test outcomes.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- Over 3 years of experience in design verification within the automotive industry.
- Proficient in simulation and analysis software, particularly ANSYS or MATLAB.
- Familiarity with testing methodologies and performance evaluation techniques for automotive components.
- Strong knowledge of regulatory standards related to automotive safety and quality assurance.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.

## Other Requirements
- Strong communication skills and ability to work in a team environment.
- Experience in statistical analysis methods for data interpretation.
- Capacity to manage multiple tasks and projects while adhering to deadlines.
