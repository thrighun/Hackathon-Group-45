## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the design and deployment of scalable and secure cloud infrastructures for healthcare applications, ensuring alignment with industry regulations such as HIPAA and HITECH.
- Collaborate with technical and clinical teams to gather requirements and translate them into robust cloud solutions that drive efficiencies in patient data management and clinical workflows.
- Assess and optimize existing cloud environments, providing recommendations for enhancements in performance, reliability, and cost-efficiency.
- Conduct risk assessments and develop comprehensive security frameworks to protect sensitive healthcare data in the cloud.
- Mentor and support junior cloud engineers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement within the team.
- Research and evaluate new cloud technologies and methodologies to ensure the organization's cloud strategy remains innovative and competitive.

## Skill Requirements
- At least 8+ years of hands-on experience in cloud architecture, particularly with platforms like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
- Deep knowledge of cloud security best practices and compliance standards applicable to the healthcare industry.
- Proven track record in designing APIs and microservices that integrate seamlessly with existing healthcare systems.
- Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field; relevant cloud certifications preferred.
- Strong analytical skills and the ability to communicate complex cloud concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with infrastructure as code (IaC) tools such as Terraform or AWS CloudFormation is desirable.
- Familiarity with data analytics tools and techniques in the context of healthcare data.
- Excellent interpersonal skills, with a focus on teamwork and collaboration across diverse groups.
