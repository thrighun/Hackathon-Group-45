## Job Title
AI Product Manager

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the entire product lifecycle for AI-driven solutions, from concept to launch and iteration.
- Collaborate closely with AI/ML engineers to translate technical capabilities into marketable features.
- Conduct market analysis to identify trends, user needs, and competitive landscape to inform product direction.
- Create and maintain product roadmaps, prioritizing features based on customer feedback and business objectives.
- Develop comprehensive product requirements and specifications to guide development teams.
- Act as the primary liaison between technical teams and stakeholders to ensure alignment on project goals.
- Monitor product performance post-launch, gathering user feedback and making data-driven recommendations for enhancements.
- Champion best practices in user experience design to ensure AI features are intuitive and valuable to users.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Business, or a related field.
- Minimum of 6 years of experience in product management, with at least 2 years focused on AI/ML products.
- Strong familiarity with AI technologies and their application in product development.
- Proficiency in data analysis and visualization tools to derive insights from user data.
- Exceptional communication skills to present product concepts succinctly to both technical and non-technical audiences.
- Experience with Agile methodologies and product management tools (e.g., JIRA, Trello).

## Other Requirements
- Background in user experience design and customer-centric product development.
- Knowledge of software development processes and collaboration with engineering teams.
- Experience in a fast-paced, multinational technology environment.
- Understanding of ethical considerations in AI product development.
