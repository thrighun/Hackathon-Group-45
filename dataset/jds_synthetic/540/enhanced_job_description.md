## Job Title
Automotive Systems Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Global Automotive Manufacturer

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Leverage Siemens NX or SolidWorks for detailed 3D CAD modeling, assembly design, and technical drawings related to automotive systems.
- Collaborate on the design and integration of automotive electrical systems, including components like engine systems, transmission systems, and ECUs.
- Participate in the product design lifecycle, ensuring designs meet performance, safety, and regulatory requirements.
- Utilize knowledge of manufacturing processes, including additive manufacturing and stamping, to optimize designs for manufacturability.
- Conduct Finite Element Analysis (FEA) and simulation to validate design integrity and performance under various conditions.
- Facilitate design reviews and provide actionable feedback to enhance design quality and functionality.
- Prepare comprehensive technical documentation and reports for internal and external stakeholders.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in Siemens NX or SolidWorks software.
- Extensive experience in 3D CAD modeling, assembly design, and creation of technical drawings.
- Understanding of system integration for automotive electrical components.
- Familiar with manufacturing practices, including additive manufacturing and stamping.
- Knowledge of FEA and simulation methodologies.
- Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- Experience with automotive industry standards and regulations is a plus.

## Other Requirements
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
- Capability to manage multiple projects and meet deadlines efficiently.
