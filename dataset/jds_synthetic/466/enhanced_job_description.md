## Detailed Responsibilities
- Collaborate with project managers to assess and prioritize project resource requirements, ensuring timely and effective assignment of personnel.
- Maintain a dynamic inventory of internal talent, efficiently tracking team members' availability and skill sets for optimal project allocation.
- Generate and analyze resource utilization reports and dashboards, providing actionable insights to management for strategic decision-making.
- Assist in the onboarding and integration of new team members, ensuring their smooth transition into ongoing projects.
- Foster communication with various departments to ensure alignment and support for resource mobilization across projects.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong organizational capabilities with a focus on resource management.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills for effective collaboration.
- Basic knowledge of project management methodologies and software.
- Proficient in Microsoft Office Suite, especially Excel and relevant project management tools.
- Ability to thrive in a dynamic and fast-paced work environment.

## Other Requirements
- A bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Human Resources, Project Management, or a related discipline is preferred.
- Previous internship or work experience in project coordination or human resources is advantageous.
- Familiarity with workforce planning tools and resource management systems is a plus.
