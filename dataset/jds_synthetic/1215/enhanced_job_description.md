## Job Title
Senior Software Engineer - Backend Services

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain robust backend services using Java and Kotlin in a cloud-native environment.
- Collaborate with product managers and UX designers to understand user requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
- Design and implement microservices that communicate over REST and gRPC protocols.
- Conduct thorough unit and integration testing to ensure the functionality and performance of applications.
- Lead architectural discussions and propose innovative solutions to enhance scalability and reliability.
- Mentor and coach junior engineers to foster their technical and professional growth.
- Analyze system performance and implement optimizations based on user feedback and performance metrics.
- Participate in Agile ceremonies, contributing to iterative design and development processes.
- Stay abreast of new technologies and industry trends, incorporating them into development practices where appropriate.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a closely related field.
- A minimum of 6 years of experience in software development with a deep understanding of Java and microservices.
- Proficiency in Kotlin is a strong advantage.
- Solid understanding of cloud services (AWS, Azure, or GCP) and their integration.
- Extensive experience with API design, management, and documentation tools.
- Familiarity with front-end technologies is a plus (React, Angular).
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail.
- Exceptional communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
- Experience using CI/CD tools and methodologies.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of database technologies like SQL and NoSQL (e.g., MongoDB).
- Experience with container orchestration using Kubernetes or OpenShift.
- Familiarity with DevOps practices and tools.
- Relevant certifications in cloud technologies or software development methodologies is an advantage.
