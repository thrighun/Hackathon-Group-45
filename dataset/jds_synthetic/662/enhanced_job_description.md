## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement firmware for a variety of microcontrollers utilized in consumer electronics, ensuring seamless functionality and user experience.
- Collaborate with hardware engineers to validate hardware functionality and guarantee compatibility with firmware solutions.
- Develop clean, maintainable, and efficient firmware code while adhering to best practices and coding standards.
- Execute comprehensive testing, validation, and debugging processes to ensure firmware reliability and performance on embedded devices.
- Manage and oversee firmware updates, including release management and version control.
- Actively participate in product design discussions and offer insights to improve product functionality through firmware enhancements.
- Continuously monitor firmware performance, diagnose any issues, and implement necessary fixes for optimized device operation.
- Stay updated with advancements in firmware development tools and methodologies to enhance team capabilities and innovation.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a closely related field.
- 4-5 years of experience in firmware development using C and C++ in embedded systems.
- Proficient in debugging techniques, performance analysis, and firmware optimization strategies.
- Solid understanding of microcontroller architecture and low-level programming concepts.
- Experience in hardware/software integration and familiarity with common communication protocols (e.g., I2C, SPI, UART).
- Strong analytical skills and attention to detail while troubleshooting complex issues.
- Effective verbal and written communication skills to foster team collaboration and documentation.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of real-time operating systems (RTOS) and their application in firmware development.
- Familiarity with Agile development methodologies and their application in firmware projects.
- Exposure to consumer electronics and software compliance standards is a plus.
- Relevant certifications in embedded systems or firmware development are advantageous.
