## Detailed Responsibilities
- Ensure the stability and performance of database systems by monitoring for issues and implementing timely solutions.
- Collaborate with clients to gather database requirements, offering regular updates on performance and optimization.
- Engage in quality assurance processes and maintain thorough documentation for procedures and updates.
- Develop and implement data backup and recovery strategies, alongside robust data security measures.
- Prioritize tasks effectively to manage project timelines and meet client deliverables.

- Strong understanding of data backup, recovery processes, and data security protocols.
- Excellent problem-solving and analytical abilities.
- Ability to prioritize tasks and manage project timelines effectively.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Proactive approach to client relationship management.
- Availability for after-hours support on a rotational basis.
