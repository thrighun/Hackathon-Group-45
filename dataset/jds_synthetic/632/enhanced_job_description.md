## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the development and design of cutting-edge surgical instruments used in minimally invasive procedures.
- Collaborate with clinical and regulatory teams to capture and document product specifications, ensuring compliance with industry standards.
- Perform concept feasibility studies and prototype evaluations, refining designs based on user feedback and performance data.
- Manage risk assessments and develop mitigation strategies throughout the product development lifecycle.
- Establish and execute design validation and verification processes to guarantee product reliability and quality exceedance.
- Analyze industry trends and competitor technologies to drive innovative design solutions.
- Engage with suppliers and external manufacturing partners to optimize production quality and efficiency.
- Conduct educational workshops for internal teams to enhance knowledge about new product features.
- Provide regular updates on project milestones and address technical challenges with stakeholders.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering or a related field, with a preference for graduates from prestigious institutions.
- 7-12 years of experience in medical device design and development, with a strong portfolio of relevant projects.
- Solid understanding and practical experience with ISO and FDA regulatory standards, especially in the context of medical devices.

## Other Requirements
- Proficiency in statistical analysis methods and quality assurance processes.
- Strong project management capabilities, with the ability to prioritize tasks in a fast-paced environment.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to present complex information clearly.
