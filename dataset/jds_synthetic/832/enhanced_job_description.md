## Job Title
Senior Software Engineer - Cloud Applications

## Location
Bangalore or Pune

## Client Industry
Information Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement scalable cloud-based solutions, ensuring optimal performance and reliability.
- Collaborate closely with product managers and stakeholders to analyze requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
- Build and maintain applications utilizing Java, Spring Boot, and Node.js, with a focus on microservices architecture.
- Participate in code reviews and ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices.
- Develop automated CI/CD pipelines to streamline deployment processes and enhance productivity.
- Troubleshoot and optimize application performance, identifying bottlenecks and implementing effective solutions.
- Engage in Agile development processes, contributing to sprint planning and continuous improvement initiatives.
- Stay abreast of industry advancements and emerging technologies to inform innovation and development strategies.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 3+ years of experience in software development, with expertise in Java, Spring Boot, and Node.js.
- Strong understanding of microservices architecture and its implementation.
- Proficient in CI/CD tools and practices, with a focus on automating deployment processes.
- Excellent problem-solving abilities and attention to detail.
- Strong communication skills, with a knack for explaining complex technical concepts to diverse audiences.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with serverless architectures and RESTful API development.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
- Knowledge of Agile methodologies and experience working in cross-functional teams.
- Relevant certifications in Java or cloud technologies are a plus.
