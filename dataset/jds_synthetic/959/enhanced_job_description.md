## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and architect robust cloud solutions tailored to specific client requirements.
- Collaborate with business units to identify needs and create effective cloud migration strategies.
- Monitor cloud infrastructure to troubleshoot issues, optimize performance, and enhance security.
- Conduct training sessions and provide mentoring for junior architects and technical staff.
- Research and evaluate emerging cloud technologies to ensure the company remains at the forefront of industry advancements.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Extensive experience with cloud architecture and services, particularly with AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
- Strong understanding of networking, security, and storage solutions within cloud environments.
- Proven ability to work collaboratively in a team-oriented environment.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.

## Other Requirements
- Relevant certifications such as AWS Certified Solutions Architect or Azure Solutions Architect Expert.
- Experience in automation and CI/CD tools.
- Familiarity with Infrastructure as Code (IAC) principles and tools like Terraform or Ansible.
