## Job Title
Frontend Engineer

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement engaging user interfaces using Vue.js and Vuex.
- Collaborate with UX/UI designers to translate designs into high-quality code.
- Integrate third-party APIs and services to enhance application functionality.
- Conduct performance tuning and optimization of web applications.
- Participate in Agile development processes and sprint planning meetings.
- Provide mentorship to junior developers and contribute to team knowledge sharing.
- Research and recommend new tools and technologies to improve development processes.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- At least 4 years of professional experience in frontend development.
- Strong proficiency with Vue.js and state management patterns (Vuex).
- Solid understanding of HTML5, CSS3, and responsive design principles.
- Experience with RESTful services and modern build tools (Webpack, Babel).
- Familiarity with testing frameworks like Jest or Mocha.
- Excellent analytical and troubleshooting abilities.
- Effective communication skills to work collaboratively with diverse teams.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with TypeScript.
- Knowledge of server-side rendering with Nuxt.js.
- Familiarity with Progressive Web App (PWA) best practices.
- Understanding of Agile methodologies and version control systems like Git.
