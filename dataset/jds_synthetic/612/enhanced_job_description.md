## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement data governance strategies for cloud environments, ensuring compliance with data privacy regulations.
- Conduct regular audits of data usage and access to identify non-compliance and areas for improvement.
- Collaborate with IT, legal, and compliance teams to provide guidance on data management policies and procedures.
- Monitor cloud data interactions and report on compliance status to stakeholders.
- Provide training sessions for employees on data governance and compliance best practices.
- Stay abreast of changes in regulations affecting data governance and recommend necessary adjustments to policies.
- Assist in incident response planning and investigation related to data breaches.

## Skill Requirements
- A minimum of 8 years of experience in data governance, compliance, or data privacy within cloud environments.
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Information Management, Law, or a related field.
- Relevant certifications such as CIPP, CIPM, or CGEIT.
- Strong understanding of data privacy regulations like GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA.
- Proficient in data management tools and governance frameworks.
- Excellent analytical skills and attention to detail for auditing and risk assessment.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills to engage with various stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of cloud security principles and best practices.
- Familiarity with data protection technologies and management platforms.
- Experience working with cross-functional teams in a cloud setting.
