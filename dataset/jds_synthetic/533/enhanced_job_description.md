## Detailed Responsibilities
- Collaborate with stakeholders to gather business requirements and define the scope of AI projects.
- Design and architect scalable AI/ML solutions that meet organizational needs.
- Implement machine learning algorithms in production environments, ensuring high performance and reliability.

- Proficient in software development lifecycle and deployment methodologies.

- Excellent communication skills, with the ability to articulate complex concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
