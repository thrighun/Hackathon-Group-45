## Job Title
Senior Data Engineer

## Location
New York, NY or San Francisco, CA

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain scalable and robust data pipelines to support data ingestion and processing at scale.
- Work closely with data scientists and analysts to translate complex data requirements into efficient data architectures.
- Implement data integration processes that ensure the quality, consistency, and availability of data across multiple sources.
- Optimize database systems for performance and efficiency, ensuring timely access to data for analytical purposes.
- Develop and enforce data governance policies, best practices, and security protocols.
- Monitor system performance and troubleshoot data-related issues to maintain optimal operations.
- Lead initiatives to adopt new technologies and methodologies that improve data engineering practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Minimum of 10 years of experience in data engineering or a related discipline.
- Strong proficiency in programming languages such as Python, Java, or Scala.
- Extensive experience with ETL tools and frameworks (e.g., Apache Airflow, Talend).
- Deep understanding of database technologies (e.g., SQL, NoSQL) and data warehousing solutions.
- Excellent problem-solving skills with a solid understanding of data modeling and data architecture principles.
- Proven ability to work collaboratively in a cross-functional team environment.
- Strong communication skills to effectively share insights with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with cloud-based data solutions (e.g., AWS Redshift, Google BigQuery, Azure Data Lake).
- Familiarity with containerization and orchestration technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes).
- Background in a technology firm or a startup environment with a fast-paced development cycle.
