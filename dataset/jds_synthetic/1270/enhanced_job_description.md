## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement firmware for low-power IoT devices using embedded C and C++.
- Collaborate with product management and hardware teams to define new device functionalities.
- Optimize firmware to enhance energy efficiency and overall performance.
- Diagnose and troubleshoot firmware issues in constrained environments.
- Engage in architectural discussions to improve firmware design and functionality.
- Conduct rigorous testing and validation of firmware to ensure reliability.
- Document firmware designs and updates to facilitate future development efforts.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- At least 4 years of experience in embedded firmware development.
- Proficient in C and C++ programming languages, particularly for low-power applications.
- Solid understanding of IoT protocols and standards (e.g., MQTT, CoAP).
- Experience with microcontrollers and low-power hardware platforms.
- Familiarity with development tools such as JTAG, oscilloscopes, and logic analyzers.
- Strong analytical abilities and attention to detail.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with wireless communication technologies (e.g., BLE, Wi-Fi, Zigbee).
- Knowledge of Agile development practices.
- Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal.
