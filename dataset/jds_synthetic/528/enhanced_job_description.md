## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect and implement hybrid cloud solutions utilizing AWS infrastructure to support scalable life sciences applications and services.
- Lead initiatives to integrate on-premise systems with cloud environments, ensuring seamless data flow and operational continuity.
- Collaborate with clinical researchers and data scientists to design data pipelines that facilitate analytics and machine learning applications.
- Establish best practices for cloud governance and cost management, ensuring compliance with healthcare regulations like HIPAA.
- Perform capacity planning and performance tuning for cloud resources to optimize application performance and reliability.
- Create comprehensive training and support resources for technical teams and stakeholders to enhance cloud understanding and adoption.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum of 10+ years of experience in cloud architecture, emphasizing hybrid cloud solutions and AWS services.
- Deep expertise in AWS services including EC2, VPC, RDS, IAM, and CloudTrail.
- Background in life sciences or healthcare technology, with a strong record of delivering scalable and compliant cloud solutions.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field; advanced AWS certifications are strongly preferred.
- Exceptional analytical and troubleshooting skills, demonstrating a proactive approach in a fast-paced environment.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with infrastructure as code (IaC) tools such as Terraform or AWS CloudFormation.
- Knowledge of data governance and security best practices specific to the healthcare industry.
- Strong leadership and communication skills to engage effectively with diverse teams and stakeholders.
