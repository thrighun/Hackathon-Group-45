## Detailed Responsibilities
- Manage user access, roles, and permissions within Salesforce to ensure data integrity and security.
- Create custom reports and dashboards to provide actionable insights for sales and marketing teams.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into effective Salesforce solutions.
- Perform regular data audits and maintain data quality through cleaning and deduplication efforts.
- Customize Salesforce layouts, fields, and processes to meet organizational needs.
- Provide technical support and training for end-users to optimize Salesforce usage and functionality.
- Stay updated on Salesforce releases and new features to maximize platform performance.
- Facilitate documentation of processes, technical specifications, and user guides for enhanced knowledge sharing.
- Assist in the implementation and testing of new integrations with third-party applications.
- Participate in Agile development cycles, contributing ideas for enhancing Salesforce capabilities.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Information Systems, or related field.
- Proven experience as a Salesforce Administrator, with certifications such as Salesforce Administrator or Advanced Administrator preferred.
- Strong knowledge of Salesforce Sales Cloud and Marketing Cloud functionalities.
- Proficiency in creating complex reports, dashboards, and workflows in Salesforce.
- Familiarity with CRM best practices and data governance frameworks.
- Excellent analytical skills with attention to detail and data accuracy.
- Strong problem-solving abilities and effective communication skills.
- Experience working in Agile environments and collaborating with cross-functional teams.

## Other Requirements
- Salesforce Certified Advanced Administrator or Salesforce App Builder certification is a plus.
- Experience with Salesforce integrations, APIs, and third-party applications.
- Prior experience in a multinational or large-scale enterprise organization is highly desirable.
- Knowledge of additional platforms such as Service Cloud or Pardot is advantageous.
- Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously.
