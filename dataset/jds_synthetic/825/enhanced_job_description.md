## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and maintain full-stack web applications using Node.js, React.js, and Bootstrap.
- Collaborate with product owners and UI/UX designers to create intuitive and engaging user experiences.
- Build and manage RESTful APIs, ensuring seamless communication between front-end and back-end systems.
- Optimize application performance, scalability, and security, addressing potential issues proactively.
- Create and manage database schemas, ensuring data integrity and efficient data retrieval processes.
- Participate in agile development processes, including sprint planning, daily stand-ups, and retrospectives.
- Conduct thorough testing and debugging of applications, maintaining high standards of code quality.
- Mentor junior developers and assist in onboarding new team members, helping to cultivate a collaborative team environment.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- 3+ years of experience in full-stack JavaScript development with a focus on Node.js and React.js.
- Proficiency in Bootstrap for responsive design and front-end development.
- Strong knowledge of database technologies such as MongoDB, PostgreSQL, or MySQL.
- Familiarity with version control systems, particularly Git.
- Experience with cloud services (AWS, Heroku) and microservices architecture.
- Excellent analytical skills and the ability to think critically and solve complex problems.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills, with a proven ability to collaborate effectively within a team.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with front-end state management libraries (Redux or MobX).
- Knowledge of containerization technologies such as Docker.
- Familiarity with CI/CD tools and practices for automated testing and deployment.
- Understanding of web accessibility standards and best practices.
- Previous experience in the retail sector or e-commerce development is a plus.
