## Job Title
Automotive Systems Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Global Automotive Manufacturer

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Leverage expertise in SolidWorks or NX for advanced 3D modeling, assemblies, and sheet metal design to create high-quality automotive components.
- Integrate electrical systems within automotive designs, focusing on enhancing safety and performance.
- Design and develop key automotive components such as brakes, steering systems, and electrical harnesses that meet stringent industry standards.
- Collaborate with manufacturing engineers to apply knowledge of CNC machining, additive manufacturing, and injection molding processes in product development.
- Conduct material selection and properties analysis to optimize performance and durability in automotive applications.
- Work closely with cross-functional teams, including testing and quality assurance, to ensure designs are validated and meet regulatory compliance.
- Drive continuous improvement initiatives to enhance design efficiency and product reliability.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong proficiency in SolidWorks or NX (Siemens).
- Extensive experience with 3D modeling, assemblies, and sheet metal design.
- Familiarity with electrical systems integration in automotive design.
- Proven experience in designing automotive components such as brakes, steering systems, and electrical harnesses.
- Solid understanding of manufacturing processes, including CNC machining, additive manufacturing, and injection molding.
- Knowledge of materials selection and properties relevant to automotive applications.
- Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.

## Other Requirements
- Relevant certifications in CAD software or automotive design methodologies.
- Experience with additional engineering software tools for simulations.
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
