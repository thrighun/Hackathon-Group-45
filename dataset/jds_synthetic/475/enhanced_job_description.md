## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and execute comprehensive test plans and test cases based on software requirements and specifications.
- Conduct performance, regression, and usability testing to ensure applications meet the highest standards of quality.
- Collaborate with software developers and product managers throughout the development lifecycle to identify and resolve issues.
- Analyze and document testing results, providing recommendations for improvements and enhancements.
- Participate in peer reviews and provide constructive feedback on testing processes and methodologies.

## Skill Requirements
- Primary Skills: Performance Testing
- Secondary Skills: Manual Testing (Intermediate Level)
- Proficient in using testing tools and frameworks such as Selenium, JMeter, or similar.
- Strong analytical skills with a focus on detail-oriented testing practices.
- Excellent communication skills to effectively liaise with team members and stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Minimum of 3 years of experience in software quality assurance or testing roles.
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies and software development lifecycle (SDLC).
- A degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field is preferred.
- Relevant certifications in software testing (e.g., ISTQB) are a plus.
