## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead and manage a team of software developers, ensuring high-quality code delivery and adherence to best practices.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to establish project goals, timelines, and resource allocation.
- Mentor and support team members in their professional development and foster a collaborative team environment.
- Review and enhance existing software architecture and design to improve performance and scalability.
- Conduct regular sprint planning and retrospectives to continuously improve team performance and efficiency.
- Ensure compliance with industry standards and company policies regarding software development practices.
- Stay informed about new technologies and trends to guide the team in the adoption of relevant tools and methodologies.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- 7-10 years of experience in software development, with at least 3 years in a leadership role.
- Proven expertise in Java, J2EE, as well as familiarity with modern frameworks like Spring Boot.
- Strong knowledge of database systems, particularly with SQL and NoSQL technologies.
- Experience in Agile/Scrum environments, with the ability to facilitate various Agile ceremonies.
- Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills, capable of working with diverse teams.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with CI/CD practices and tools such as Jenkins or GitLab.
- Experience managing cloud-based applications and services, preferably in AWS or Azure environments.
- Relevant certifications in software development or project management (such as PMP or Agile certifications) are a plus.
