- Lead cross-functional project teams to deliver complex SAP solutions tailored to client specifications.
- Facilitate the collection and documentation of business requirements, transforming them into technical specifications for implementation.
- Prepare and manage comprehensive project budgets and timelines, ensuring all milestones are met.
- Conduct risk management and proactively develop mitigation strategies to address project disruptions.
- Serve as the main client liaison, managing expectations and providing regular updates on project progression.
- Collaborate with technical teams to ensure alignment between solutions and client needs while adhering to best practices.

- Evaluate project outcomes to contribute to continuous improvement initiatives for future projects.
