## Detailed Responsibilities
- Collaborate with business units to identify pain points and recommend SAP solutions that streamline operations.
- Assist in defining project scope, objectives, and deliverables in collaboration with stakeholders.
- Conduct gap analysis to ensure business requirements are effectively translated into SAP functionalities.
- Liaise between IT and business users to ensure seamless communication and understanding.
- Develop and conduct training and workshops for end-users to maximize adoption of SAP solutions.
- Monitor project progress and report on key performance indicators to management.
- Participate in change management initiatives to ensure user adoption of enhancements and upgrades.

- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Computer Science, or a related field.
- 4+ years of experience in a business analysis role with a focus on SAP solutions.
- Knowledge of SAP modules such as FICO, SD, and MM is essential.
- Experience in process mapping and documentation techniques.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and organizational skills.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with SAP Business Workflow and Performance Management.
- Relevant certifications in SAP Business Analysis or Project Management.
- Experience with SAP HANA and its functionalities.
- Knowledge of business intelligence tools and data analytics.
