## Job Title
Automotive Component Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Leading Automotive Manufacturer

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Utilize SolidWorks or Autodesk Inventor for 3D CAD modeling, including component creation, assemblies, and parametric design.
- Design and develop automotive exterior components such as bumpers, grilles, and lighting systems, ensuring aesthetic and functional quality.
- Collaborate on electrical systems integration in automotive product designs to enhance vehicle functionality.
- Ensure that designs are optimized for manufacturing techniques such as injection molding, stamping, and CNC machining.
- Apply knowledge of material properties when selecting appropriate materials for different automotive applications.
- Conduct Finite Element Analysis (FEA) to validate and optimize designs for performance and safety.
- Create and maintain comprehensive technical documentation and support design reviews for quality assurance.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in SolidWorks or Autodesk Inventor.
- Experience with 3D CAD modeling and parametric design practices.
- Familiarity with automotive exterior component design.
- Strong understanding of manufacturing techniques including injection molding, stamping, and CNC machining.
- Knowledge of material properties and selection processes in automotive applications.
- Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering or a related technical discipline.
- Certification in CAD software and engineering analysis tools is preferred.

## Other Requirements
- Previous experience integrating electrical systems into automotive designs is advantageous.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
- Effective communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
