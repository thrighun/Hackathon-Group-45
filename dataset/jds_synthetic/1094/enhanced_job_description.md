## Detailed Responsibilities
- Construct and optimize data pipelines to ensure seamless data flow from various sources into centralized data storage.
- Implement data governance practices to maintain data integrity and quality throughout its lifecycle.
- Partner with data scientists and analysts to identify data needs and create tailored solutions for effective analysis.
- Develop and manage ETL processes to transform unrefined data into structured formats suitable for analysis.
- Design flexible data models that cater to diverse analytical requirements and enhance stakeholder data access.
- Monitor and optimize performance of data infrastructure to improve data processing and retrieval efficiencies.
- Keep abreast of emerging trends and advancements in data engineering and analytics to incorporate into processes.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong programming skills in Python or Java, along with proficiency in SQL.
- In-depth understanding of data warehousing concepts and proficiency with big data tools such as Hadoop or Spark.
- Familiarity with cloud data services like AWS Redshift or Google BigQuery.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in data engineering or related roles.
- Certifications in data engineering or cloud platforms are a plus.
