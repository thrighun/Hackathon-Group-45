## Detailed Responsibilities
- Provide Level 2 support for complex server and networking issues, ensuring effective deployment of new systems and enhancements.
- Engage in installation, configuration, monitoring, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement of IT infrastructures.
- Collaborate with senior engineers to design scalable and reliable systems architecture.
- Participate in a rotational on-call L3 support team, responding to escalated incidents.
- Identify potential security vulnerabilities and manage patch deployment.
- Utilize scripting languages for automating tasks and enhancing system management efficiency.
- Participate in rotating shifts (24x7) as part of the support model.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or related field.
- 7-10 years of experience in systems engineering and administration.
- Proficiency with Active Directory and Microsoft Exchange.
- Strong understanding of networking concepts, including TCP/IP, DNS, and DHCP.
- Familiarity with virtualization technologies, particularly Hyper-V and VMware.
- Experience with cloud services, including Azure and AWS.
- Scripting experience with PowerShell and Python.
- Solid troubleshooting skills in complex network and server setups.

## Other Requirements
- Certification in Microsoft technologies (e.g., MCSA, MCSE) or cloud certifications (Azure/AWS).
- Experience with system monitoring tools and creating performance dashboards.
- Knowledge of containerization technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes) is a plus.
- Familiarity with backup and disaster recovery strategies for Windows environments.
