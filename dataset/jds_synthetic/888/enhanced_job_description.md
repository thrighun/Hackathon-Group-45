## Detailed Responsibilities
- Build dynamic and responsive web applications using Vue.js and Tailwind CSS to enhance user experience on e-commerce platforms.
- Work closely with product managers and UI/UX designers to implement intuitive shopping experiences that meet customer needs.
- Optimize frontend performance for high traffic scenarios and ensure fast load times.
- Collaborate with backend developers to integrate RESTful APIs and third-party services seamlessly.
- Conduct thorough testing and debugging to maintain code quality and application reliability.
- Participate in Agile methodologies, including sprint planning and daily stand-ups, to ensure projects are delivered on time.
- Maintain comprehensive documentation of development processes and technical specifications, facilitating team collaboration and knowledge sharing.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or relevant field.
- 3+ years of experience in frontend development, with a strong focus on Vue.js.
- Proficiency in Tailwind CSS, along with strong knowledge of JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3.
- Experience working with version control systems such as Git.
- Solid understanding of responsive design principles and e-commerce best practices.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills coupled with a keen attention to detail.
- Excellent communication skills for effective collaboration within a team.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with serverless architecture and cloud services (e.g., AWS, Azure).
- Understanding of CI/CD processes and DevOps practices.
- Experience with testing frameworks like Jest or Mocha for frontend applications.
- Previous experience in an e-commerce development environment is a plus.
- Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic work environment while managing multiple projects.
