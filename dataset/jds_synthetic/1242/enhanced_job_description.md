## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain efficient, reusable, and reliable web applications with Django and React.
- Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to implement new features and improve existing functionalities.
- Ensure the technical feasibility of UI/UX designs while maintaining consistency in application performance.
- Create APIs and integrate them successfully to connect with various third-party services and databases.
- Develop and execute testing protocols to ensure code reliability and security.
- Contribute to all stages of the software development lifecycle, from planning through deployment.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in Python and experience working with Django framework.
- Strong understanding of React.js and its ecosystem, including state management libraries.
- Familiar with database management systems such as PostgreSQL or MySQL.
- Experience with RESTful APIs and integrating front-end with back-end technologies.
- Knowledge of responsive design principles and frameworks.
- Familiar with tools such as Docker, Git, and CI/CD pipelines.
- Understanding of web security and performance optimization techniques.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with JavaScript libraries other than React, such as jQuery or Vue.js, is a plus.
- Strong problem-solving skills and a proactive approach to development challenges.
- Effective communication skills and the ability to collaborate within a team environment.
- A Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent experience.
- At least 3 years of experience in full-stack web development.
