## Job Title
Full Stack JavaScript Developer (Node.js & Bootstrap CSS)

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Retail

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement robust back-end services using Node.js while also developing dynamic front-end applications with Bootstrap CSS.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including product designers and backend engineers, to create seamless user experiences.
- Ensure application security and data protection by implementing best practices in both front-end and back-end development.
- Construct and maintain RESTful APIs to support various application features and functionalities.
- Write efficient, testable, and maintainable code, contributing to a shared codebase with version control.
- Participate in daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and retrospectives to support Agile development practices.
- Perform thorough testing and debugging of applications to maintain a high standard of quality across platforms.
- Keep abreast of industry trends and emerging technologies to drive innovation within the development team.
- Contribute to performance optimization and troubleshooting, ensuring minimal downtime and fast response times.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Proven experience as a Full Stack Developer with a focus on JavaScript technologies.
- Strong proficiency in Node.js, along with a solid understanding of front-end frameworks like Bootstrap CSS.
- Extensive experience with database management (SQL and NoSQL).
- Familiarity with cloud services (e.g., AWS, Azure) and deployment processes.
- Knowledge of version control systems, specifically Git.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with a strong attention to detail.
- Strong collaboration and communication skills to work effectively within a team.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in the retail sector or similar consumer-focused industries.
- Understanding of Agile/Scrum methodologies.
- Familiar with testing frameworks (e.g., Jest, Mocha) and CI/CD pipelines.
- Previous experience with microservices architecture and containerization (e.g., Docker).
- Commitment to accessibility standards and performing code audits for inclusivity.
