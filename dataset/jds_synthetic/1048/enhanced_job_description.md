## Job Title
Firmware Engineer - Embedded Systems

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Embedded Systems Development 

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement firmware for microcontrollers and embedded systems utilizing C and assembly language.
- Collaborate with hardware teams to ensure seamless integration of firmware with various devices and technologies.
- Perform in-depth analysis of system performance and identify areas for firmware optimization.
- Execute robust testing protocols and validate firmware functionality against specifications.
- Mentor junior developers and participate in setting best practices within the firmware team.
- Document firmware design processes, version changes, and technical specifications for compliance and future reference.
- Research and evaluate new embedded technologies to continuously enhance firmware capabilities.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related discipline.
- At least 4 years of experience in embedded firmware development with C and assembly.
- In-depth knowledge of microcontroller architecture and peripheral interfaces.
- Proficiency in low-level programming and debugging techniques for hardware integration.
- Experience with development tools and technologies, including JTAG and oscilloscopes.
- Strong analytical skills with a focus on performance tuning and optimization.
- Effective communication skills and ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with wireless communication protocols such as Bluetooth, Zigbee, or Wi-Fi.
- Experience with continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) practices.
- Understanding of safety-critical software development standards, such as ISO 26262 or DO-178C.
- Participation in open-source projects or contributions to software communities is a plus.
