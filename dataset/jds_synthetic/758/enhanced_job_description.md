## Job Title
Front-End Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
B2B SaaS

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement dynamic user interfaces for project management applications using frameworks like Angular or Vue.js.
- Collaborate with UX/UI designers and product teams to create an engaging and user-friendly experience.
- Write clean, maintainable, and efficient code while adhering to best practices in software development.
- Conduct code reviews and mentor junior developers to promote coding standards and practices.
- Ensure the technical feasibility of UI designs and optimize applications for maximum performance.
- Participate in agile ceremonies and contribute to sprint planning and retrospectives.
- Troubleshoot and resolve issues in web applications and user interfaces.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum of 4 years of experience in front-end development.
- Strong knowledge of JavaScript and familiarity with modern frameworks such as Angular or Vue.js.
- Proficient understanding of web markup, including HTML5 and CSS3.
- Experience with version control systems like Git.
- Familiarity with RESTful APIs and asynchronous programming.
- Understanding of responsive design and cross-browser compatibility.
- Strong problem-solving abilities and attention to detail.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with testing frameworks such as Jest or Mocha.
- Knowledge of TypeScript or similar strongly typed languages.
- Ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced, team-oriented environment.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
- A degree in Computer Science or a related field is preferred.
