## Job Title
Senior Embedded Software Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and optimize embedded software solutions utilizing C++ in real-time operating systems.
- Collaborate closely with hardware engineers to design and validate the overall system architecture.
- Conduct performance analysis, profiling, and troubleshooting of real-time applications to enhance system efficiency.
- Lead software integration efforts, ensuring compatibility between software and hardware components.
- Ensure compliance with industry standards and best practices in software development.
- Mentor and provide support to junior developers, fostering a culture of learning and improvement.
- Oversee code quality through regular reviews, audits, and adherence to coding standards.
- Document technical designs, system architecture, and development processes rigorously.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- At least 7 years of experience in embedded software development using C++ and RTOS.
- Strong expertise in real-time operating systems and embedded system design.
- Proficiency in performance analysis tools and techniques specific to real-time applications.
- Exceptional debugging skills and experience with embedded software testing frameworks.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills for effective teamwork.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with additional programming languages such as Assembly or Rust.
- Experience working in an Agile development environment.
- Knowledge of safety-critical and industry-specific compliance standards (e.g., ISO 26262).
- Prior experience in developing software for consumer electronics or automotive applications.
