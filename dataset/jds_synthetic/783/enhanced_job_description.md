## Job Title
Embedded Software Engineer with IoT System Development Experience

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop embedded software solutions for Internet of Things (IoT) devices.
- Collaborate with hardware engineers to ensure seamless integration of software with hardware components.
- Implement secure communication protocols for data transmission in IoT applications.
- Conduct system testing and validation to ensure reliability and performance in real-world scenarios.
- Optimize existing software for performance and resource management in constrained environments.
- Work with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
- Mentor junior engineers and contribute to the establishment of best practices in the development process.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in C and C++ programming languages, with a focus on low-level programming.
- Experience in developing software for IoT devices and familiarity with networking protocols like MQTT and CoAP.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Strong analytical and debugging skills with attention to detail.
- Excellent teamwork and communication skills, capable of working in a collaborative environment.
- Familiarity with development environments, version control systems, and Agile methodologies.

## Other Requirements
- Understanding of wireless communication technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or Zigbee.
- Experience with cloud platforms and services for IoT applications.
- Previous experience in a technology startup or a fast-paced development environment.
- Certifications relevant to embedded systems or IoT technologies.
