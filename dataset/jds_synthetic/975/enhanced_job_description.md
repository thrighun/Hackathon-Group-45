## Job Title
Machine Learning Engineer (Reinforcement Learning, AI Optimization)

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology Services

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement reinforcement learning algorithms to optimize client-specific applications and systems.
- Collaborate with product managers and data scientists to define project goals and deliver AI-driven solutions that enhance user experiences.
- Analyze user interactions and system performance to refine and retrain AI models for improved decision-making and efficiency.
- Design end-to-end machine learning pipelines, ensuring integration with backend systems and databases for seamless operation.
- Conduct experiments and A/B testing to evaluate the effectiveness of different algorithms and approaches.
- Document technical processes, findings, and lessons learned to foster a culture of knowledge sharing and continuous improvement.
- Stay updated with the latest research in reinforcement learning and machine learning to innovate and incorporate advanced methodologies into projects.
- Assist in client presentations and workshops to explain AI concepts and demonstrate the value of deployed solutions.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field.
- Solid experience in machine learning development, particularly in reinforcement learning techniques.
- Strong programming skills in Python and proficiency in relevant ML libraries (e.g., TensorFlow, Keras, OpenAI Gym).
- Understanding of system design principles and experience with cloud-based ML solutions.
- Familiarity with data engineering and ETL processes for effective data handling.
- Excellent analytical skills and a proactive approach to problem-solving in complex environments.
- Strong communication skills to effectively share insights with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with containerization and orchestration tools (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes) for deploying ML models.
- Knowledge of ethical considerations and practices in AI usage and development.
- Participation in AI-focused hackathons or conferences, and contributions to relevant projects or publications.
