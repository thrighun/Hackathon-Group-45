## Job Title
Senior Data Analyst

## Location
Austin, TX or San Francisco, CA

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Interpret complex data sets to guide strategic decision-making across the organization.
- Develop interactive dashboards using visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI) to track and report on key performance metrics.
- Work closely with product management and engineering teams to identify data-driven opportunities for enhancing user experience and operational efficiency.
- Conduct ad-hoc analysis and present findings to senior management to facilitate data-informed decisions.
- Lead efforts in data governance to ensure accuracy, reliability, and integrity of analytics processes.
- Mentor junior analysts and support their development in analytical techniques and tools.
- Implement best practices in data analysis and visualization, ensuring adherence to company standards.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Analytics, Statistics, or a related field.
- Minimum of 8 years of experience in data analysis or a similar role.
- Proficiency in SQL and experience with data manipulation using Python or R.
- Strong expertise in data visualization tools such as Tableau or Power BI.
- Solid understanding of statistical methods and data modeling techniques.
- Excellent analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and attention to detail.
- Strong communication skills, capable of conveying analytical concepts to non-technical audiences.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with data warehousing solutions and ETL processes.
- Familiarity with cloud-based analytics tools and platforms (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud).
- Previous experience in a data-driven technology company or startup.
