## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement robust data pipelines that capture, transform, and store data from varied sources.
- Collaborate with data scientists and analysts to understand data requirements and optimize data flow for analytics.
- Develop ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes to ensure high data quality and availability for business intelligence.
- Monitor and troubleshoot data pipeline performance and establish best practices for maintenance and reliability.
- Create and maintain documentation for data models, data flow architecture, and data processing workflows.
- Utilize cloud services for data storage and processing, ensuring scalability and cost efficiency.
- Work closely with IT security teams to ensure data privacy and compliance with regulations.
- Stay abreast of industry trends and technology advancements in data engineering to implement innovative solutions.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 3 years of experience in data engineering or a similar role.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python or Scala for data manipulation.
- Strong experience with data processing frameworks like Apache Spark or Hadoop.
- Familiarity with database technologies including SQL, NoSQL, and data warehousing solutions.
- Understanding of data modeling concepts and practice with data governance.
- Experience with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud, Azure) for data storage and analytics utilities.
- Solid problem-solving skills and ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with workflow orchestration tools like Apache Airflow or similar.
- Understanding of machine learning concepts and workflow integration.
- Knowledge of containerization technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes) is a plus.
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies and participation in cross-functional teams is desired.
- Relevant certifications in data engineering or cloud technologies are advantageous.
