## Detailed Responsibilities
- Gather and analyze business requirements to develop comprehensive reporting and analytics solutions.
- Design and maintain automated reporting systems to track key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Collaborate with business stakeholders to identify opportunities for process improvements based on data analysis.
- Develop dashboards and visualizations that effectively communicate data-driven insights to non-technical audiences.
- Conduct training sessions and workshops for teams on how to utilize data tools and interpret analytics reports.
- Monitor industry trends and best practices in business intelligence and analytics.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in data visualization tools such as Tableau, QlikView, or Power BI.
- Strong analytical skills and experience with statistical analysis.
- Familiarity with SQL for data retrieval and manipulation.
- Excellent interpersonal skills for collaboration with cross-functional teams.
- Ability to communicate complex data insights clearly and effectively to stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Business, Computer Science, Statistics, or a related field.
- 5+ years of experience in business intelligence or data analytics.
- Experience in agile methodologies and project management.
- Knowledge of ETL processes and data warehousing concepts. 
- Familiarity with cloud data platforms like AWS or Google Cloud.
