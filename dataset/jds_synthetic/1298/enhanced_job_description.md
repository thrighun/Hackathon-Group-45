- Design and implement dynamic user interfaces utilizing React.js, ensuring a responsive and engaging user experience.
- Collaborate with backend developers and product managers to architect and develop cloud-based solutions that enhance application functionality.
- Integrate third-party APIs and cloud services, ensuring secure and efficient data exchange between systems.
- Conduct performance optimization and debugging, addressing issues to improve application efficiency and user satisfaction.
- Lead code reviews and provide mentorship to junior developers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement.
- Create and maintain technical documentation related to system architecture, integration workflows, and best practices.
- Actively participate in Agile ceremonies, including sprint planning and daily stand-ups, to contribute to the team's success and agility.

- At least 5 years of experience in front-end development, with a strong command of React.js and related technologies.
- Proven experience with cloud integration services and deployment, specifically in AWS or Azure environments.
- Strong proficiency in JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, and modern web development practices.
- Familiarity with state management patterns, particularly Redux, and understanding of component lifecycle.
- Experience with version control (Git) and automated deployment pipelines.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience.
- Excellent problem-solving skills and the ability to communicate effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
