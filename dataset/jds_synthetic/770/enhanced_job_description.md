## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design intuitive and visually compelling user interfaces for web applications
- Collaborate with product managers and developers to define and implement innovative solutions for the product direction
- Create wireframes, mockups, and prototypes to visualize design concepts and user flows
- Conduct user research and usability testing to gather insights for improving the UI
- Ensure design consistency across various platforms and devices
- Stay updated on the latest design trends, tools, and technologies to enhance product offerings
- Create and maintain a style guide and component library to ensure brand consistency
- Work on improving accessibility standards and ensuring compliance in designs

## Skill Requirements
- Proven experience as a UI Designer or similar role
- Strong portfolio showcasing design work and projects
- Proficiency in design tools such as Sketch, Figma, or Adobe XD
- Understanding of HTML/CSS and basic front-end development concepts
- Knowledge of responsive and mobile-first design principles
- Familiarity with design systems and UI frameworks (e.g., Bootstrap, Material Design)
- Excellent visual design skills and attention to detail
- Strong communication and collaboration skills

## Other Requirements
- Experience with user-centered design methodologies
- Familiarity with JavaScript and front-end frameworks is a plus
- Understanding of analytics tools to measure user interaction
- Ability to effectively prioritize and manage multiple projects in a fast-paced environment
