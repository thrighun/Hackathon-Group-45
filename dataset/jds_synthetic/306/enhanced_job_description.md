## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop efficient, scalable applications using Node.js that meet business criteria and enhance functionality.
- Create and manage RESTful APIs to ensure smooth communication between the front-end and back-end systems.
- Implement dynamic user interfaces in React.js to deliver an engaging experience for end-users.
- Collaborate closely with product managers and other stakeholders to translate business needs into technical specifications.
- Conduct thorough testing and debugging to identify and resolve issues, ensuring reliability and performance standards are met.
- Stay informed on the latest trends in software development, applying advancements to improve existing systems and processes.
- Engage actively in Agile workflows, contributing to sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives to enhance team dynamics.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in Node.js and its frameworks, with a strong understanding of asynchronous programming.
- Experience in building and consuming RESTful APIs and a solid grasp of API design principles.
- Strong background in front-end development using React.js, including state management and responsive design.
- Knowledge of relational and non-relational database systems, preferably SQL and NoSQL databases.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline (or equivalent experience).
- Familiarity with cloud computing services (e.g., AWS, Azure) is advantageous.
- Prior experience working in an Agile development team is a plus.
- Understanding of containerization technologies (like Docker) and CI/CD pipelines is desirable.
