## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain responsive web applications using Vue.js, with an emphasis on mobile eCommerce platforms.
- Collaborate with UI/UX designers to effectively translate wireframes and mockups into working code.
- Integrate various payment gateways and third-party tools to streamline the online shopping experience for users.
- Ensure high-performance applications by optimizing for speed, usability, and mobile responsiveness.
- Work closely with backend developers to implement and test RESTful APIs for seamless data exchange.
- Conduct thorough code reviews and maintain coding standards to enhance software quality and team knowledge sharing.
- Engage in agile methodologies, actively participating in sprint planning, daily stand-ups, and retrospectives.
- Troubleshoot and resolve issues through the entire development lifecycle, from design to deployment.
- Stay informed about industry trends, best practices, and emerging technologies related to frontend and eCommerce development.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field, or significant practical experience in web development.
- Proven experience with Vue.js and a solid understanding of modern JavaScript frameworks.
- Proficient in CSS and HTML with a focus on responsive design and mobile-first development.
- Experience in integrating headless CMS solutions (such as Sanity, Prismic, or similar) into web applications.
- Familiarity with eCommerce platforms and payment integration strategies.
- Knowledge of version control systems, particularly Git, for collaborative development.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with modern JavaScript features (ES6+) and TypeScript.
- Familiarity with cloud-based services (such as AWS or Firebase).
- Experience working in an Agile/Scrum environment.
- Prior involvement in projects focusing on mobile-first design and high-traffic retail websites.
- Understanding of SEO principles as they pertain to frontend development.
