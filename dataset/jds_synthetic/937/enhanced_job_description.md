## Detailed Responsibilities
- Monitor application performance, troubleshoot issues, and implement necessary fixes and enhancements based on user feedback and business needs.
- Assist clients by gathering feedback, resolving technical tickets, and delivering timely updates on maintenance activities while ensuring quality standards are met.
- Create and update support documentation and knowledge base articles for internal and client use in accordance with compliance requirements.
- Mentor and support junior team members with technical challenges and career growth.
- Collaborate with product managers and software engineers to understand new features and contribute to requirement discussions.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Experience in application support or software development, with a solid understanding of software architecture and design.
- Proficiency in scripting languages such as JavaScript, Ruby, or Bash for automation tasks.
- Familiarity with monitoring tools and ticketing systems, plus a strong analytical mindset.
- Excellent problem-solving capabilities and the ability to communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with ITIL principles and practices.
- Understanding of database management systems and SQL.
- Willingness to participate in on-call rotations for urgent support needs.
