## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the product design lifecycle, creating user-focused interfaces that improve interactions and engagement.
- Conduct user testing and research to iteratively develop design solutions that resonate with target audiences.
- Collaborate with engineering and product management teams to ensure technical feasibility and seamless implementation of designs.
- Develop a range of design deliverables including user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity prototypes.
- Keep abreast of industry trends and best practices in UX/UI to inform design strategies and project development.
- Provide constructive feedback and mentorship to less experienced designers to enhance team skill sets and performance.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong expertise in UX/UI design with a compelling portfolio showcasing diverse projects.
- Proficient in CAD software for incorporating technical considerations into design workflows.
- Experience with design tools such as Figma, Sketch, or Adobe XD for prototyping and interface design.
- Comprehensive understanding of user experience principles, usability testing, and research methodologies.
- Excellent communication skills for effective collaboration across multidisciplinary teams.
- Strong attention to detail and a passion for aesthetics in design.

## Other Requirements
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in product design or a related field.
- Familiarity with agile methodologies and collaborative design processes.
- Experience mentoring or leading junior designers is a plus.
- Strong organizational and documentation skills to manage design projects effectively.
