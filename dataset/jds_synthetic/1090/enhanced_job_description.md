## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement scalable full stack applications tailored for the ecommerce industry.
- Develop and maintain RESTful APIs that support mobile and web applications for a seamless shopping experience.
- Collaborate with UI/UX designers to create responsive and user-friendly interfaces leveraging modern JavaScript frameworks.
- Employ microservices architecture to enhance application modularity and maintainability.
- Engage with product management and business teams to define project scope, goals, and deliverables.
- Conduct performance tuning and optimization of existing applications to enhance user experience.
- Mentor junior developers and ensure adherence to coding standards through code reviews and pair programming.
- Research and incorporate new technologies and tools to continuously elevate the ecommerce platform.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- A minimum of 6 years experience in software development, with at least 3 years in ecommerce development.
- Strong proficiency in JavaScript frameworks such as Angular or Vue.js, along with Node.js for backend development.
- Solid understanding of RESTful services and microservices architecture.
- Experience with SQL and NoSQL databases, and familiarity with cloud services (AWS, GCP).
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with a keen attention to detail.
- Strong communication skills, enabling effective collaboration across multi-disciplinary teams.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) practices.
- Understanding of mobile application development practices.
- Experience with Agile/Scrum methodologies and tools such as JIRA.
