## Job Title
Firmware Engineer - IoT Solutions

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Internet of Things (IoT)

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain firmware for IoT devices using C and C++ programming languages.
- Collaborate with product management and engineering teams to gather and validate system requirements for IoT functionality.
- Design and implement low-level driver software to interface with different sensors and actuators.
- Optimize firmware for performance, memory usage, and power efficiency in embedded IoT devices.
- Execute comprehensive testing and validation of firmware to ensure it meets the required specifications and performance standards.
- Engage in peer code reviews, contributing to team knowledge and adherence to coding standards.
- Work closely with software and hardware teams to ensure seamless integration of the firmware with various hardware platforms.
- Stay informed about emerging technologies and best practices in IoT firmware development.
- Prepare technical documentation for firmware architecture, design, and implementation processes.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- At least 4 years of experience in firmware development for embedded systems, with a focus on IoT applications.
- Solid understanding of firmware architecture and design principles for IoT devices.
- Experience with peripheral interfaces and protocols such as MQTT, HTTP, BLE, and Zigbee.
- Proficient in debugging tools and techniques specific to embedded development.
- Familiarity with version control systems like Git and continuous integration tools.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a focus on detail.
- Effective communication skills and ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with additional scripting languages such as Python or JavaScript.
- Knowledge of cloud platforms and IoT protocols for device communication.
- Understanding of Agile development methodologies.
- Certification in IoT development or related technologies.
