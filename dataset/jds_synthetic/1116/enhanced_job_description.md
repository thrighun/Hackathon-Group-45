## Detailed Responsibilities
- Coordinate and lead testing efforts within software development teams, ensuring all testing phases are effectively completed.
- Establish and document test strategies and plans that align with product goals for each project.
- Design and implement comprehensive test cases covering user interfaces, APIs, and data handling.
- Monitor testing processes and provide actionable insights for continuous improvement to stakeholders.
- Facilitate test sessions and lead defect triage meetings, prioritizing issues with development teams.
- Champion best practices in quality assurance and advocate for improvement in testing methodologies.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams for thorough requirement analysis and effective communication around testing objectives.
- Produce detailed test reports and metrics to track progress and quality, reporting to project managers and product owners.
- Enhance team knowledge through mentoring and training in quality assurance tools and techniques.
- Stay updated with industry trends and emerging technologies to guide the team in adopting new testing frameworks.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- 5+ years of experience in Quality Assurance or Software Testing.
- Proficiency in developing test strategies, plans, and cases for different types of software applications.
- Strong experience with functional, automated, and performance testing methodologies.
- Familiarity with agile methodologies and experience working within Scrum or Kanban frameworks.
- Knowledge of programming languages such as Java, Python, or JavaScript for automation purposes.
- Experience with test management tools and bug tracking systems, such as JIRA or Selenium.
- Understanding of cloud-based technologies and services, especially in AWS or Azure environments.
- Ability to analyze complex systems and identify potential risks in software quality.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in the financial services or healthcare domain preferred.
- Familiarity with API testing tools such as Postman or SoapUI.
- Proven ability to work collaboratively across diverse technical teams.
- Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal, with the ability to present findings clearly to different audiences.
