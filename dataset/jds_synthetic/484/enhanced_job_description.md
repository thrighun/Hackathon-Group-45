## Job Title
Senior Data Analyst

## Location
New York, NY or San Francisco, CA

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Perform in-depth analyses of complex data sets to identify actionable insights and business opportunities.
- Create and optimize data visualization dashboards to present critical metrics to stakeholders.
- Collaborate with product and marketing teams to define key performance indicators (KPIs) and develop reporting frameworks.
- Utilize statistical methods to interpret data trends and forecast future outcomes.
- Conduct data quality assessments and implement validation processes to ensure accuracy of information.
- Lead data-driven projects from inception to execution while providing recommendations based on findings.
- Mentor junior analysts and provide guidance on data interpretation and analysis techniques.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Analytics, Business Analytics, or a related field.
- Minimum of 8 years of experience in data analysis or a related role.
- Proficient in data visualization tools such as Tableau, Power BI, or Looker.
- Strong knowledge of SQL and experience with relational databases.
- Familiarity with statistical analysis software (e.g., SAS, SPSS) and data manipulation in Python or R.
- Excellent analytical skills with a strong attention to detail.
- Ability to communicate complex data insights clearly to both technical and non-technical teams.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with data warehousing solutions and ETL processes.
- Understanding of cloud data services and architecture.
- Previous experience in a startup or fast-growth environment.
