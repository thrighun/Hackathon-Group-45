## Job Title
Data Verification Specialist

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology Services

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Conduct thorough reviews of data entries to ensure accuracy and compliance with established standards.
- Identify and resolve discrepancies by coordinating with data entry teams and other departments.
- Implement and update data verification protocols to enhance data quality.
- Generate and present detailed reports on data quality metrics to management.
- Monitor trends in data integrity and recommend improvements to existing processes.
- Facilitate training sessions for staff on data accuracy and verification techniques.
- Assist in maintaining data privacy and regulatory compliance in all verification processes.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a keen eye for detail.
- Fluency in English and Hindi, both written and spoken, is essential.
- Familiarity with database management systems and data verification tools.
- Proficient in Microsoft Excel and data reporting software.
- Minimum educational qualification: Associate's Degree or relevant certification.
- Previous experience in data verification or data analysis roles is preferred.
- Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment.

## Other Requirements
- Understanding of data protection regulations and best practices.
- Certification in data analysis or quality assurance is a plus.
- Willingness to engage in continuous learning and development within the field.
