## Job Title
User Interface Designer with Figma and Bootstrap Experience

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Create visually appealing and user-friendly interfaces for web applications using Figma and Bootstrap.
- Collaborate with developers and product managers to gather and refine user requirements and create wireframes.
- Design high-fidelity prototypes that effectively communicate design ideas and concepts.
- Conduct user research and usability testing to gather feedback and iterate on designs.
- Stay current with design trends and best practices to provide insights into enhancing user engagement.
- Prepare design specifications and graphics assets for the development team.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design, User Experience Design, or a related field.
- 3-6 years of professional experience in UI design.
- Proficiency in design tools such as Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, and Bootstrap.
- Strong understanding of design principles, typography, and color theory.
- Experience with HTML/CSS and basic knowledge of JavaScript.
- Excellent communication skills and ability to articulate design concepts effectively.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with Agile development processes.
- Experience designing for mobile and responsive platforms.
- Knowledge of accessibility standards in web design.
- Understanding of front-end development processes and collaboration with developers.
