## Detailed Responsibilities
- Gather and preprocess data from diverse sources to prepare for in-depth analysis.
- Conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA) and create comprehensive reports for stakeholders.
- Work closely with product and marketing teams to establish analytics requirements and performance metrics.
- Design and maintain interactive dashboards to visualize key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Execute data quality assessments and propose enhancements for data management practices.
- Contribute to standardizing reporting methodologies and analytical practices within the organization.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in SQL and Excel for efficient data manipulation and analysis.
- Experience with data visualization tools such as Looker or Qlik.
- Strong analytical skills combined with strategic thinking capabilities.

## Other Requirements
- Master’s degree in Business Analytics, Data Science, or a related field is preferred.
- Familiarity with data warehousing and extract, transform, load (ETL) processes.
- Understanding of statistical modeling techniques and their application in business settings.
