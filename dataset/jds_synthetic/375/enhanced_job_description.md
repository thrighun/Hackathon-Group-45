## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and architect robust voice communication systems using Cisco collaboration technologies, ensuring scalability, security, and compliance with industry standards.
- Collaborate with clients to gather requirements and translate them into tailored voice solutions, leveraging cloud-based platforms to enhance communication capabilities.
- Conduct system assessments, identifying opportunities for optimization, migration, and integration of next-gen voice technologies.
- Facilitate technical workshops and demonstrations for clients, effectively communicating the benefits and functionalities of proposed solutions.
- Oversee the end-to-end deployment of voice solutions, ensuring seamless integration into existing systems and minimal operational disruption.
- Monitor and stay abreast of emerging trends and technologies in the voice communications space to continuously enhance solution offerings.
- Provide high-level technical support post-implementation, addressing any advanced issues and ensuring customer satisfaction through effective communication.

- Deep expertise in designing and implementing Cisco collaboration solutions, including CUCM, Webex, and cloud communication technologies.
- Proven experience in voice system assessments and strategic planning for technology upgrades and migrations.

- Familiarity with security protocols and compliance measures relevant to voice communication systems.
- Must have: Experience with cloud-based voice communication solutions and the ability to integrate them with existing infrastructure.
