- Oversee the architecture and design of microservices-based applications, ensuring they align with business objectives and technical feasibility.
- Lead a team of software engineers to develop high-quality, scalable, and performant microservices, promoting best practices in design and development.
- Foster a culture of innovation and continuous improvement within the team while providing mentorship and guidance to junior engineers.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including product management, QA, and cloud operations, to define requirements and deliver robust solutions.
- Drive the implementation of CI/CD pipelines, ensuring efficient deployment processes and high-quality code delivery.
- Stay abreast of the latest trends in cloud computing and microservices architecture, recommending upgrades and solutions to enhance application efficiency.
- Establish coding standards and quality metrics, ensuring compliance throughout the software development lifecycle.

- Strong experience in microservices architecture and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).

- Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to troubleshoot complex issues in production environments.
- Effective communication skills, with a proven ability to present technical information to non-technical stakeholders.
