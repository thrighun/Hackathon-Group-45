## Job Title
Automotive Component Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Global Automotive Manufacturer

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Utilize SolidWorks or Autodesk Inventor for advanced 3D CAD parametric modeling, assembly design, and simulation of automotive components.
- Design and analyze critical automotive components, focusing on braking systems, fuel systems, and drivetrain systems to enhance vehicle performance.
- Conduct performance analysis and vehicle dynamics simulations to validate design integrity and functionality.
- Implement knowledge of advanced manufacturing techniques such as additive manufacturing and composite materials in component design.
- Create and manage detailed technical documentation and specifications for engineering proposals to ensure clarity and compliance with industry standards.
- Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, including testing, production, and procurement, to ensure designs align with manufacturing capabilities and requirements.
- Continuously seek out innovations and improvements in design processes to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of component development.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in SolidWorks or Autodesk Inventor.
- Extensive experience in 3D CAD parametric modeling, assembly design, and simulation.
- Familiarity with design principles related to braking systems, fuel systems, and drivetrain systems.
- Knowledge of vehicle dynamics and performance analysis methods.
- Understanding of modern manufacturing techniques, including additive manufacturing and the use of composite materials.
- Strong understanding of mechanical engineering principles and thermal management systems.
- Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering or a related discipline.
- Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills.

## Other Requirements
- Relevant certifications in CAD software or automotive component design.
- Experience with simulation software for vehicle dynamics analysis.
- Strong communication skills, with the ability to work effectively in multidisciplinary teams.
