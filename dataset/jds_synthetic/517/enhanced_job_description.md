## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement a comprehensive data governance framework that aligns with the organization's strategic objectives and regulatory requirements.
- Collaborate cross-functionally with IT, legal, and business operations to assign data stewardship roles and ensure effective oversight of data assets.
- Perform periodic audits of data governance practices to assess compliance and identify areas for process improvement, ensuring high standards for data quality.
- Serve as the primary liaison for data governance questions and compliance challenges, delivering expert advice on regulatory obligations and best practices.
- Initiate and lead training sessions that promote understanding and adherence to data governance principles across all departments.
- Stay abreast of new regulations and industry trends in data governance, proactively adapting policies and procedures to meet evolving compliance needs.
- Prepare comprehensive reports and presentations for senior management on the status of data governance initiatives, highlighting key metrics and compliance achievements.

## Skill Requirements
- Proven experience in data governance and management, particularly within the information technology sector.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a keen attention to detail.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, capable of engaging with stakeholders at all levels.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with data protection laws and regulations such as GDPR, CCPA, etc.
- Experience with data governance tools and technologies is highly desirable.
- Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities effectively in a dynamic environment.
