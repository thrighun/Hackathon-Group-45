## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the design and deployment of cloud-based solutions to drive organizational efficiency and performance.
- Oversee the architecture of large-scale cloud infrastructure, ensuring seamless integration with existing on-premises systems.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define project scopes, timelines, and performance metrics to align with business objectives.
- Provide Level 3 support for cloud systems and related applications, troubleshooting and resolving complex issues.
- Conduct performance tuning and capacity planning while managing system upgrades, migrations, and optimizations.
- Develop and enforce security measures for cloud systems to protect sensitive data and adhere to compliance standards.
- Mentor junior staff and contribute to technical documentation and system architecture designs.
- Participate in on-call rotations to address urgent infrastructure issues as needed.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- 10-12 years of experience in cloud architecture and systems administration.
- Strong analytical skills with hands-on experience in designing and implementing cloud solutions across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.
- Expertise in Linux server administration and familiarity with UNIX systems.
- Proficiency in cloud architecture frameworks and services, including Kubernetes and Terraform.
- In-depth understanding of networking concepts, TCP/IP, VPN, and firewall configurations.
- Familiarity with DevOps practices and CI/CD tools such as Jenkins and GitLab.
- Scripting experience in Python and Bash for process automation.
- Experience with SQL and NoSQL databases, especially in performance tuning.

## Other Requirements
- Certifications such as AWS Certified Solutions Architect, Azure Solutions Architect Expert, or Google Cloud Certified Professional Cloud Architect are beneficial.
- Excellent communication abilities and capability to thrive in a fast-paced, collaborative setting.
- Demonstrated capacity for mentorship and technical leadership within a team.
