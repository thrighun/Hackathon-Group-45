## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and build robust data pipelines to facilitate the flow of data from source to destination.
- Develop and maintain scalable databases and data storage solutions.
- Collaborate with data scientists and analysts to understand data requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
- Monitor and optimize data pipeline performance and conduct troubleshooting as needed.
- Implement data governance and data quality measures.
- Create documentation for data processes and workflows.
- Stay updated on emerging technologies and best practices in data engineering.
- Ensure compliance with data privacy regulations and security protocols.
- Mentor junior data engineers and provide technical guidance where needed.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, SQL, or Scala.
- Hands-on experience with data warehousing solutions like Snowflake or Redshift.
- Familiarity with big data technologies such as Apache Spark and Kafka.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Proven experience in data engineering, ETL processes, and data warehousing.
- Certification in data engineering or cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).
- Knowledge of machine learning concepts and data modeling.
- Understanding of Agile methodologies in project management.
