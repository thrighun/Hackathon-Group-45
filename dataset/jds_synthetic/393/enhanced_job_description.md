## Detailed Responsibilities
- Serve as the primary contact for customer inquiries and technical support, ensuring timely and effective resolutions.
- Troubleshoot and diagnose hardware, software, and network issues, guiding customers through step-by-step solutions.
- Maintain clear and detailed records of customer interactions in the ticketing system for future reference and knowledge sharing.
- Collaborate with internal teams to achieve continuous service improvement and recommend enhancements based on customer feedback.
- Provide proactive support by identifying trends in customer issues and working on solutions to prevent recurrence.
- Ensure customer satisfaction by conducting follow-up calls and surveys post-resolution to gather feedback and improve service.
- Engage in ongoing training initiatives to enhance technical skills and customer service capabilities.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in troubleshooting technical issues across various platforms and devices.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills for effective customer interaction.
- Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment with a customer-centric approach.
- Familiarity with ITIL frameworks and best practices in IT service management.

## Other Requirements
- Prior experience in a technical support or customer service role is essential.
- Knowledge of networking principles and relevant software applications is preferred.
- Availability to work flexible hours, including evenings and weekends, to support a 24/7 operation.
