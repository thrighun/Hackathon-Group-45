## Job Title

## Location

## Client Industry

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the design and architecture of innovative IoT firmware solutions for smart devices.
- Collaborate with cross-disciplinary teams to define system requirements and design specifications.
- Design and optimize embedded firmware, ensuring interoperability and scalability across devices.
- Drive technical discussions and influence the direction of firmware development and system integration.
- Oversee the implementation of security measures and compliance with industry standards in firmware.
- Mentor and develop a team of engineers, promoting best practices and fostering technical growth.
- Conduct performance analysis and troubleshooting of complex embedded systems.
- Stay current with emerging technologies and trends in IoT and embedded systems.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
- 10+ years of experience in embedded firmware development with a focus on IoT solutions.
- Strong background in architecture design and implementation for embedded systems.
- Proficient in programming languages such as C, C++, and Python, with extensive experience in low-level device interfacing.
- Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail.
- Proven ability to communicate effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Other Requirements

- Knowledge of Agile methodologies and experience leading development teams.
- Familiarity with cloud integration for IoT applications and data management.
