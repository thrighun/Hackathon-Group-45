## Job Title
Automotive Systems Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Leading Automotive Engineering Firm

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Utilize Autodesk Inventor or SolidWorks for advanced 3D modeling, simulation, and prototyping of automotive systems.
- Design and optimize HVAC systems tailored for improved vehicle performance and passenger comfort.
- Conduct materials selection for automotive components, focusing on applications in plastics, metals, and composites.
- Implement and analyze manufacturing processes such as additive manufacturing, CNC machining, and assembly strategies.
- Apply FMEA (Failure Mode and Effects Analysis) and DFM (Design for Manufacturing) principles to enhance product reliability and manufacturability.
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams to integrate design innovations and ensure adherence to industry standards and customer specifications.
- Document design processes and maintain comprehensive technical records.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in Autodesk Inventor or SolidWorks.
- Experience with advanced 3D modeling, simulation, and prototyping for automotive applications.
- Strong background in HVAC system design and optimization.
- Familiarity with materials selection and their applications in automotive engineering.
- Knowledge of manufacturing techniques including additive manufacturing and CNC machining.
- Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Automotive Engineering, or a related field.
- Certifications in relevant design software and methodologies are advantageous.

## Other Requirements
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent communication skills and ability to work effectively within a team environment.
- Enthusiasm for automotive technology and innovation.
