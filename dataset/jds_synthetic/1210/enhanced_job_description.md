## Job Title
Embedded Systems Architect

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and architect embedded systems solutions that integrate software and hardware components effectively.
- Collaborate with product management and engineering teams to gather requirements and translate them into system architecture specifications.
- Develop high-level design documents and detailed specifications for embedded system projects.
- Optimize system performance and efficiency through innovative architectural decisions.
- Conduct feasibility studies and proof-of-concept projects to validate design choices.
- Supervise and mentor software engineers and hardware developers, promoting best practices in system design.
- Conduct system-level testing and validation to ensure reliability and performance benchmarks are met.
- Stay abreast of emerging technologies and methodologies in embedded systems development.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- 8+ years of experience in embedded systems development, with a focus on software and hardware integration.
- Proficient in programming languages such as C, C++, and Python, with a strong understanding of embedded operating systems.
- Extensive experience in system-level design and architecture, including modeling and performance analysis.
- Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Strong leadership and communication skills, with the ability to guide cross-functional teams.
- Familiarity with communication protocols (e.g., I2C, SPI, UART) and microcontroller architectures.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with Agile development methodologies.
- Knowledge of safety standards and regulatory requirements for embedded systems.
- Relevant certifications in embedded systems or software architecture are a plus.
