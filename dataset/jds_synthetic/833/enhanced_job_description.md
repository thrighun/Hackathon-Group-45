- Develop and implement ETL processes for data ingestion, transformation, and storage from diverse data sources.
- Optimize performance and scalability of data storage solutions, ensuring quick access and high availability for analytics.
- Monitor data quality metrics and apply data governance practices to ensure consistency and reliability of data.
- Conduct database design and data modeling activities to create high-performance, cost-effective storage solutions.
- Keep abreast of the latest advancements in data technology, adopting and integrating innovative solutions where applicable.
