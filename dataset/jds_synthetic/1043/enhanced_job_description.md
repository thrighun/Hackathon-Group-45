## Job Title
Senior Data Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect, design, and maintain scalable ETL processes to facilitate data ingestion and transformation for analytics.
- Collaborate with data scientists and analysts to understand data requirements and deliver solutions that enhance data accessibility and usability.
- Optimize data pipelines for performance, ensuring data integrity and reliability across multiple data sources.
- Implement data quality checks and monitor data flows to proactively identify and address data issues.
- Evaluate and incorporate new data technologies and tools to improve data processing efficiency and flexibility.
- Lead data integration projects, working with cross-functional teams to implement best practices and standard operating procedures.
- Document data models, workflows, and system configurations for transparency and future reference.
- Mentor junior data engineers and promote a culture of knowledge sharing within the team.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Engineering, or a related field.
- Extensive experience in data engineering with a particular focus on ETL processes.
- Proficiency in SQL and familiarity with data warehousing solutions (e.g., Snowflake, Redshift, BigQuery).
- Experience with data processing frameworks such as Apache Spark, Apache Kafka, or similar technologies.
- Solid understanding of data modeling and schema design for relational and NoSQL databases.
- Familiarity with cloud data services (AWS, Azure, GCP) and container orchestration (e.g., Kubernetes) is a plus.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to present technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Previous experience in a large-scale data-driven organization or analytics-focused environment.
- Exposure to machine learning and data science projects is desirable.
- Understanding of data governance and compliance frameworks.
- Experience with agile project management and methodologies.
- Participation in data engineering forums or contributions to relevant open-source projects.
