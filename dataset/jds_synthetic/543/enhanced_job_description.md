## Job Title
Data Scientist - AI/ML Applications

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement predictive analytics algorithms to enhance business performance.
- Build and maintain machine learning models for data-driven insights and decision-making.
- Collaborate with various stakeholders to gather and define project requirements.
- Analyze and interpret complex data sets to provide actionable recommendations.
- Develop data visualization solutions to effectively communicate insights and findings.
- Monitor model performance and iterate based on feedback and changing data patterns.
- Stay updated on the latest trends and technologies in AI/ML to inform strategic direction.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong experience in data science, particularly in AI/ML applications.
- Proficiency in statistical methods and predictive modeling techniques.
- Solid programming skills in languages such as Python, R, or Java.
- Familiarity with data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI) and libraries (e.g., Matplotlib, Seaborn).
- Experience with machine learning frameworks (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn).
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Statistics, or a related field.
- 3-10 years of relevant experience.

## Other Requirements
- Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
- Experience working with cloud-based data solutions.
- Knowledge of big data technologies (e.g., Hadoop, Spark) is a plus.
- Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, agile environment.
