## Job Title
Senior Software Engineer - Backend Services

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Information Technology Services

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the development of complex backend systems using Python, ensuring they are robust, scalable, and maintainable.
- Architect backend solutions that integrate seamlessly with front-end components and cloud-based services.
- Conduct thorough code reviews and enforce coding standards to maintain high-quality code across the team.
- Analyze system performance metrics and implement strategies for optimization and efficiency.
- Collaborate closely with product owners and stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into actionable engineering tasks.
- Mentor and train junior developers, fostering a culture of learning and innovation within the team.
- Define and implement security measures to safeguard sensitive data and prevent vulnerabilities.
- Research and adopt new technologies, frameworks, and methodologies to enhance the team’s technical arsenal.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in backend software development with a focus on Python.
- Expertise in Python web frameworks such as Django, Flask, or Tornado.
- Strong comprehension of RESTful and GraphQL API design principles.
- Experience working with SQL (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and NoSQL databases (MongoDB, Cassandra).
- Familiarity with continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) practices and tools.
- Solid understanding of cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud, including microservices and serverless architectures.
- Excellent analytical and troubleshooting abilities.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with Agile development methodologies and project management tools (e.g., Trello, Asana).
- Knowledge of container orchestration tools like Kubernetes is a plus.
- OpenAI or similar AI integration experience is desirable.
- Relevant certifications in software engineering or cloud technologies are highly regarded.
