## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop, implement, and maintain firmware for embedded systems in various applications.
- Collaborate with product managers, hardware engineers, and software developers to specify and refine firmware functionality.
- Conduct thorough testing and validation of firmware to guarantee optimal performance and reliability.
- Optimize firmware for memory and processing efficiency, adapting to different hardware constraints.
- Maintain comprehensive documentation for firmware architecture, design decisions, and troubleshooting guides.
- Identify and resolve firmware-related issues, providing timely support for hardware and software integration.
- Mentor and guide junior engineers, promoting best practices and knowledge sharing within the firmware team.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- At least 3 years of experience in embedded firmware development with a strong focus on low-level programming.
- Proficiency in C/C++ and assembly language for firmware development on microcontrollers.
- Solid understanding of hardware interfaces (PWM, ADC, GPIO) and communication protocols (I2C, SPI, UART).
- Experience with version control systems, ideally Git, and familiarity with continuous integration practices.
- Strong analytical skills and a meticulous approach to testing and debugging firmware.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with development tools such as oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, and in-circuit debuggers.
- Experience with real-time operating systems (RTOS) and embedded Linux is a plus.
- Prior involvement in Agile development methodologies and practices.
- Certifications related to firmware development or embedded systems are highly beneficial.
