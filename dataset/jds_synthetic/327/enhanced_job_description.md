## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop firmware solutions for IoT applications, utilizing C and C++ to meet performance and functionality requirements.
- Collaborate with product management, hardware engineers, and UX designers to establish firmware requirements and technical specifications.
- Conduct rigorous testing and validation of firmware on various hardware platforms to ensure compatibility and reliability.
- Participate in code reviews, offering constructive feedback and improvements to maintain high coding standards and best practices.
- Analyze and optimize firmware performance, focusing on power consumption and resource utilization.
- Document firmware architecture, development processes, and troubleshooting guides to assist team members and users.
- Stay updated on industry advancements and emerging IoT technologies, integrating relevant innovations into firmware development.
- Provide technical support during the deployment of IoT products and assist with firmware troubleshooting as needed.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or related discipline.
- 4-5 years of experience in firmware development for embedded systems, particularly within the IoT domain.
- Strong grasp of low-level programming, microcontroller architecture, and real-time operating systems (RTOS).
- Proficiency in debugging techniques and tools, including JTAG and oscilloscopes for hardware integration testing.
- Experience in developing integration solutions for heterogeneous systems and mixed-signal circuits.
- Strong analytical skills and a commitment to producing quality, maintainable code.
- Excellent communication abilities and a team-oriented mindset.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with wireless communication protocols (e.g., Bluetooth, Zigbee, LoRa, MQTT).
- Knowledge of Agile software development methodologies and their application in firmware projects.
- Experience with security best practices in IoT device firmware development.
- Relevant certifications in IoT, embedded systems, or firmware engineering are advantageous.
