## Job Title
Lead Quality Assurance Engineer

## Location
Bengaluru

## Client Industry
Technology - Multinational Corporation

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead comprehensive testing efforts for web and mobile applications, ensuring high-quality software delivery.
- Design and implement automated testing strategies that provide robust test coverage and efficient performance assessments.
- Collaborate closely with development teams to seamlessly integrate automated testing into CI/CD pipelines.
- Analyze test results meticulously and offer actionable feedback to enhance overall software quality.
- Mentor and guide junior QA engineers, fostering their skills in automation and adherence to industry best practices.
- Document test processes and methodologies comprehensively to improve team workflow efficiency.

## Skill Requirements
- 7+ years of experience in quality assurance with a focus on automation testing.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python or Java for test automation scripting.
- Strong understanding of modern testing frameworks and tools (e.g., Selenium, Appium).
- Experience with CI/CD tools and methodologies (e.g., Jenkins, Git).
- Excellent analytical skills and a detail-oriented mindset.

## Other Requirements
- Previous experience in a lead role within a multinational technology company is preferred.
- Exceptional communication skills and the ability to work effectively within a diverse team environment.
- Familiarity with Agile development methodologies and practices.
