## Detailed Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders, translating them into ServiceNow solution specifications.
- Collaborate with ServiceNow Architects and Developers to design workflows and processes that align with client needs and best practices.
- Conduct detailed analysis of current ITSM and HRSD workflows, identifying areas for improvement and recommending enhancements.
- Assist in the testing and validation of ServiceNow features, ensuring they meet business requirements and specifications.
- Provide training and support to end-users to maximize the value of ServiceNow solutions.
- Develop and maintain documentation for processes, workflows, and system configurations.
- Support the project management team in planning and executing ServiceNow-related projects.
- Engage with stakeholders to facilitate workshops and discussions on process improvements and ServiceNow capabilities.
- Monitor industry trends and emerging technologies to provide informed recommendations for ServiceNow enhancements and integrations.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Information Technology, or a related field.
- 5+ years of experience in business analysis within the ITSM or HRSD domains.
- Proficient understanding of ServiceNow platform functionalities and capabilities.
- Strong analytical skills with a focus on problem-solving and process improvement.
- Experience in gathering and documenting business requirements and translating them into technical specifications.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills to engage effectively with stakeholders.
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies and project management principles.
- ServiceNow Certified Application Developer or equivalent certifications is a plus.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with data analysis and reporting tools.
- Understanding of ITIL processes and best practices related to IT service management.
- Prior experience in working within a consulting or client-facing role.
