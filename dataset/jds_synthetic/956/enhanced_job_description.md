## Job Title
C++ Embedded Systems Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Embedded Systems Development

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain firmware in C++ for various embedded hardware platforms.
- Collaborate with hardware engineers to define firmware requirements and ensure seamless integration with hardware components.
- Implement and optimize low-level drivers and system interfaces to enhance device functionality.
- Conduct rigorous testing and debugging of firmware to ensure high performance and reliability under various operating conditions.
- Contribute to technical documentation, including design specifications and user manuals.
- Participate in design reviews and agile sprints, providing insight into firmware architecture and implementation.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in C++ programming, particularly for embedded systems.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- Demonstrated expertise in developing firmware for microcontrollers and processors.
- Solid understanding of hardware/software interaction and real-time constraints.
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, capable of working independently or as part of a team.
- Proficient in source code management tools and methodologies.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with real-time performance analysis tools and techniques.
- Experience in implementing communication protocols such as SPI, I2C, or UART.
- Knowledge of safety standards and compliance in embedded systems.
