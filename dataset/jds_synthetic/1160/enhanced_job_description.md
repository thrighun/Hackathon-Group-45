- Collaborate with stakeholders to gather and document system requirements for current and future projects.
- Assist in the testing and deployment of system updates, ensuring seamless transitions and minimal disruption to users.
- Monitor system performance metrics to identify potential issues and recommend solutions for optimization.
- Work closely with IT support teams to address any technical issues, escalating when necessary to maintain system integrity.

- Basic knowledge of Linux operating systems and systems administration.
- Familiarity with scripting languages such as Bash or Python for automating tasks.
- Understanding of ITIL framework and basic project management principles.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field (preferred).
- Previous internship or training experience in system analysis or IT support is beneficial.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a focus on detail and accuracy.
- Excellent communication skills to convey technical concepts to non-technical users.
