## Job Title
Data Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, construct, and maintain robust data pipelines to support data processing and analytics.
- Collaborate closely with data scientists and analysts to understand data requirements and optimize data flow.
- Implement ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes to facilitate data ingestion from various sources into data warehouses.
- Ensure data quality and integrity by performing data validation and cleansing operations.
- Monitor and optimize existing data systems for performance and reliability.
- Create and maintain documentation related to data architecture and pipeline workflows.
- Stay updated with industry trends and technologies to continually improve data engineering practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in Python and/or Java for data processing and analysis.
- Strong understanding of ETL processes and data warehousing concepts.
- Experience with big data technologies such as Hadoop, Spark, or Kafka.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field.
- 3+ years of experience in data engineering or related roles.
- Familiarity with cloud data services (e.g., AWS Redshift, Google BigQuery).
- Excellent analytical skills with a strong attention to detail.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with database management systems (SQL and NoSQL).
- Knowledge of data modeling and data architecture best practices.
- Familiarity with data governance and compliance standards.
- Relevant certifications in data engineering or cloud technologies are a plus.
- Ability to work effectively in a team-oriented and fast-paced environment.
