## Job Title
Senior Java Software Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect, develop, and maintain scalable, high-performance Java applications focusing on microservices and APIs.
- Collaborate closely with product management and UX teams to define product requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
- Design and implement RESTful APIs that ensure seamless integration with various front-end applications.
- Conduct regular code reviews to promote best practices and maintain high code quality across the team.
- Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks to enhance system efficiency.
- Mentor and guide junior engineers, fostering a collaborative and innovative team environment.
- Utilize automated testing frameworks and CI/CD pipelines to streamline the development process.
- Stay abreast of industry trends and new technologies to continually enhance the development process and product offerings.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical discipline.
- At least 7 years of experience in Java software development with a strong emphasis on microservices architecture.
- Deep understanding of Spring Boot and Spring Cloud frameworks.
- Proficiency in building and consuming RESTful web services.
- Knowledge of front-end frameworks like Angular or React is desirable.
- Excellent analytical and troubleshooting skills with a keen eye for detail.
- Strong communication and collaboration abilities to work effectively in a team setting.
- Experience with version control systems, particularly Git.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with cloud computing services such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform.
- Experience with container orchestration technologies, specifically Kubernetes.
- Understanding of agile methodologies and experience working in Agile development environments.
- Professional certification in Java or cloud technologies is a plus.
