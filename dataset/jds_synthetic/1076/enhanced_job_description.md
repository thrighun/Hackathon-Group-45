## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement visually appealing user interfaces using React JS while ensuring a cohesive and engaging user experience.
- Conduct user research and usability testing to gather insights and iterate on design solutions based on feedback.
- Collaborate with product managers and developers to translate business requirements into design specifications.
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups to communicate design ideas clearly.
- Ensure that applications are optimized for performance and accessibility standards.
- Stay informed about emerging UI/UX trends and technologies to keep our products ahead of the curve.
- Provide guidance and mentorship on UI/UX best practices within the team.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in React JS, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with a strong focus on user interface design.
- Experience with design tools such as Figma, Sketch, or Adobe XD.
- Solid understanding of user-centered design principles and methodologies.
- Familiarity with version control using Git and collaborative development workflows.
- Basic knowledge of front-end build tools and processes.
- Bachelor's degree in Design, Human-Computer Interaction, Computer Science, or a related field.
- Minimum of 2 years of experience in UI/UX design and development.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with mobile-first design and responsive design techniques.
- Understanding of accessibility standards and best practices in web design.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills for effective collaboration.
- Strong analytical skills and attention to detail in design execution.
