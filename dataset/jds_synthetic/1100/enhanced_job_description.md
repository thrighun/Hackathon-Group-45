## Job Title
Lead Software Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect, develop, and maintain high-performance applications utilizing modern programming practices and microservices architecture.
- Lead a team of software engineers in the design and implementation of robust and scalable software solutions.
- Collaborate with product managers and stakeholders to translate business requirements into technical specifications.
- Review code produced by team members to enforce coding standards and best practices.
- Engage in troubleshooting and debugging complex software issues, providing timely resolutions.
- Drive continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) practices within the team.
- Mentor junior and mid-level developers, fostering a culture of learning and technical excellence.
- Keep abreast of new technologies and evaluate their adoption within existing systems.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Minimum of 7 years of experience in software development with a focus on backend technologies.
- Strong proficiency in Kotlin and familiarity with Java ecosystems.
- Extensive experience with microservices design patterns and RESTful APIs.
- Solid understanding of container orchestration using Kubernetes and Docker.
- Experience with NoSQL and relational databases.
- Exceptional analytical skills and a detail-oriented mindset.
- Proven ability to lead technical teams and work collaboratively across departments.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with cloud-native application development on platforms such as Google Cloud or Azure.
- Knowledge of Agile development methodologies and practices.
- Familiarity with DevOps tools and practices for efficient software delivery.
- Relevant certifications in software development or cloud technologies are advantageous.
