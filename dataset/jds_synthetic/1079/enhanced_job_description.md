## Job Title
Full Stack Developer - Vue.js & Node.js

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Retail

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop the ecommerce platform's back-end services using Node.js and integrate them with frontend Vue.js components.
- Collaborate closely with product managers and designers to create user-friendly and highly responsive interfaces.
- Ensure the application is mobile-friendly and functions seamlessly across various devices.
- Implement and manage RESTful APIs for connecting front-end and back-end services.
- Conduct code reviews and mentor junior developers to promote best practices in coding and design.
- Monitor application performance and optimize for scalability and user experience.
- Keep abreast of emerging technologies and industry trends to drive innovation in development practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum of 4 years of experience in full stack web development.
- Proficiency in Vue.js for front-end development and Node.js for back-end services.
- Strong understanding of JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, and web application architecture.
- Experience with database management systems (e.g., MongoDB, MySQL) and ORM frameworks.
- Familiarity with version control systems such as Git and CI/CD pipelines.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.

## Other Requirements
- Experience working with cloud services (AWS, Azure) for deployment and hosting.
- Understanding of microservices architecture and containerization (Docker).
- Excellent analytical skills and the ability to troubleshoot complex issues.
- Strong interpersonal skills and ability to work effectively within a team environment.
