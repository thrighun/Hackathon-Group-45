## Job Title
Automation Quality Analyst

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and execute automated test scripts for web applications and APIs, focusing on performance and functional testing.
- Collaborate with development teams to understand application requirements and translate them into test cases and scripts.
- Utilize tools such as Postman and Selenium to perform end-to-end testing and ensure cross-browser compatibility.
- Participate in Agile development processes, including sprint planning, stand-ups, and retrospectives to align testing with project milestones.
- Document testing processes, results, and recommendations for improvements to enhance overall quality assurance practices.
- Analyze test results and provide detailed reports to stakeholders, clearly communicating any issues and risks identified during testing.
- Work closely with the DevOps team to integrate testing into the CI/CD pipeline and ensure seamless deployment processes.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field.
- Experience in automation testing with a strong background in JavaScript and relevant testing tools.
- Familiarity with API testing, especially with tools like Postman and testing frameworks.
- Proficient in version control systems such as Git for managing and tracking changes to scripts and documentation.
- Understanding of performance testing concepts and tools to assess application responsiveness and stability.
- Strong analytical skills with the ability to troubleshoot and resolve testing issues efficiently.
- Excellent written and verbal communication abilities for effective collaboration and reporting.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of Agile and DevOps methodologies preferred.
- Experience with containerization technologies such as Docker is a plus.
- Familiarity with security testing principles and tools.
- Relevant industry certifications (e.g., ISTQB, Certified Agile Tester) are advantageous.
