## Job Title
Automotive Electrical Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Automotive Engineering Firm

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Utilize SolidWorks or Autodesk Inventor for 3D CAD modeling, assembly design, and surface modeling.
- Design and develop automotive electrical systems, focusing on wiring harnesses, connectors, and safety components.
- Work on the design of automotive safety systems, including airbags, seat belts, and collision detection technologies.
- Collaborate with manufacturing teams to ensure that designs align with production capabilities, including additive manufacturing and CNC machining.
- Employ design validation tests and simulation tools to verify mechanical performance and reliability of systems.
- Prepare and present technical documentation detailing design specifications and test results.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in SolidWorks or Autodesk Inventor.
- Experience in 3D CAD modeling, assembly design, and surface modeling.
- Familiarity with automotive electrical systems and safety technologies.
- Strong understanding of manufacturing methods, including additive manufacturing and CNC machining.
- Experience in conducting design validation tests and utilizing simulation tools.
- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field.
- Additional certifications in electrical design or automotive safety standards are a plus.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of industry standards related to automotive safety.
- Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.
- Excellent collaboration and communication skills.
