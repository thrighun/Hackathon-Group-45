## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement dynamic user interfaces for eCommerce websites leveraging Vue.js to enhance user experience and engagement.
- Collaborate closely with UX/UI designers to translate wireframes and mockups into high-quality code.
- Engage with product managers to identify user requirements and translate them into innovative technical solutions.
- Conduct thorough testing and debugging to ensure application performance and reliability across various devices and browsers.
- Spearhead efforts to improve website performance and SEO optimization to drive traffic and enhance conversion rates.
- Mentor junior frontend developers, sharing best practices and promoting a culture of continuous improvement and learning.
- Evaluate and integrate third-party services and APIs to enhance eCommerce functionality.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of frontend development experience focusing on Vue.js and responsive design principles.
- Strong proficiency in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with a solid understanding of web accessibility standards.
- Proven experience in developing user-centric eCommerce applications with an appreciation for design aesthetics.
- Excellent analytical skills and attention to detail in coding and design.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with version control systems like Git.
- Experience working in an Agile/Scrum environment.
- Knowledge of backend technologies such as PHP or Ruby on Rails is an advantage.
- Portfolio demonstrating previous projects focused on eCommerce or similar applications.
