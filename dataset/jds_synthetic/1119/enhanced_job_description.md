## Job Title
Node.js Application Architect

## Location
Bangalore or Hyderabad

## Client Industry
Software Development Firm

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect and design innovative, scalable applications focused on performance and high availability, using Node.js and Express.js.
- Lead a team of developers to implement technical solutions, ensuring adherence to best practices in development and deployment.
- Integrate third-party APIs and services to enhance application functionalities while ensuring optimal performance.
- Collaborate with product managers and stakeholders to understand business requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
- Conduct code reviews, mentoring team members, and driving continuous improvement in code quality and development processes.
- Champion Agile methodologies within the development team, facilitating sprint planning and retrospectives to enhance team performance.
- Identify and resolve complex issues in the application lifecycle promptly, ensuring minimal disruption to services.
- Stay abreast of emerging technologies and industry trends to propose enhancements and innovations to existing systems.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5+ years of professional experience in software development with a strong focus on Node.js and Express.js.
- Proven expertise in developing microservices architectures and RESTful APIs.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, with a keen eye for performance optimization.
- Excellent interpersonal and communication skills, capable of articulating technical concepts to non-technical audiences.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with database technologies such as MongoDB or Redis.
- Proficiency in GraphQL for efficient API design and development.
- Familiarity with CI/CD tools and practices to automate deployment pipelines.
- Knowledge of container orchestration tools like Docker and Kubernetes is a plus.
