## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze and document business processes and requirements related to HRSD and PSM.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify opportunities for improvements in ServiceNow functionalities.
- Develop user stories and use cases to guide the development of ServiceNow applications.
- Conduct gap analysis between business requirements and existing ServiceNow capabilities, proposing solutions to address any disparities.
- Coordinate with ServiceNow developers to ensure that implemented solutions meet business needs and specifications.
- Facilitate workshops and meetings with stakeholders to gather feedback and refine requirements.
- Assist in the development of training materials and user documentation for end-users.
- Monitor project progress and provide status updates to stakeholders.
- Support user acceptance testing (UAT) efforts, ensuring solutions are validated prior to deployment.
- Stay updated on ServiceNow releases and industry trends, providing recommendations for potential enhancements.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Information Technology, or a related field.
- 5+ years of experience as a Business Analyst, preferably in a ServiceNow environment.
- Strong understanding of HR services and Project Management processes in a technology setting.
- Experience in gathering requirements, creating business process documentation, and developing use cases.
- Proficient in utilizing ServiceNow functionalities and tools, with a focus on HRSD and PSM.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Strong communication and interpersonal abilities, capable of engaging stakeholders effectively.
- ServiceNow Certified Application Specialist is preferred but not required.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with Agile project management methodologies.
- Familiarity with ITIL processes and best practices.
- Prior experience in a multinational corporation is a plus.
