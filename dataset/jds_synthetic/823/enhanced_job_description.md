## Job Title
Data Engineer – Analytics & Workflow Automation

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology (Multinational Technology Provider)

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain robust data pipelines that support analytics and reporting needs across the organization.
- Collaborate with data scientists, analysts, and stakeholders to understand data requirements and improve data workflows.
- Implement data integration solutions that aggregate data from diverse sources, ensuring data quality and consistency.
- Optimize query performance and maintain database systems to support real-time analytics and decision-making.
- Develop and document ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes to streamline data handling and reporting tasks.
- Automate repetitive data processing tasks using Python and workflow orchestration tools.
- Continuously test and refine data models to align with changing business needs and to enhance data accessibility.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field.
- Proven experience (2+ years) in data engineering or a similar role, with strong proficiency in SQL and Python.
- Familiarity with big data technologies such as Apache Hadoop, Spark, or Kafka.
- Experience with cloud data platforms (e.g., AWS Redshift, Google BigQuery) and data warehousing concepts.
- Strong analytical skills with a keen attention to detail and the ability to troubleshoot data issues collaboratively.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI) to generate insightful reports.
- Experience with CI/CD practices in data engineering workflows.
- Familiarity with machine learning concepts and their application in data processing.
- Relevant industry certifications in Data Engineering or Cloud Computing.
