- Develop and implement custom applications on the ServiceNow platform to meet diverse business needs.
- Work closely with product owners and technical teams to refine project requirements and deliver comprehensive technical documentation.
- Create and manage complex business rules, client scripts, and scheduled jobs within ServiceNow.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to create effective integration solutions using APIs and web services.
- Conduct thorough testing of new workflows and applications, ensuring they meet performance and user satisfaction standards.
- Provide ongoing maintenance and enhancements for existing ServiceNow applications, responding to user feedback and system performance metrics.
- Maintain a robust knowledge base of ServiceNow functionalities and industry best practices.
- Participate in all phases of the software development lifecycle, including design, development, testing, and deployment.
- Mentor junior developers and assist in knowledge transfer sessions to enhance team capabilities.
- Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives and support the adoption of Agile methodologies within the team.

- Demonstrated experience in ServiceNow application development and customization.

- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills with a focus on user experience.
