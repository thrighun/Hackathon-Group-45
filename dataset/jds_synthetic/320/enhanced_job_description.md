## Job Title
Data Scientist & Analytics Engineer

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Information Technology & Services

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement data pipelines to ensure the efficient flow of data for analytics and real-time processing.
- Develop and validate predictive models to extract insights and drive data-informed decision-making across various business units.
- Analyze large datasets to identify trends, patterns, and anomalies using statistical and machine learning techniques.
- Collaborate closely with product managers and software engineers to incorporate analytical insights into product development and feature enhancements.
- Create data visualizations and dashboards to communicate findings to stakeholders and facilitate strategic planning.
- Conduct A/B testing and experimentation to evaluate model effectiveness and optimize business outcomes.
- Document methodologies, data sources, and analytical processes for transparency and organizational knowledge.
- Stay abreast of advancements in data science and analytics tools, applying new techniques to enhance analytical capabilities.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, Mathematics, or a related field.
- Demonstrated experience in data analysis, predictive modeling, and statistical learning techniques.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python or R, with familiarity in data manipulation libraries (e.g., Pandas, NumPy).
- Experience with data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI, D3.js) for presenting complex data insights clearly and effectively.
- Knowledge of relational and non-relational databases (e.g., SQL, NoSQL) and data warehousing concepts.
- Strong analytical skills with an ability to communicate complex findings to non-technical audiences.
- Familiarity with real-time data processing frameworks (e.g., Apache Kafka, Spark Streaming) is a plus.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in deploying machine learning models using cloud services (AWS, Azure, or GCP).
- Understanding of MLOps practices to streamline and automate the model deployment process.
- Knowledge of advanced analytics methodologies, including time series analysis and optimization techniques.
- Active participation in data science communities, conferences, or relevant publications to contribute to the field.
