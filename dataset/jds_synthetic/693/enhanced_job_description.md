## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and optimize integrated vehicle systems, focusing on Electrical Systems, Control Systems, and Vehicle Dynamics.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless systems integration within the automotive architecture.
- Utilize simulation tools like MATLAB/Simulink to model system behavior and performance under various scenarios.
- Implement software solutions for automotive applications, including coding in languages such as C/C++ or Python.
- Conduct testing and validation of vehicle systems to ensure compliance with industry standards and performance requirements.
- Analyze data from diagnostic tools to troubleshoot and resolve system issues effectively.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, Automotive Engineering, or a related field.
- At least 3 years of experience in automotive systems engineering with a focus on Electrical and Control Systems.
- Proficiency in software development, particularly in C/C++ or Python.
- Familiarity with communication protocols, including CAN, LIN, and Ethernet.
- Experience with simulation tools such as MATLAB/Simulink and diagnostic tools.
- Strong analytical skills with a capability to conduct thorough system analysis.

## Other Requirements
- Excellent communication and teamwork skills for effective collaboration.
- Ability to manage multiple projects and deliver results within tight timelines.
- Knowledge of industry regulations and best practices related to automotive systems.
