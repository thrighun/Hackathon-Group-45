## Job Title
Full Stack Developer with Angular and Node.js

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain full-stack web applications using Angular for front-end development and Node.js for back-end services.
- Collaborate with product owners and stakeholders to understand requirements and develop technical specifications.
- Ensure high performance of applications and responsiveness to various requests.
- Write and maintain clean, efficient, and well-documented code across multiple layers of the application stack.
- Implement security and data protection measures throughout the software development lifecycle.
- Facilitate code reviews and mentor junior developers on best practices and innovative solutions.
- Engage in continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) processes to streamline development workflows.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a relevant field.
- 3-6 years of experience in full-stack development with a focus on Angular and Node.js.
- Strong proficiency in JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, CSS, and RESTful services.
- In-depth understanding of front-end technologies and back-end development principles.
- Familiarity with database management systems such as MongoDB or PostgreSQL.
- Excellent analytical and troubleshooting abilities.
- Strong interpersonal skills and ability to work in a collaborative team environment.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure) and containerization technologies (e.g., Docker).
- Knowledge of Agile methodologies and DevOps practices.
- Experience with testing frameworks (e.g., Jasmine, Mocha) and automated testing tools.
- Understanding of application performance monitoring and optimization techniques.
