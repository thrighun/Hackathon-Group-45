## Job Title
Senior Firmware Engineer with IoT Development Experience

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement firmware solutions for Internet of Things (IoT) devices using C and embedded C++.
- Work closely with hardware design teams to ensure seamless integration of firmware with physical components.
- Participate in the full software development lifecycle, from requirement gathering to deployment and maintenance.
- Optimizing firmware performance to ensure energy efficiency and reliability in connected devices.
- Lead troubleshooting and debugging efforts for IoT firmware, resolving complex technical challenges.
- Mentor and support junior engineers, sharing expertise in IoT technologies and firmware development.
- Continuously research and evaluate new IoT technologies and methodologies to enhance product offerings.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
- 5-8 years of experience in firmware development, particularly with IoT applications.
- Strong proficiency in C and embedded C++ programming languages.
- Familiarity with wireless communication protocols such as MQTT, CoAP, or BLE.
- Solid understanding of the embedded firmware development lifecycle and design patterns.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with a focus on performance optimization.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills for effective collaboration.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with development tools such as JTAG, oscilloscopes, and logic analyzers.
- Knowledge of cloud platforms and services for IoT deployment (e.g., AWS IoT, Azure IoT).
- Familiarity with Agile development methodologies and tools.
- Relevant certifications in embedded systems or IoT development.
