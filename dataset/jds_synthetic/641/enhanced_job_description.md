## Job Title
Senior Embedded Software Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement embedded software solutions that meet specific hardware requirements and maximize performance.
- Collaborate closely with hardware engineers to ensure seamless integration between hardware and software components.
- Utilize Python to develop firmware applications that are efficient and scalable.
- Conduct thorough testing and debugging to identify and resolve embedded system issues promptly.
- Lead software development initiatives and coordinate with team members to ensure project milestones are met effectively.
- Provide technical leadership and support to junior engineers, promoting best practices in embedded software development.
- Research and apply emerging technologies to enhance software capabilities and system performance.
- Create and maintain comprehensive documentation for software designs, specifications, and user guides.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- 8+ years of hands-on experience in embedded software development with strong proficiency in Python.
- Solid understanding of real-time operating systems and their application in embedded architectures.
- Demonstrated ability to troubleshoot and optimize embedded systems for performance and reliability.
- Strong analytical skills and attention to detail, with a knack for problem-solving.
- Effective communication skills and ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
- Familiarity with communication protocols such as UART, SPI, and I2C.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with agile development methodologies and tools.
- Knowledge of safety standards and practices in embedded software design.
- Certification in embedded systems or related software engineering methodologies.
