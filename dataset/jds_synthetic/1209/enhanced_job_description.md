## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze large and complex datasets to identify trends, patterns, and insights that support business objectives.
- Develop and implement statistical models and algorithms to enhance data-driven decision-making across the organization.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to define key performance indicators (KPIs) and create dashboards to visualize data insights.
- Perform hypothesis testing and predictive modeling to solve business challenges and optimize processes.
- Utilize machine learning techniques to enhance reporting and data interpretation.
- Communicate findings and recommendations to non-technical stakeholders through compelling visualizations and presentations.
- Stay current with industry trends and research in data science, and integrate relevant findings into ongoing projects.
- Mentor junior data analysts and contribute to a collaborative team environment.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python or R, with experience in data manipulation libraries like Pandas or dplyr.
- Strong understanding of statistical methods, data mining, and machine learning algorithms.
- Experience with data visualization tools, such as Tableau or Power BI, for creating insightful reports and dashboards.
- Solid experience in SQL for querying and managing large datasets.
- Familiarity with cloud-based data services, such as AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure for data storage and processing.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities, with a keen attention to detail.
- Strong communication skills, with the ability to convey complex information to non-technical audiences.
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related field.

## Other Requirements

- Experience with big data technologies, such as Hadoop or Spark.
- Familiarity with version control systems like Git.
- A strong portfolio showcasing previous data science projects and analyses.
