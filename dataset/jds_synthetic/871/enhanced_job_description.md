## Job Title
Backend Software Engineer – Node.js & Cloud Services

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain scalable backend systems using Node.js, ensuring high availability and performance.
- Create and manage RESTful APIs and GraphQL endpoints to facilitate communication between client and server.
- Design data models and manage databases using MongoDB, optimizing for speed and efficiency.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including frontend developers and product owners, to deliver comprehensive solutions.
- Implement security best practices to protect sensitive data and ensure compliance with industry standards.
- Conduct code reviews and maintain documentation to promote knowledge sharing and technical excellence.
- Diagnose and debug production issues, providing timely resolutions to ensure continuous uptime.
- Engage in Agile methodologies, participating in sprint planning, daily stand-ups, and retrospective meetings.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Extensive experience with backend development in Node.js and server-side frameworks.
- Proficient in designing and consuming RESTful APIs and GraphQL services.
- Strong knowledge of cloud services (AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure) and serverless architecture.
- Familiarity with version control (Git) and CI/CD processes.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with a detail-oriented mindset.
- Strong communication skills to collaborate effectively with team members across various locations.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with containerization tools like Docker and orchestration platforms such as Kubernetes.
- Understanding of monitoring and logging solutions (e.g., Prometheus, ELK stack) for production environments.
- Previous work experience in a fast-paced Agile development environment is a plus.
- Certifications in Node.js, cloud services, or database technologies are advantageous.
