## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the design and development of firmware solutions for embedded systems in consumer electronic devices.
- Collaborate with hardware design teams to integrate firmware with new technologies and ensure optimal performance.
- Mentor junior firmware developers and provide guidance on best practices in firmware coding and design.
- Develop and maintain comprehensive firmware documentation, including architecture overviews and interface specifications.
- Implement testing protocols and quality assurance measures to ensure firmware reliability and security.
- Participate in scrum meetings and contribute to sprint planning and retrospectives to enhance team productivity.
- Innovate and propose enhancements to existing firmware systems to improve performance and user experience.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- At least 4 years of experience in firmware development for embedded systems.
- Strong proficiency in C/C++ programming languages and a deep understanding of microcontrollers and architecture.
- Experience with real-time operating systems (RTOS) and low-level hardware programming.
- Familiarity with software development tools such as JTAG debuggers, oscilloscopes, and signal analyzers.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of communication protocols (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Zigbee, etc.) used in consumer electronics.
- Experience with version control systems, particularly Git.
- Familiarity with Agile/Scrum methodologies and experience working in an Agile environment.
- Previous involvement in IoT projects or smart device firmware development is a plus.
