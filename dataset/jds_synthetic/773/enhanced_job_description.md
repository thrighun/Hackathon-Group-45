## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop engaging interfaces for web applications using React and Next.js.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and create user-centered designs.
- Prototype and iterate on designs based on user feedback and testing results.
- Ensure design consistency across multiple web products and maintain a coherent brand identity.
- Conduct user research and usability testing to inform ongoing design improvements.
- Work with developers to bridge the gap between design and technical implementation.
- Document design processes and maintain a design system to streamline development.

- Bachelor’s degree in Design, Computer Science, or a related field.
- 3-6 years of experience in UI/UX design and development using React and Next.js.
- Proficient in design tools like Sketch, Figma, or Adobe Creative Suite.
- Strong understanding of HTML, CSS, and front-end development practices.
- Experience with responsive design and mobile-first methodologies.
- Familiarity with accessibility standards and best practices in design.
- Excellent communication skills and a strong portfolio showcasing your design work.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of user research techniques and usability testing frameworks.
- Experience with version control tools, particularly Git.
- Ability to work collaboratively in an agile environment.
