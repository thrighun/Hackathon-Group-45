## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze large and complex datasets to derive actionable insights and trends that drive business success.
- Develop and implement predictive models using statistical and machine learning techniques.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify business needs and translate them into data-driven solutions.
- Design and conduct experiments to validate hypotheses and improve product offerings.
- Prepare comprehensive reports and visualizations to communicate findings to non-technical stakeholders.
- Monitor and enhance existing data models and ensure their accuracy and scalability.
- Utilize tools such as R, Python, SQL, and Tableau for data analysis and visualization.
- Stay informed about advancements in data science and related fields to continuously enhance the team's knowledge and skills.
- Mentor junior data team members and support their professional growth.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong expertise in statistical analysis and machine learning techniques.
- Proficiency in programming languages like Python, R, and SQL.
- Experience with data visualization tools such as Tableau, Power BI, or similar.
- Strong problem-solving skills with a focus on data-driven decision making.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or a related field.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with big data tools such as Hadoop or Spark.
- Familiarity with cloud computing platforms like AWS or Azure.
- Knowledge of natural language processing (NLP) and time series analysis.
- Background in product development or business analysis.
