## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and manage the full lifecycle of web applications using React for the front-end and Node.js for the back-end.
- Work closely with product managers and UX/UI designers to create user-centric applications that meet business requirements.
- Ensure high performance and responsiveness of applications by optimizing code and managing the interplay between front-end and back-end services.
- Implement database solutions and manage data storage efficiently, ensuring data integrity and security.
- Conduct thorough testing and debugging of applications to deliver high-quality software.
- Collaborate with DevOps teams to deploy applications seamlessly and maintain CI/CD pipelines.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Solid experience in full stack development with React and Node.js.
- Proven proficiency in JavaScript, Express.js, and database management systems such as MongoDB or PostgreSQL.
- Familiarity with front-end frameworks and libraries, along with knowledge of RESTful API development.
- Strong understanding of version control systems, particularly Git, and agile methodologies.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with a keen attention to detail.
- Strong communication skills to effectively collaborate within a team-oriented environment.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with TypeScript and GraphQL is a plus.
- Knowledge of cloud services (AWS, Azure) for deploying and managing applications.
- Understanding of security best practices in web development.
- Familiarity with testing frameworks like Mocha or Chai and performance optimization tools.
