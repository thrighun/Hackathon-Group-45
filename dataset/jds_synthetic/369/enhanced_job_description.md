## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain responsive web applications with a strong emphasis on user experience and accessibility.
- Collaborate with UX/UI designers to translate design concepts into functional web interfaces.
- Ensure web applications are optimized for performance and compatibility across various devices and browsers.
- Write well-structured, maintainable code while following industry standards and best practices.
- Conduct thorough testing and debugging to identify and resolve performance bottlenecks and usability issues.
- Stay informed about the latest trends and technologies in web development and incorporate them into projects as needed.
- Mentor and provide guidance to junior developers in coding practices and problem-solving.
- Contribute to team knowledge-sharing sessions and assist in the development of internal documentation and user guides.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Web Development, Computer Science, or a related field.
- Minimum of 4 years of experience in front-end development, particularly with JavaScript frameworks.
- Strong knowledge of HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript (ES6+).
- Familiarity with responsive design frameworks such as Bootstrap or Foundation.
- Experience working with RESTful APIs and understanding of asynchronous programming.
- Proficient with version control systems such as Git and collaborative workflows.
- Strong analytical and debugging skills with attention to detail.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills for effective collaboration.

## Other Requirements
- Previous experience in the retail or e-commerce sector is preferred.
- Knowledge of other front-end libraries or frameworks like Angular or Svelte is a plus.
- Experience with Agile methodologies and participation in Scrum ceremonies.
- Familiarity with testing frameworks such as Jest or Mocha.
