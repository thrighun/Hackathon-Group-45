## Job Title
Automotive Systems Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Automotive Technology Innovator

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Use Siemens NX or SolidWorks for advanced parametric 3D modeling, assembly, and detailing, with a focus on sheet metal design.
- Collaborate in the design and development of innovative automotive electrical systems, including wiring harnesses and control units.
- Conduct simulation and testing of Thermal Management Systems to ensure optimal performance in automotive applications.
- Apply knowledge of various manufacturing processes such as additive manufacturing, CNC machining, and composite materials to enhance design efficiency.
- Utilize Finite Element Analysis (FEA) to validate designs and ensure reliability under various operational conditions.
- Work closely with cross-disciplinary teams to align design goals with project timelines and customer specifications.
- Generate comprehensive technical documentation, including specifications and design rationale.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in Siemens NX or SolidWorks.
- Experience with 3D CAD parametric modeling, detailing, assembly, and sheet metal design.
- Hands-on experience in designing automotive electrical systems.
- Familiarity with manufacturing processes such as additive manufacturing, CNC machining, and composite materials.
- Understanding of FEA and its application for design validation.
- Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field.
- Relevant certifications in CAD tools and design analysis techniques are advantageous.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in thermal management system design will be beneficial.
- Excellent analytical skills coupled with a detail-oriented mindset.
- Strong interpersonal and collaboration skills to work effectively in team environments.
