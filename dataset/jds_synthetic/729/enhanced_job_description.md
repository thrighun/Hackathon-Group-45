## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze recruitment metrics to enhance the effectiveness of talent acquisition strategies.
- Conduct comprehensive market research to identify trends in talent availability and industry hiring practices.
- Utilize advanced statistical methods to predict future workforce requirements and support strategic planning.
- Collaborate with recruitment teams to evaluate the impact of employment branding and sourcing channels on candidate quality.
- Analyze employee turnover data to develop targeted retention initiatives and improve overall employee satisfaction.
- Create interactive dashboards to visualize key talent analytics and present findings to stakeholders.
- Stay informed about the latest trends in workforce analytics and ensure continuous improvement in data practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Human Resources, Business Analytics, or a related field.
- Demonstrable experience in talent analytics and familiarity with recruitment processes.
- Proficiency in statistical analysis and predictive modeling techniques.
- Strong ability to interpret data and provide actionable insights to non-technical stakeholders.
- Excellent communication and presentation skills, with experience in reporting findings and recommendations.
- Familiarity with data visualization tools such as Tableau or Power BI.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with workforce planning and analysis methodologies.
- Certification in data analysis or human resources preferred.
- Knowledge of database management and query languages (e.g., SQL).
