## Job Title
Embedded Software Developer (Python)

## Location
N/A

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and optimize embedded software solutions utilizing Python for various applications.
- Collaborate closely with firmware engineers and product managers to gather requirements and translate them into functional embedded software.
- Develop and maintain comprehensive documentation for software design and development processes.
- Engage in unit testing and integration testing to ensure high-quality software delivery.
- Analyze and resolve software issues in embedded systems during all phases of development and post-deployment.
- Continuously enhance existing software features and troubleshoot performance bottlenecks.
- Contribute to software architecture discussions and technology direction to align with industry innovations.
- Provide mentorship and guidance to junior developers on best practices in embedded programming with Python.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline.
- 4-5 years of experience in embedded software development with a strong emphasis on Python.
- In-depth knowledge of microcontroller architecture and interfacing for embedded applications.
- Familiarity with embedded Python frameworks and libraries (such as MicroPython or CircuitPython).
- Experience in debugging software and hardware systems using advanced tools and techniques.
- Strong analytical skills and a methodical approach to problem-solving.
- Effective collaboration and interpersonal skills for cross-functional teamwork.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of communication protocols like TCP/IP, MQTT, and Modbus is preferred.
- Understanding of IoT concepts and experience with interconnected embedded systems.
- Exposure to DevOps practices and continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) methodologies.
- Relevant certifications or training in embedded software programming are an added advantage.
