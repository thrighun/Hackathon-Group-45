## Job Title
Front-End Engineer specializing in Vue.js

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and maintain user-friendly web interfaces using Vue.js and associated technologies.
- Collaborate closely with UX/UI designers and backend engineers to ensure cohesive application functionality and aesthetics.
- Implement responsive design principles to provide an optimal user experience across devices.
- Conduct code reviews and mentor junior developers in best practices and coding standards.
- Monitor and enhance application performance and user engagement through optimization techniques.
- Engage in troubleshooting and debugging to maintain high-quality application performance.
- Research and integrate innovative technologies and frameworks to improve workflow and product performance.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 3 years of professional experience in frontend development with a focus on Vue.js.
- Strong understanding of JavaScript (ES6+) and familiarity with Typescript.
- Experience with state management libraries such as Vuex.
- Knowledge of connecting frontend applications to RESTful APIs and GraphQL services.
- Comfortable using Git for version control and collaboration.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities with a keen eye for detail.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills to collaborate effectively within a team.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with Nuxt.js for server-side rendering and static site generation.
- Experience with CSS preprocessors such as SASS or LESS.
- Understanding of Agile methodologies and tools such as Jira or Trello.
- Knowledge of automated testing frameworks and best practices in frontend testing.
