## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement comprehensive cloud architectures tailored to meet specific client needs and business goals.
- Collaborate with sales and technical teams to formulate innovative cloud strategies for new and existing clients.
- Develop and maintain strong relationships with clients to ensure alignment and satisfaction with cloud solutions.
- Conduct workshops and training sessions to educate clients on cloud capabilities and implementation strategies.
- Stay informed on the latest cloud technologies and trends, integrating them into service offerings to maintain competitive advantage.

- Extensive experience with AWS and Azure platforms.

- Strong problem-solving and analytical skills with the ability to design complex solutions.
- Excellent communication and presentation skills, demonstrating confidence in client interactions.

- Proven experience in developing and leading cloud solutions in a client-facing role.
- Ability to work in a dynamic, multicultural, and collaborative environment. 
- Experience in driving sustainability initiatives and incorporating ESG principles into technology solutions.
