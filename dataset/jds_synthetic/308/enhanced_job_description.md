## Job Title
Python Embedded Systems Firmware Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain embedded firmware solutions utilizing Python for a variety of hardware platforms.
- Collaborate with software and hardware teams to define, implement, and integrate innovative features into embedded devices.
- Optimize firmware for enhanced performance, security enhancements, and reliability.
- Create comprehensive testing protocols to validate firmware functionality against specifications.
- Document development processes, design specifications, and testing results for compliance and future reference.
- Research and implement new technologies in embedded systems to continuously improve firmware quality and efficiency.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related discipline.
- A minimum of 2 years of experience in embedded firmware development using Python.
- Strong understanding of embedded systems and low-level programming principles.
- Excellent troubleshooting skills with the ability to solve complex problems in software and hardware integration.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills to effectively collaborate with teams.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with hardware development tools, including oscilloscopes and logic analyzers.
- Experience with version control systems such as Git and continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines.
- Knowledge of industry standards for embedded systems and firmware development best practices.
