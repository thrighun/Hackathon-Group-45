## Job Title
Cloud Infrastructure Security Architect

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and oversee the deployment of secure cloud infrastructure solutions across multiple platforms, with a primary focus on Azure.
- Evaluate and enhance the security architecture of cloud services, ensuring they adhere to best practices and compliance requirements.
- Lead threat modeling and security risk assessments to proactively identify and mitigate potential vulnerabilities in cloud infrastructure.
- Implement and manage Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) systems to monitor, analyze, and respond to security threats in real-time.
- Collaborate with DevOps and engineering teams to integrate security into the software development lifecycle and continuous integration processes.
- Develop incident response plans and provide incident management support during security events to minimize impact on business operations.
- Conduct security training and awareness programs for technical teams to foster a culture of security within the organization.

## Skill Requirements
- A minimum of 8 years of experience in cloud security architecture, with a strong emphasis on Azure and hybrid cloud environments.
- Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Cybersecurity, Information Systems, or a related field.
- Industry certifications such as AWS Certified Security – Specialty, Certified Cloud Security Professional (CCSP), or Microsoft Certified: Azure Solutions Architect Expert.
- Proficient in cloud security frameworks and best practices, including NIST, CIS, and ISO standards.
- Strong experience with infrastructure as code (IaC) tools like Terraform or Azure Resource Manager for secure deployments.
- Excellent analytical skills with a methodical approach to problem-solving and risk management.
- Outstanding communication skills, with the ability to articulate complex security concepts to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with other cloud platforms such as Google Cloud Platform and AWS is advantageous.
- Knowledge of container security and orchestration tools, including Kubernetes and Docker.
- Previous experience in leading cross-functional security initiatives or projects is preferred.
