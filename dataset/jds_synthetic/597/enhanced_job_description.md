## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect and oversee the deployment of hybrid cloud solutions using Microsoft Azure, ensuring optimal performance for healthcare applications.
- Work closely with IT teams and clinical staff to develop cloud infrastructure that supports data analytics, patient management systems, and telehealth platforms.
- Maintain compliance with healthcare regulations, including GDPR and HIPAA, ensuring the protection of sensitive patient data.
- Lead workshops and strategy sessions to educate stakeholders on cloud functionalities and best practices in cloud migration.
- Monitor cloud performance and resource utilization, implementing enhancements and optimizations as necessary to meet evolving organizational needs.

## Skill Requirements
- At least 8 years of experience in cloud architecture, with a strong focus on Microsoft Azure services.
- Deep knowledge of hybrid cloud infrastructures and services such as Azure Virtual Machines, Azure Functions, Azure SQL Database, and Azure DevOps.
- Experience in the healthcare sector, specifically in designing solutions that address challenges in patient data management and regulatory compliance.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field; Microsoft Azure certifications are highly preferred.
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, with the ability to navigate complex environments.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with containerization technologies (e.g., Kubernetes, Azure Container Instances) is advantageous.
- Understanding of data governance and security frameworks relevant to the healthcare industry.
- Excellent communication skills to effectively collaborate with technical and non-technical stakeholders, presenting ideas clearly and confidently.
