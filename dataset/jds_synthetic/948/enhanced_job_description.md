## Job Title
Data Coordinator

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology Services

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Manage the collection, organization, and maintenance of data across various projects to ensure accuracy and accessibility.
- Collaborate with project managers and other stakeholders to gather and clarify data requirements for ongoing initiatives.
- Conduct data analysis to identify trends, discrepancies, and opportunities for process improvement.
- Develop and implement data management strategies to enhance data quality and reporting accuracy.
- Support the creation of dashboards and visual data reports for senior management and stakeholders.
- Ensure compliance with data protection regulations and internal policies regarding data handling.
- Train and assist team members on best practices for data entry and management.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong data management skills with proficiency in data analysis tools and techniques.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills in both English and Hindi.
- Attention to detail and problem-solving abilities to effectively address data-related issues.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (particularly Excel) and familiarity with data visualization software (e.g., Tableau, Power BI).
- Minimum educational qualification: Bachelor’s degree in a related field (e.g., IT, Business Administration).
- Previous experience in data coordination, analysis, or a related administrative role is preferred.
- Ability to work collaboratively within a diverse team and adapt to changing project needs.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of data governance and best practices in data management.
- Experience with data management systems or CRM software is a plus.
- Willingness to engage in continuous learning and professional development in data management trends.
