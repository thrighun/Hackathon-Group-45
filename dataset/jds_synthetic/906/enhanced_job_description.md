## Job Title
Frontend Software Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and maintain interactive web applications using Vue.js.
- Work closely with product managers and designers to translate business requirements into technical specifications.
- Implement state management and routing solutions to enhance application performance and user experience.
- Conduct code reviews and maintain high coding standards to ensure quality and efficiency.
- Collaborate with back-end developers to integrate RESTful services and APIs seamlessly.
- Mentor junior developers and provide guidance on best practices in frontend development.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum of 4 years of professional experience in frontend development with a focus on Vue.js.
- Strong understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with an emphasis on modern ES6+ syntax.
- Experience with Vuex for state management and Vue Router for navigation.
- Familiarity with build tools and package managers like Vue CLI, Webpack, and npm.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Proven ability to debug complex issues and optimize applications for speed and performance.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of accessibility standards and best practices for web applications.
- Experience with testing frameworks such as Jest or Mocha.
- Understanding of version control systems, particularly Git.
- Ability to work in an Agile development environment and adapt to changing priorities.
