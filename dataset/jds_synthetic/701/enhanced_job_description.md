## Client Industry

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement software solutions for advanced IoT applications across diverse platforms.
- Collaborate with hardware engineers to design and integrate sensor and device communications.
- Optimize software performance for low-power operation and minimal latency.
- Lead architecture discussions and decisions to ensure best practices in design and implementation.

- Strong understanding of communication protocols (e.g., MQTT, CoAP, HTTP) commonly used in IoT.
- Proven experience with microcontrollers, sensor integration, and real-time data processing.
- Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related discipline.
- Excellent analytical skills with a knack for problem-solving in embedded environments.
- Strong interpersonal skills with ability to work effectively in cross-functional teams.

- Familiarity with Linux-based embedded systems and development environments.
- Previous involvement in agile software development methodologies.
