## Job Title
Embedded Software Engineer (Python & Linux)

## Location
Hyderabad

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and optimize embedded software solutions using Python programming within Linux environments.
- Design, implement, and maintain software components that interact with various hardware interfaces and peripherals.
- Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, including firmware engineers and product managers, to define software requirements and design specifications.
- Conduct thorough testing, validation, and documentation of embedded systems to ensure reliability and performance.
- Lead the debugging of complex software issues and drive the resolution of performance bottlenecks in embedded applications.
- Mentor junior engineers in software development best practices and innovative engineering techniques.
- Stay updated with emerging technologies and industry trends to enhance software capabilities and align with product roadmaps.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or related field.
- 4-6 years of professional experience in embedded software development, emphasizing Python and Linux.
- Strong experience with embedded system architecture and hardware interface protocols (I2C, SPI, UART).
- Proficiency in software testing methodologies, including unit testing and integration testing.
- Familiarity with version control systems, particularly Git, and CI/CD practices.
- Exceptional analytical skills and a strong desire to solve complex engineering problems.
- Excellent collaboration and communication skills to effectively work in a team-oriented environment.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with additional programming languages such as C or Java.
- Knowledge of Agile/Scrum development methodologies.
- Familiarity with real-time systems and their application in embedded development.
- Relevant certifications in embedded systems or software development methodologies preferred.
