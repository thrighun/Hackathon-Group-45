## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze large datasets to uncover trends, patterns, and insights that support business objectives.
- Work closely with stakeholders to gather requirements for data analysis projects and reporting.
- Design and maintain data collection processes and analytical frameworks to facilitate data-driven decision-making.
- Validate and ensure the integrity and accuracy of data through meticulous quality checks.
- Create comprehensive visualizations and reports that effectively communicate analytical findings to diverse audiences.
- Keep abreast of emerging technologies and methodologies in data analytics to propose enhancements to current practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, or a related field.
- At least 2 years of experience in data analysis or a related analytical role.
- Proficiency in data analysis tools such as SQL, R, Python, or Tableau.
- Strong analytical skills with the ability to transform complex data into meaningful insights.
- Excellent communication skills for articulating data-driven insights to non-technical stakeholders.
- Experience with statistical analysis and predictive modeling techniques is a plus.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with big data technologies (e.g., Hadoop, Spark).
- Knowledge of machine learning concepts and their application in data analytics.
- Understanding of data privacy laws and ethics in data management.
