## Job Title
Data Engineer – Cloud & AI Analytics Solutions

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology & Services (Multinational Technology Solutions)

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain scalable data pipelines that facilitate data ingestion and processing for AI analytics projects.
- Collaborate with data scientists to create robust data architectures that support machine learning model training and deployment on cloud platforms (such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud).
- Implement data quality checks and validation mechanisms to ensure the integrity and reliability of datasets used in AI applications.
- Optimize data processing workflows for performance efficiency and cost-effectiveness on cloud infrastructures.
- Develop and manage ETL processes to transform raw data into actionable insights for business intelligence and reporting.
- Partner with software engineers and product teams to integrate data solutions into existing applications and services.
- Monitor and troubleshoot data pipeline performance, addressing bottlenecks and ensuring high availability.
- Stay informed on industry trends in big data, cloud technologies, and AI, applying this knowledge to improve data solutions.
- Contribute to code reviews, documentation, and best practice development to foster a culture of continuous improvement.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Data Science, or a related field.
- Proven experience in data engineering, with a strong portfolio showcasing cloud-based data solutions.
- Proficiency in at least one major cloud platform (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud) and data services within those environments.
- Solid understanding of data modeling concepts, ETL processes, and data warehousing solutions.
- Experience with programming languages such as Python or Scala for data manipulation and analysis.
- Familiarity with big data technologies like Hadoop, Spark, or Kafka.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and collaborative communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with machine learning data preparation and feature engineering.
- Knowledge of data visualization tools and techniques (e.g., Tableau, Power BI) is preferred.
- Familiarity with infrastructure-as-code practices and tools (e.g., Terraform, CloudFormation).
- Relevant cloud certifications (e.g., AWS Certified Data Analytics, Google Data Engineer) are advantageous.
- Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments promoting rapid iteration and collaboration.
