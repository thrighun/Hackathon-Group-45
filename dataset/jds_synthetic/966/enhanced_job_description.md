## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and maintain scalable data pipelines and ETL processes to transform and load data from various sources.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather data requirements and develop data models that support business objectives.
- Ensure data quality and integrity by implementing data validation and cleansing processes.
- Optimize database performance and storage by analyzing and fine-tuning existing data architectures.
- Troubleshoot and resolve data-related issues, ensuring minimal downtime and data loss.
- Document data processes and systems to maintain an up-to-date reference for future projects.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong proficiency in SQL and experience with relational databases like MySQL, PostgreSQL, or Oracle.
- Experience with data warehousing solutions and technologies such as Snowflake or Redshift.
- Familiarity with big data technologies such as Hadoop, Spark, or Kafka.
- Knowledge of programming languages such as Python or Scala for data manipulation and analysis.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with machine learning frameworks and integration into data systems.
- Familiarity with DevOps practices and tools related to data deployment and monitoring.
- Knowledge of continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) principles.
- Experience in Agile methodologies and working in cross-functional teams.
