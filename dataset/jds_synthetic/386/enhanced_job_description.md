## Job Title
MLOps Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement automation workflows for deploying machine learning models into production.
- Collaborate with data scientists to convert models from development to production-ready systems.
- Monitor and optimize ML pipelines for efficiency and scalability.
- Develop and maintain infrastructure as code for machine learning environments.
- Implement CI/CD practices specifically tailored for machine learning projects.
- Ensure robust logging and monitoring mechanisms are in place to detect anomalies in model performance.
- Provide technical expertise to cross-functional teams to drive ML operational excellence.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong experience in MLOps principles and practices.
- Proficiency in software engineering, with a focus on automation and scripting.
- Knowledge of model versioning and performance monitoring tools.
- Familiarity with cloud-based ML services and containerization technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes).
- Understanding of machine learning frameworks and libraries.
- Ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced environment.
- Educational background in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline.
- 3-10 years of relevant experience.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in building robust CI/CD pipelines for ML workflows.
- Strong problem-solving skills with a focus on scalability and optimization.
- Excellent communication skills, able to articulate technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
- Familiarity with agile methodologies and project management tools.
