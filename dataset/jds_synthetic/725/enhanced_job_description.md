## Detailed Responsibilities
- Provide Level 2 support for Windows-based systems, including installation, upgrade, and troubleshooting of server applications.
- Collaborate with engineering teams to design and implement system architecture that meets evolving business needs.
- Oversee integration and security of systems while ensuring compliance with industry standards and best practices.
- Review, test, and deploy system updates and security patches in a controlled environment.
- Participate in disaster recovery planning and conduct recovery exercises to meet business continuity objectives.
- Automate routine operational tasks using PowerShell or other scripting languages to enhance efficiency.
- Provide after-hours support as needed and be part of an on-call rotational schedule.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology or a related field.
- 5-8 years of experience in systems administration and engineering roles focused on Windows environments.
- Strong work ethic with the ability to support and manage a high-availability Windows server environment.
- Experience with Azure Cloud services and Active Directory management.
- Knowledge of virtualization technologies, specifically VMware or Hyper-V.
- Familiarity with network protocols and configuration including DNS, DHCP, and VPN.
- Experience with backup and disaster recovery solutions to ensure data protection.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with configuration management tools such as Ansible or SCCM is a plus.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices is advantageous.
- Relevant certifications such as MCSA, MCSE, or equivalent are beneficial.
- Experience with scripting languages like Python or Bash to streamline processes.
