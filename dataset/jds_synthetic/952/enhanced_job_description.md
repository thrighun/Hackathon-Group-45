## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze business requirements and workflows to identify opportunities for ServiceNow enhancements and optimizations.
- Serve as a liaison between business units and technical teams to ensure the effective use of ServiceNow tools.
- Develop and maintain reports and dashboards to provide insights into service performance and operational metrics.
- Assist in the management of ServiceNow incidents, requests, and changes to ensure efficient service delivery.
- Conduct user training sessions and create educational materials to support end-users in adopting ServiceNow functionalities.
- Monitor system performance and user feedback to propose improvements and best practices.
- Collaborate with IT teams to ensure seamless integration of ServiceNow with existing systems and platforms.
- Participate in project planning and execution, ensuring alignment with overall business objectives.
- Support data migration projects, ensuring accuracy and completeness of data within ServiceNow.
- Stay updated on ServiceNow features and industry trends to make informed recommendations for system improvements.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Computer Science, or related field.
- Experience with ServiceNow data analysis, reporting, and dashboard creation.
- Familiarity with ITSM processes and ServiceNow modules related to service delivery.
- Proficient in creating and maintaining documentation, user guides, and training materials.
- Strong analytical skills and problem-solving capabilities.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to work collaboratively across teams.
- Knowledge of basic programming concepts and exposure to SQL or similar database technologies is a plus.

## Other Requirements
- ServiceNow Certified System Administrator (CSA) is a plus but not mandatory.
- Experience in customer support or service management roles preferred.
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies and project management frameworks.
- Prior experience in multinational settings or large organizations is advantageous.
- A proactive approach to learning new tools and technologies within the ServiceNow platform.
