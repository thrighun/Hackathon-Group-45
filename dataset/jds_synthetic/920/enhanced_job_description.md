## Job Title
Node.js Cloud Developer

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Software Development Firm

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop scalable applications using Node.js and Express with a focus on API development.
- Collaborate with product managers and UX/UI designers to gather requirements and create technical specifications.
- Implement robust backend services that interface with various third-party APIs and microservices.
- Optimize application performance and ensure system reliability and security.
- Provide mentorship and guidance to junior developers, fostering a culture of continuous learning.
- Conduct thorough code reviews and maintain high coding standards across the team.
- Troubleshoot production issues and implement solutions rapidly.
- Stay informed about emerging technologies and industry trends to incorporate them into ongoing projects.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum of 6 years of experience in Node.js and Express development.
- Strong knowledge of API design principles and RESTful services.
- Familiarity with cloud services (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud) for deploying and managing applications.
- Experience with database technologies such as MongoDB, PostgreSQL, or MySQL.
- Excellent analytical and troubleshooting skills in software development.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Additional certifications in Node.js or cloud computing are considered an advantage.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with containerization tools like Docker and orchestration platforms such as Kubernetes.
- Understanding of Agile methodologies and experience working in cross-functional teams.
- Strong communication skills with an emphasis on collaboration and feedback.
