## Detailed Responsibilities
- Engage with stakeholders to gather detailed business requirements and translate them into solution specifications for SAP implementations.
- Conduct gap analysis to identify discrepancies between current processes and desired outcomes.
- Collaborate with technical teams to design and document functional specifications for SAP enhancements.
- Monitor project progress and address any issues that arise during implementation.
- Provide support during user acceptance testing (UAT) and assist in training end-users on new functionalities.
- Develop process documentation and training materials to assist users in adapting to SAP systems.
- Stay updated on industry trends and SAP innovations to provide strategic recommendations.
- Facilitate communication and training sessions for stakeholders to ensure comprehensive understanding of SAP solutions.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Information Technology, or a related field.
- 4+ years of experience as an SAP business analyst or similar role.
- Strong understanding of SAP modules like FICO, MM, and SD, and their integration within business processes.
- Familiarity with business process modeling and project management methodologies.
- Excellent problem-solving abilities and attention to detail.
- Ability to communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with SAP S/4 HANA implementations.
- SAP certification in one or more relevant areas.
- Background in change management and user training within an SAP context.
- Proficiency in process automation tools and data analytics.
