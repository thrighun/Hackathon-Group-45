## Detailed Responsibilities
- Assist in gathering and analyzing data from various internal and external sources to identify key trends and insights that drive improvement.
- Create and maintain reports and dashboards using data visualization tools to effectively communicate analysis results to stakeholders.
- Write SQL queries to extract and manipulate data from databases, ensuring accuracy and relevance in findings.
- Collaborate with various teams to align on objectives and ensure data-driven decisions are made consistently.
- Participate in the design of analytical models and methodologies that aid in project evaluation and strategic planning.
- Provide actionable recommendations based on qualitative and quantitative data analysis, contributing to process optimization and enhancing performance metrics.

## Skill Requirements
- Basic knowledge of SQL and relational databases.
- Familiarity with data visualization tools such as Tableau or Power BI.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- A bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
- Ability to work independently and effectively in a team within a fast-paced environment.
- Understanding of data privacy regulations and best practices.
