- Design and optimize ETL processes to ensure efficient data flow and transformation across various data sources and destinations.
- Implement and manage data quality frameworks to monitor and verify data integrity throughout the data lifecycle.
- Drive continuous improvement initiatives to enhance data processing efficiency, reliability, and performance.
- Stay updated on the latest trends in data engineering and analytics technologies, advocating for the adoption of new tools and methodologies.
- Foster a culture of innovation and professional development within the team, encouraging knowledge sharing and skills enhancement.

- Exceptional analytical and troubleshooting skills, with a strong attention to detail.
