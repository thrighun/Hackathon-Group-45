- Collaborate closely with UI/UX designers to transform design concepts into functional and attractive interfaces.
- Build and maintain RESTful APIs and ensure seamless integration with front-end applications and external services.
- Participate in code reviews, ensuring adherence to best practices and coding standards for enhanced software quality.
- Optimize application performance through monitoring, debugging, and implementing necessary improvements.
- Actively engage in sprint planning and agile ceremonies, contributing insights for continuous improvement.
- Stay updated with the latest industry trends and technologies, proposing innovative solutions to enhance application performance and capabilities.

- Strong troubleshooting, analytical, and communication skills, with an emphasis on teamwork and collaboration.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a relevant field (or equivalent work experience).
