## Job Title
Automotive Component Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Leading Automotive Manufacturer

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Apply expertise in SolidWorks or Autodesk Inventor for advanced 3D CAD modeling and design for manufacturability.
- Develop and design key automotive components, focusing on Brake Systems, Fuel Systems, and Electrical Subsystems.
- Integrate electrical systems within mechanical components to enhance overall vehicle performance and efficiency.
- Utilize simulation tools to test and validate designs, ensuring they meet safety and functionality standards.
- Conduct material selection analysis to optimize product design and performance while considering cost-effectiveness and manufacturability.
- Collaborate closely with electrical and mechanical teams to facilitate a seamless integration of systems in new automotive products.
- Drive continuous improvement initiatives in design methodologies to enhance product reliability and reduce time-to-market.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in SolidWorks or Autodesk Inventor.
- Strong experience with 3D CAD modeling and assembly design.
- Knowledge of electrical systems integration within automotive designs.
- Hands-on experience in designing automotive components including Brake Systems, Fuel Systems, and Electrical Subsystems.
- Deep understanding of material properties and their implications on design and manufacturing processes.
- Familiarity with simulation and analysis tools.
- Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.

## Other Requirements
- Certifications in CAD software or related design methodologies.
- Experience with project management tools and Agile methodologies.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills within multidisciplinary teams.
