## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and establish sophisticated test architectures and frameworks for scalable e-commerce applications.
- Collaborate with development, product, and operations teams to gather testing requirements and implement test automation best practices.
- Execute both manual and automated testing methodologies, focusing on performance and load testing to ensure system resilience under high traffic conditions.
- Implement CI/CD practices utilizing containerization technologies, including Docker and Kubernetes, to streamline testing processes.
- Conduct security testing to identify vulnerabilities and ensure the robustness of web applications against potential attacks.
- Mentor and lead junior testers, providing guidance on testing strategies and fostering a culture of quality within the team.

## Skill Requirements
- 8 to 12 years of experience in software testing with a strong focus on e-commerce applications.
- Proficient in programming languages such as Ruby, Go, or JavaScript, with hands-on experience in relevant testing frameworks.
- Comprehensive knowledge of performance testing tools, such as JMeter or Gatling.
- Strong understanding of cloud environments and experience with CI/CD tools in containerized settings.
- Familiarity with web application security testing principles and vulnerabilities.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in leading testing initiatives and mentoring teams is crucial.
- Knowledge of agile methodologies and a commitment to promoting quality-focused practices within cross-functional teams is essential.
