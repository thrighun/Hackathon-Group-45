## Detailed Responsibilities
- Work closely with cross-functional teams to gather and document data requirements, ensuring alignment with business objectives.
- Design, build, and maintain robust data pipelines that facilitate seamless data flow from diverse sources into analytical systems.
- Implement ETL processes that ensure the integrity, quality, and accessibility of data for business intelligence and analytics.
- Collaborate with data scientists and business analysts to provide high-quality data sets tailored to their analytical needs.
- Oversee the management of data storage solutions, emphasizing data security and compliance with regulatory standards.
- Deliver technical presentations on data architecture and analytics best practices to stakeholders.
- Provide mentorship and support to junior data engineers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related disciplines.
- At least 5+ years of experience in data engineering, particularly within cloud ecosystems.
- Strong proficiency in languages such as Python, SQL, and Java for data manipulation and processing.
- Familiarity with big data technologies, including Hadoop and Spark, as well as cloud-based data services like AWS Redshift and Google BigQuery.
- Deep understanding of data modeling techniques and database design methodologies.

## Other Requirements
- Proven experience in constructing ETL/ELT pipelines using leading frameworks such as Apache Airflow or Talend.
- Proficiency in employing data visualization tools like Tableau and Power BI to present data insights effectively.
- Knowledge of data governance principles alongside experience in maintaining data quality standards.
- Understanding of real-time data processing systems and familiarity with technologies such as Apache Kafka would be advantageous.
