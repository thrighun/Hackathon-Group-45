## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze workforce data to identify opportunities for improving employee engagement and satisfaction.
- Develop predictive models to forecast turnover and recommend proactive retention strategies.
- Utilize behavioral data to design interventions aimed at enhancing employee performance and well-being.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to create engaging data stories that highlight insights and trends within the workforce.
- Implement data-driven initiatives to enhance team collaboration and employee empowerment.
- Regularly review and refine metrics and KPIs to ensure alignment with business goals and employee needs.
- Lead workshops to train HR and management teams on the use of data tools and insights for strategic decision-making.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Business Analytics, Human Resources, Psychology, or a relevant field.
- Strong experience in workforce optimization and data analysis, with a focus on employee engagement metrics.
- Proficient in predictive analytics techniques and tools.
- Excellent storytelling skills with a knack for translating complex data into actionable insights.
- Experience with employee engagement surveys and analysis.
- Strong communication skills, capable of bridging the gap between data analytics and HR practices.
- Familiarity with data visualization and reporting tools (e.g., Google Data Studio, QlikView).

## Other Requirements
- Experience with employee experience design and related methodologies.
- Certifications in analytics, HR management, or organizational psychology.
- Knowledge of programming languages for data analysis (e.g., SQL, Python).
