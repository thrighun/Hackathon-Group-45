## Detailed Responsibilities
- Integrate and optimize electrical, software, and mechanical systems in automotive applications to ensure seamless vehicle performance.
- Utilize system modeling and simulation tools, such as MATLAB/Simulink, to design and validate system architectures.
- Collaborate with electrical and mechanical teams to develop wiring harnesses, sensors, and control units that meet safety and performance standards.
- Conduct diagnostics and testing of integrated systems to identify issues and verify compliance with industry standards, including ISO 26262.
- Manage the development lifecycle of projects using established project management methodologies and tools.
- Participate in design reviews and provide technical guidance to ensure vehicle dynamics and performance requirements are met.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 3 years of experience in automotive systems integration, focusing on electrical and embedded systems.
- Proficiency in system modeling and simulation tools, particularly MATLAB/Simulink.
- Understanding of vehicle networking protocols (CAN, LIN, Ethernet) and their application in automotive systems.
- Familiarity with safety standards, particularly ISO 26262, and their impact on system design and testing.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail.

## Other Requirements
- Excellent communication and teamwork skills.
- Proven ability to manage multiple projects and meet tight deadlines.
- Knowledge of vehicle dynamics and overall automotive performance considerations.
