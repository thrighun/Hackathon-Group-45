## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement robust e-commerce platforms that support high traffic volumes and ensure optimal performance.
- Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, including product managers and UX/UI designers, to convert business requirements into actionable technical tasks.
- Integrate external APIs and services to enrich e-commerce functionalities while ensuring seamless user interaction.
- Conduct routine testing and performance assessments to identify and rectify potential bottlenecks in the platform.

- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
