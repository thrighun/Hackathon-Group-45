## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the integration of electronic systems in automotive applications focusing on ADAS, infotainment, and communication networks.
- Conduct system simulations and performance validations to ensure optimal functionality of integrated components.
- Collaborate with hardware and software teams to define requirements and ensure alignment across development stages.
- Evaluate and apply automotive safety standards, particularly ISO 26262, in the integration process.
- Resolve integration issues and provide technical support throughout the development lifecycle.
- Perform risk analysis and create documentation to support compliance and traceability of systems.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Over 3 years of experience in automotive systems integration with a strong focus on electronic systems.
- Experience with automotive communication protocols like CAN, LIN, and Ethernet.
- Familiarity with ECU development processes and tools.
- Proficient in simulation and validation techniques for electronic systems.
- Knowledge of electromagnetics and automotive safety standards.

## Other Requirements
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
- Ability to thrive in a collaborative, fast-paced environment and manage multiple priorities effectively.
