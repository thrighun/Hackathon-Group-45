## Job Title
Senior Full Stack Developer

## Location
Remote or On-site

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement full-stack applications using Node.js and React, ensuring a seamless user experience.
- Collaborate with product managers, designers, and other developers to deliver high-quality software solutions.
- Develop RESTful APIs and GraphQL endpoints to facilitate data exchange between client and server.
- Write clean, maintainable code and conduct regular code reviews for team members.
- Troubleshoot and debug application issues, optimizing performance and scalability.
- Mentor junior developers, fostering a collaborative team environment and knowledge sharing.
- Research and adopt new technologies to enhance development processes and software quality.

## Skill Requirements
- 6-9 years of experience in full-stack web development, with a strong focus on JavaScript technologies.
- Proficiency in Node.js and React, with a solid understanding of front-end and back-end integration.
- Familiarity with microservices architecture and its implementation.
- Strong knowledge of database systems, both SQL and NoSQL, and experience with data modeling.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with cloud platforms like AWS or Azure is advantageous.
- Understanding of CI/CD tools and practices to enhance deployment efficiency.
- Knowledge of testing frameworks and methodologies to ensure software reliability.
