## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement machine learning algorithms and frameworks that enable generative AI functionalities for diverse applications.
- Collaborate with product teams and stakeholders to identify opportunities for AI-driven enhancements and contribute to the product development lifecycle.
- Develop, test, and deploy scalable ML models, ensuring high accuracy and performance in production environments.
- Optimize and maintain data ingestion and processing workflows for machine learning applications, focusing on automation and efficiency.
- Participate in code reviews and provide mentorship to junior engineers in best practices for machine learning and AI development.
- Create and maintain comprehensive documentation for model architectures, data flows, and technical processes to facilitate knowledge sharing within the team.
- Research and experiment with cutting-edge AI techniques, staying informed about industry trends and advancements.
- Troubleshoot and resolve performance issues in machine learning deployments, ensuring system reliability and responsiveness.

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field.

- Strong proficiency in Python and familiarity with libraries such as TensorFlow, Keras, or PyTorch specifically for generative tasks.

- Experience with data management tools and methodologies, including data preprocessing and feature engineering.
- Familiarity with API design for machine learning services and experience in deploying solutions using cloud infrastructures.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to work independently and collaboratively in a team environment.
