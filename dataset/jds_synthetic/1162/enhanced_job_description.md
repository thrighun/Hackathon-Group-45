## Job Title
Full Stack Developer with Angular and Node.js

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain scalable web applications utilizing Angular for the front end and Node.js for the back end.
- Work closely with UX/UI designers to implement responsive and user-friendly interfaces.
- Collaborate with product management to define and deliver product features and enhancements.
- Ensure code quality and application performance through automated testing and code reviews.
- Continuously monitor application performance and implement optimizations as needed.
- Participate in Agile development processes, including sprint planning and retrospectives.
- Mentor junior developers and promote best practices within the development team.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- 4-6 years of professional experience in full stack development using Angular and Node.js.
- Strong proficiency in TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS3.
- Extensive knowledge of RESTful APIs and microservices architecture.
- Familiar with database technologies such as MongoDB or SQL.
- Experience with version control tools (e.g., Git) and CI/CD pipelines.
- Strong analytical skills with a focus on problem-solving and debugging.

## Other Requirements
- Understanding of containerization technologies like Docker.
- Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS or Azure.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, capable of translating technical jargon for non-technical audiences.
- A passion for learning and staying up-to-date with emerging technologies and industry trends.
