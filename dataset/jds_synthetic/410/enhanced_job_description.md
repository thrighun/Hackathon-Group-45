## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement automated test cases to enhance testing efficiency and coverage across software applications.
- Conduct manual testing of complex features to validate functionality and ensure quality standards are met.
- Identify, document, and track software defects, collaborating closely with software developers to ensure timely resolutions.
- Create detailed documentation for test plans, test cases, and testing outcomes to maintain a clear record of the quality assurance process.
- Engage with product managers and development teams to understand application requirements and provide constructive feedback on usability and performance.
- Act as a mentor for junior QA engineers, providing training and guidance on best practices in quality assurance and testing methodologies.
- Stay updated on the latest developments in QA tools and techniques, recommending improvements to current processes and tools utilized within the team.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in automated testing frameworks such as Selenium, JUnit, or TestNG.
- Strong understanding of performance testing tools like JMeter or LoadRunner.
- Familiarity with test management tools like JIRA or TestRail.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills to effectively identify issues.
- Ability to work in an agile environment, adapting to changing priorities and project needs.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- 3+ years of experience in software testing and quality assurance roles.
- Strong communication skills, both written and verbal, for effective collaboration with team members and stakeholders.
- Knowledge of software development lifecycle and agile methodologies.
