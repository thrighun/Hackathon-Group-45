## Job Title
Automotive Component Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Leading Automotive Engineering Firm

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Utilize Siemens NX or SolidWorks for 3D CAD design, ensuring high-quality modeling, simulation, and prototyping of key automotive components.
- Collaborate on the design of braking systems, safety features, and electronic assemblies, integrating electrical systems seamlessly into mechanical designs.
- Conduct thorough analysis of designs using Finite Element Analysis (FEA) and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) to optimize performance.
- Ensure compliance with automotive regulatory standards by incorporating applicable testing procedures throughout the design process.
- Participate in cross-functional project teams, aligning engineering deliverables with production timelines and customer requirements.
- Continuously adapt and refine design methodologies to drive innovation and improve product reliability.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in Siemens NX or SolidWorks.
- Experience with 3D CAD design, encompassing modeling, simulation, and prototyping.
- Familiarity with electrical and electronics integration in automotive systems is advantageous.
- Strong understanding of automotive regulatory standards and relevant testing procedures.
- Knowledge in FEA and CFD to validate and enhance design integrity.
- Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering or a related discipline.
- Excellent analytical skills and a focus on precision and quality in design.

## Other Requirements
- Relevant certifications in CAD software or automotive component design.
- Experience with additional simulation and analysis software.
- Strong interpersonal skills and ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
