## Job Title
Senior Software Architect - Java and Cloud Technologies

## Location
Bangalore or Hyderabad

## Client Industry
Information Technology Services Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the architectural design and development of robust software solutions leveraging Java and cloud technologies to meet evolving business requirements.
- Mentor and guide the development team, fostering a culture of innovation and adherence to best practices in coding and application design.
- Collaborate with product management and other stakeholders to define and clarify project scope, objectives, and technical specifications.
- Evaluate and recommend appropriate technologies and tools to enhance system performance, scalability, and maintainability.
- Oversee cloud deployment processes and ensure applications are optimized for cloud environments, including security and compliance standards.
- Conduct code reviews and architecture reviews to ensure technical excellence and facilitate continuous improvement within the team.
- Stay abreast of emerging trends in software architecture, cloud technologies, and development methodologies to drive innovation across projects.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in software development with expertise in Java technologies.
- Strong experience with cloud service providers (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud) and cloud architecture principles.
- Proficient in designing scalable and secure software applications, with a focus on backend and microservices architecture.
- Demonstrated ability to lead technical discussions and architect solutions that align with business goals.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with a keen attention to detail.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in implementing CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices to streamline software delivery.
- Familiarity with NoSQL databases and data management strategies.
- Knowledge of front-end frameworks such as Angular or React is a plus.
- Relevant certifications in cloud architecture or Java development.
