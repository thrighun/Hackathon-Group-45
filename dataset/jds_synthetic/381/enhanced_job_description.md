## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze employee performance metrics to identify trends and inform workforce strategy.
- Design and execute studies to evaluate the effectiveness of training and development programs through statistical methods.
- Implement sentiment analysis on employee feedback to measure workplace satisfaction.
- Work closely with cross-functional teams to determine analytics requirements and deliver tailored reports that support talent management.
- Develop and maintain interactive dashboards that provide insights into key employee metrics and trends for stakeholders.
- Stay updated on industry best practices in people analytics and leverage innovative tools to enhance analytics capabilities.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Human Resources, Data Science, Psychology, or a related field.
- Strong background in statistical analysis and workforce analytics.
- Familiarity with sentiment analysis tools and techniques.
- Ability to collaborate with various departments and manage analytics requests effectively.
- Proficient in data visualization tools (e.g., QlikView, Microsoft Excel).
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to convey complex analytics results to non-technical audiences.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with predictive analytics and workforce modeling techniques.
- A certification in People Analytics or Talent Management is a plus.
- Knowledge of statistical programming tools (e.g., SAS, SPSS) is beneficial.
