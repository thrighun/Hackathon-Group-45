## Job Title
Machine Learning Engineer (Computer Vision, AI Applications)

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology Services

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement advanced computer vision solutions using Python, focusing on image processing and object detection.
- Collaborate with product teams to understand user requirements and translate them into machine learning models that enhance product functionality.
- Develop and optimize algorithms for real-time image analysis and recognition tasks, enabling data-driven decision-making.
- Work with cross-functional teams to deploy computer vision models in production environments, ensuring robustness and efficiency.
- Conduct thorough testing and validation of models to ensure high accuracy and performance in diverse scenarios.
- Maintain clear documentation of processes, algorithms, and results to foster knowledge sharing within the team.
- Continuously explore and evaluate new technologies and methodologies in the field of computer vision and AI to improve existing solutions.
- Engage with stakeholders to communicate technical details and demonstrate the capabilities of developed solutions.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
- Proven experience in Python development with a solid understanding of machine learning principles.
- Hands-on experience with computer vision libraries and frameworks (e.g., OpenCV, TensorFlow, Keras).
- Familiarity with deep learning architectures such as CNNs (Convolutional Neural Networks) for image-related tasks.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a keen interest in developing innovative AI applications.
- Ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced, team-oriented environment and manage multiple projects simultaneously.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills for effective collaboration with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with cloud computing platforms (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud) for deploying machine learning solutions.
- Knowledge of MLOps practices to streamline model training and deployment workflows.
- Experience in contributing to open source projects or research in the field of computer vision.
- Awareness of ethical implications and best practices regarding AI and data privacy in developing AI solutions.
