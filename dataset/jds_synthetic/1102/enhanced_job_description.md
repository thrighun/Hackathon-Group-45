## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and deploy machine learning models tailored for natural language processing tasks, including text classification, entity recognition, and language generation.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather and analyze requirements, and transform them into practical NLP solutions.
- Conduct research on state-of-the-art NLP models and techniques, applying findings to improve existing algorithms and model performance.
- Build and maintain robust data pipelines for processing and analyzing large datasets, ensuring optimal data quality and accessibility.
- Perform rigorous testing and validation of NLP systems, documenting methodologies and results to facilitate knowledge sharing among team members.
- Provide technical guidance and mentorship to junior engineers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement within the team.
- Keep updated on advancements in machine learning and NLP, exploring new tools and methodologies to enhance our capabilities.
- Troubleshoot and resolve issues encountered in production, ensuring high uptime and performance of NLP applications.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Linguistics, or a related field.
- Proven experience as a Machine Learning Engineer or similar role, with a focus on natural language processing applications.
- Deep understanding of NLP frameworks and libraries (e.g., spaCy, NLTK, Hugging Face Transformers).
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python and experience with machine learning libraries (e.g., Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch).
- Knowledge of statistical methods and data analysis techniques relevant to NLP.
- Familiarity with cloud services (AWS, Azure) and deployment of machine learning models in production environments.
- Strong analytical skills and the ability to work with large, complex datasets.
- Excellent collaboration and communication skills, with the ability to convey technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
