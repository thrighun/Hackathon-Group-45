## Job Title
Data Scientist - Predictive Analytics

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop predictive models to support data-driven decision-making and enhance business strategies.
- Implement machine learning models for anomaly detection and customer segmentation.
- Conduct thorough statistical analysis on large datasets to derive actionable insights.
- Integrate and deploy machine learning models into production systems to ensure operational efficiency.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather business requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
- Utilize data visualization tools to present findings and communicate results to non-technical stakeholders.
- Continuously evaluate model performance and update methodologies based on new data and feedback.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong background in AI/ML methodologies and predictive analytics.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as R and SQL.
- Experience in conducting statistical analysis on large datasets.
- Knowledge of machine learning techniques, including supervised and unsupervised learning.
- Familiarity with data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI).
- Ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams and communicate technical concepts clearly.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with cloud-based data platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) is a plus.
- Familiarity with the software development lifecycle and agile methodologies.
- Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal.
