## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the design and development of complex user interfaces using modern JavaScript frameworks, ensuring high usability and performance.
- Collaborate closely with product and design teams to translate user needs into functional web features that enhance user engagement.
- Implement and maintain frontend architectures that facilitate reusability and scalability of components across multiple applications.
- Mentor junior developers by providing guidance in code development and promoting best coding practices.
- Conduct performance tuning and optimization of existing applications to improve responsiveness and load times.
- Participate in code reviews, ensuring high code quality and sharing knowledge of new technologies and methodologies.
- Address and resolve frontend-related technical issues, working with other teams to troubleshoot and enhance overall application performance.
- Stay updated with industry trends and emerging technologies to continuously improve our frontend development practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
- Minimum of 4 years of professional experience in frontend development with a strong emphasis on JavaScript frameworks like React or Vue.js.
- Extensive knowledge of HTML5, CSS3, and modern frontend tooling (e.g., Webpack, Babel).
- Proficiency in state management solutions such as Redux or Vuex and other architecture patterns.
- Strong understanding of web performance optimization and best practices for frontend development.
- Experience integrating RESTful APIs and working with backend teams to deliver cohesive solutions.
- Familiarity with version control systems, particularly Git, and collaborative development environments.
- Exceptional problem-solving abilities combined with an eye for detail and a focus on delivering quality user experiences.

## Other Requirements

- Understanding of CI/CD processes and cloud services (AWS, Azure, etc.).

- Familiarity with Agile methodologies and participating in Scrum teams.
