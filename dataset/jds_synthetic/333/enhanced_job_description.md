## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop, customize, and maintain Salesforce applications, ensuring they meet business needs while optimizing user experience.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to gather and analyze requirements, translating them into effective technical solutions.
- Implement Salesforce features using Apex, Visualforce, and Lightning components to enhance functionality and performance.
- Integrate Salesforce with third-party applications and services to streamline processes and data flow.
- Monitor and troubleshoot application performance, resolving issues to provide a reliable user experience.
- Maintain comprehensive documentation for system architecture, configurations, and user instructions.
- Uphold Salesforce best practices and ensure compliance with organizational policies and industry standards.
- Participate in design sessions, code reviews, and sprint planning as part of an Agile development team.
- Mentor junior developers and assist in knowledge transfer within the team.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field.
- Proven experience in Salesforce development, including hands-on experience with Apex, Visualforce, and Salesforce Lightning.
- Strong understanding of Salesforce architecture and ecosystem, including Sales Cloud and Service Cloud modules.
- Familiarity with RESTful APIs and web services for integration purposes.
- Salesforce Certified Developer (Platform Developer I or II) certification is highly preferred.
- Exceptional problem-solving skills and effective communication abilities.
- Experience working in Agile methodologies and environments.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of additional programming languages such as Java or Python is a plus.
- Experience with Salesforce Marketing Cloud or Community Cloud is advantageous.
- Previous experience in a multinational or enterprise environment is desirable.
- Strong analytical and documentation skills.
- Ability to adapt to changing technologies and learn new skills quickly.
