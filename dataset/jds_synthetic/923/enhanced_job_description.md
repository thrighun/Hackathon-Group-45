## Job Title
IT Project Manager

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead and manage technology projects across various departments, ensuring alignment with business goals.
- Develop detailed project plans, including timelines, milestones, and resource allocation.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to gather requirements and define project scopes and objectives.
- Monitor project progress, adjusting resources and priorities as needed to meet deadlines.
- Conduct risk assessments and implement mitigation strategies to address potential project challenges.
- Facilitate communication between technical teams and non-technical stakeholders to ensure understanding and collaboration.
- Prepare regular project status reports for upper management and stakeholders.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Project Management, Computer Science, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in IT project management or similar roles.
- Strong knowledge of project management methodologies (e.g., Agile, Scrum, Waterfall).
- Proficiency in project management tools and software (e.g., JIRA, Trello, Microsoft Project).
- Excellent leadership, negotiation, and conflict-resolution skills.
- Relevant certifications (e.g., PMP, Certified ScrumMaster) are highly desirable.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in budget management and resource allocation.
- Familiarity with software development life cycles (SDLC) and IT service management (ITSM) principles.
- Ability to adapt to changing priorities and manage multiple projects simultaneously.
