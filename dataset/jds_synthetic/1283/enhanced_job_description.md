## Job Title
Data Scientist / AI Product Specialist

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze complex datasets to derive actionable insights that align with product strategies and business goals.
- Develop and deploy predictive models leveraging machine learning techniques to enhance product performance and user experience.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including product development, marketing, and engineering, to define AI-driven product features.
- Create data visualizations and reports to communicate findings and recommendations to stakeholders.
- Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of AI models post-deployment, making iterative improvements as needed.
- Stay abreast of industry trends and emerging technologies to identify opportunities for innovation in product offerings.
- Conduct user research and utilize feedback to refine AI applications and ensure they meet customer needs.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, or a related field.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Python or R, with experience in data analysis libraries like Pandas and NumPy.
- Strong understanding of machine learning algorithms and experience with tools such as Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or Keras.
- Familiarity with SQL and experience with data manipulation in relational databases.
- Excellent communication skills, with the ability to clearly convey technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
- Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving aptitude.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with data visualization tools such as Tableau or Power BI is a plus.
- Knowledge of agile methodologies and experience working in fast-paced environments.
- Previous experience in a product-focused role in a technology company is advantageous.
