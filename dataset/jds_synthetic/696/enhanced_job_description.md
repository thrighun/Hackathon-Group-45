## Job Title
Senior Cloud Solutions Architect

## Location
Remote/On-site options available

## Client Industry
Life Sciences and Healthcare

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and oversee the design of hybrid cloud architectures that balance on-premises and cloud resources to meet the unique needs of Life Sciences and Healthcare clients.
- Work closely with product management and engineering teams to align cloud solutions with business objectives and regulatory standards.
- Implement cloud migration strategies to transition existing applications and data to a hybrid cloud environment seamlessly.
- Evaluate and recommend new cloud technologies, tools, and practices that enhance operational efficiency and innovation in healthcare solutions.
- Lead technical workshops and provide thought leadership on cloud adoption, best practices, and optimizations for healthcare clients.
- Supervise the implementation of cloud security measures and compliance protocols, ensuring adherence to industry regulations such as HIPAA.

## Skill Requirements
- At least 8 years of experience in cloud architecture, specifically with hybrid cloud models.
- Extensive knowledge of cloud service providers, including AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform.
- Proven experience with containerization technologies like Docker and Kubernetes.
- Experience in the Life Sciences and Healthcare sector is essential.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field; advanced degrees preferred.
- AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional or similar certification is highly advantageous.
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, with an ability to work both independently and as part of a team.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with healthcare-specific regulations and standards for data security and privacy.
- Experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools like Terraform or CloudFormation.
- Excellent interpersonal and communication skills to effectively interact with stakeholders at all levels, including non-technical audiences.
