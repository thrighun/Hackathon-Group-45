## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the design and prototyping of robotic surgical systems in alignment with clinical and user requirements.
- Conduct thorough feasibility studies and risk assessments to validate design concepts while ensuring product safety.
- Oversee design reviews, providing critical feedback on project deliverables and maintaining high engineering standards.
- Collaborate closely with regulatory affairs to prepare and submit documentation required for regulatory compliance.
- Analyze performance data post-testing to identify opportunities for design optimization and enhancements.
- Develop and execute comprehensive testing protocols to ensure products meet stringent functionality and reliability criteria.
- Assist in creating training materials and user guides that facilitate effective product use for end-users and sales teams.
- Stay informed about technological advancements in robotics, integrating innovative features into product designs.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Robotics, or related field.
- 7-10 years of experience in engineering design and development, particularly within medical devices or robotics.
- A strong background in surgical robotics or automation systems is highly preferred.
- Familiar with ISO 13485, IEC 62304, and other relevant standards governing medical device design.
- Proficient in CAD software such as SolidWorks or CATIA for complex assemblies and designs.
- Strong analytical skills with the capability to apply engineering principles to solve intricate problems.
- Proven project management skills, successfully leading cross-functional teams through the product development lifecycle.

## Other Requirements
- Ability to mentor junior engineers and guide project teams in adherence to timelines and budgets.
- Strong communication skills to effectively present technical findings to diverse stakeholders and teams.
