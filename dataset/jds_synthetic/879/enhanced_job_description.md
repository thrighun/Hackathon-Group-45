## Job Title
Automotive Systems Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Leading Automotive Manufacturer

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Apply SolidWorks or NX for advanced 3D CAD modeling, focusing on automotive electrical systems and subsystems such as wiring harnesses and control units.
- Conduct Finite Element Analysis (FEA) and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) to validate and optimize designs for performance and safety.
- Design and refine suspension systems, ensuring optimal vehicle dynamics and ride comfort.
- Collaborate with hardware and software teams to integrate electronic components into automotive products seamlessly.
- Ensure design compliance with design for manufacturability principles, facilitating a smooth transition from design to production.
- Lead design reviews and present design iterations to stakeholders for feedback and approval.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in SolidWorks or NX for 3D CAD modeling and simulation.
- Strong experience with Finite Element Analysis (FEA) and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD).
- Background in automotive electrical systems design and integration.
- Understanding of vehicle dynamics and suspension system principles.
- Familiarity with design for manufacturability principles and production methodologies.
- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field.
- Relevant certifications in CAD software and analysis techniques are preferred.

## Other Requirements
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Strong communication and teamwork abilities, especially in cross-disciplinary collaboration.
- Ability to adapt designs based on testing outcomes and stakeholder feedback.
