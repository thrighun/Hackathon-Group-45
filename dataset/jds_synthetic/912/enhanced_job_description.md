## Job Title
Senior Java Software Engineer

## Location
Bangalore

## Client Industry
Global IT Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop robust Java applications leveraging Spring framework and Kubernetes for container orchestration.
- Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, including UX/UI designers and data engineers, to gather and define project requirements.
- Build and maintain GraphQL APIs for efficient data retrieval and integration with diverse client systems.
- Implement best practices for application security, performance, and scalability, ensuring high-quality deliverables through thorough code reviews.
- Identify and resolve complex technical issues promptly, offering ongoing maintenance and support for production systems.
- Take an active role in all phases of the software development lifecycle, from conception through deployment and monitoring.
- Coach and support junior developers, fostering a culture of learning and innovation within the team.
- Keep abreast of new technologies and industry trends, advocating for their integration into the development process.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
- 5-7 years of professional experience in software development with Java and Spring framework.
- Strong expertise in Kubernetes and containerization practices.
- Proficient in building and managing GraphQL APIs.
- Experience with database management systems (SQL and NoSQL) and ORM tools.
- Excellent analytical and troubleshooting skills, with a keen eye for detail.
- Proven ability to thrive in a collaborative team environment.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with cloud services such as AWS, Azure, or DigitalOcean.
- Understanding of frontend frameworks like Vue.js or Angular.
- Experience with Agile methodologies, particularly Scrum and Kanban.
- Relevant certifications in Java, Spring, or Cloud technologies.
- Willingness to work flexible hours when needed.
