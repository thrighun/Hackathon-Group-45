## Job Title
Machine Learning Engineer

## Location
San Francisco, CA or Austin, TX

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop robust machine learning systems and scalable architectures that can handle massive datasets.
- Optimize existing algorithms to enhance performance and efficiency across a variety of applications.
- Collaborate with data scientists and software engineers to integrate machine learning models into production environments.
- Conduct research to innovate and apply cutting-edge techniques in artificial intelligence and deep learning.
- Perform data preprocessing and feature engineering to prepare datasets for model training.
- Monitor and evaluate the performance of models in production, ensuring they meet business objectives.
- Document processes and methodologies, providing clear guidance for future projects and initiatives.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related field.
- Minimum of 8 years of hands-on experience in machine learning engineering or relevant field.
- Strong proficiency in programming languages such as Python, Java, or Scala.
- In-depth knowledge of machine learning frameworks (e.g., PyTorch, Keras, or MXNet) and tools (e.g., Jupyter, Git).
- Experience with software development best practices, including version control and CI/CD pipelines.
- Solid understanding of algorithm complexity and computational efficiency.
- Strong communication skills, capable of articulating technical concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with cloud-based machine learning services (e.g., AWS SageMaker, Azure ML, Google AI Platform).
- Experience with data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI) for presenting model insights.
- Background in developing frameworks for testing and evaluation of machine learning applications.
