## Detailed Responsibilities
- Oversee the entire product development process for advanced imaging systems, aligning product designs with market requirements.
- Lead multidisciplinary teams in innovative design sessions to generate and prototype new concepts.
- Analyze competitive technologies and trends to guide product development strategies.
- Document detailed product specifications and ensure compliance with industry regulations throughout the development process.
- Design and execute robust validation and testing protocols to confirm product performance and reliability.
- Employ advanced statistical methodologies to guarantee high product quality and implement any necessary improvements.
- Collaborate effectively with suppliers and manufacturers to address and resolve any design or production challenges.
- Work alongside marketing to create technical documentation, training materials, and presentations for product launches.
- Actively participate in design reviews, providing insights and updates to stakeholders.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering, biomedical engineering, or a similar field.
- 7-12 years of experience in the medical device industry, specifically with imaging systems.
- Familiarity with FDA regulations and ISO standards related to medical devices.

## Other Requirements
- Proven experience in design thinking and the application of rapid prototyping techniques.
- Proficient in CAD software (e.g., Solidworks, AutoCAD) and simulation tools.
- Strong understanding of manufacturing methodologies and materials utilized in imaging devices.
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
