## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement cloud architectures tailored to client specifications.
- Collaborate with clients to facilitate the migration of on-premise systems to cloud environments.
- Optimize cloud operations for cost efficiency, performance, and security.
- Act as a technical lead during project phases, providing guidance and mentorship to junior team members.
- Develop and present proof of concept demonstrations for clients and stakeholders.
- Keep abreast of advancements in cloud technologies to ensure competitive edge and innovation.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in cloud solution design or related roles.
- Deep understanding of cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, GCP) and their services.
- Strong analytical skills and ability to troubleshoot complex systems.
- Excellent client-facing communication skills and ability to articulate technical concepts.
- Certifications in cloud architecture or related fields (e.g., AWS Certified Solutions Architect) are preferred.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with containerization technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes).
- Familiarity with infrastructure as code (IaC) tools (e.g., Terraform, CloudFormation).
- Knowledge of compliance standards and regulatory requirements in cloud environments.
