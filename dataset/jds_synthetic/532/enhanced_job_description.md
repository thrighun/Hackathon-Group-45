## Detailed Responsibilities
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify data needs and operational challenges, crafting efficient solutions.
- Design and implement robust data architectures that seamlessly integrate AI and ML functionalities.
- Develop and optimize scalable data pipelines capable of handling vast amounts of structured and unstructured data.
- Execute exploratory data analysis to extract insights that guide business strategies and decisions.
- Oversee the deployment and monitoring of AI/ML models, ensuring they are scalable and high-performing.
- Lead initiatives to establish best practices for data management, governance, and quality assurance.
- Document processes and conduct training sessions to uplift team proficiency in data architectures and technologies.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field.
- Minimum of 8+ years of experience in data architecture with a focus on AI and ML solutions.
- Proven expertise with cloud data engineering tools such as AWS Glue, Azure Data Factory, or Google Cloud Dataflow.
- Strong background in big data technologies, including Hadoop and Spark, along with data modeling skills.
- Proficiency in programming languages, especially Python and SQL, as well as in data visualization tools.
- Excellent project management and leadership abilities, with a successful history of leading teams.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with data warehousing solutions and ETL processes is a plus.
- Strong communication skills, capable of conveying complex technical concepts to diverse audiences.
- A proactive problem-solver with a keen analytical mindset.
- Adept at collaborating with technical and non-technical stakeholders to foster a data-driven culture.
