## Job Title
Automotive Systems Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Global Automotive Manufacturer

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Utilize expertise in Siemens NX or SolidWorks to create intricate 3D models, assemblies, and detailed CAD drawings for automotive components.
- Design and develop electrical and electronic systems related to automotive applications, focusing on integration and functionality.
- Collaborate on thermal management and HVAC systems design to enhance vehicle performance and passenger comfort.
- Employ prototyping and testing methodologies to validate designs and ensure they meet regulatory and customer standards.
- Implement FEA and CFD techniques to analyze and optimize designs for performance, durability, and efficiency.
- Liaise with suppliers and manufacturers to ensure designs are feasible for production and align with manufacturing capabilities.
- Lead design reviews and provide technical guidance to junior engineers and cross-functional teams throughout the project lifecycle.

## Skill Requirements
- Expertise in Siemens NX or SolidWorks for advanced CAD modeling.
- Solid experience in creating complex 3D models, assemblies, and detailed drawings.
- Familiarity with automotive electrical systems and their integration.
- Background in thermal management and HVAC systems design.
- Understanding of prototyping and testing methodologies in automotive engineering.
- Knowledge of FEA and CFD applications for design verification and optimization.
- Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.

## Other Requirements
- Relevant certifications in CAD tools and automotive design principles.
- Experience in project management or leading engineering teams.
- Excellent communication, collaboration, and interpersonal skills.
