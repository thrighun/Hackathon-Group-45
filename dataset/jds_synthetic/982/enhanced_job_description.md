## Job Title
Senior Software Engineer - Java Microservices Developer

## Location
Remote / On-site options available

## Client Industry
Leading Software Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect, develop, and maintain scalable microservices using Java, ensuring optimal performance and reliability in distributed environments.
- Collaborate with product management and UX teams to translate business requirements into technical specifications and software solutions.
- Implement automated testing strategies and CI/CD pipelines to enable rapid deployment and integration of new features.
- Conduct performance tuning and optimization of existing microservices to improve efficiency and reduce costs.
- Mentor junior developers, fostering a culture of knowledge sharing and continuous improvement within the team.
- Engage with stakeholders to provide updates on project status and gather feedback to refine development processes.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum of 5 years of professional experience in software development with a strong focus on Java and microservices architecture.
- Proficiency in RESTful API design and development, as well as familiarity with Spring Boot or similar frameworks.
- Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud for deploying applications.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with keen attention to detail and the ability to work independently or as part of a team.

## Other Requirements
- Understanding of containerization technologies like Docker and orchestration tools like Kubernetes.
- Familiarity with Agile/Scrum methodologies and participation in sprint planning and retrospectives.
- Experience with relational and NoSQL databases, as well as basic knowledge of data modeling and database design principles.
