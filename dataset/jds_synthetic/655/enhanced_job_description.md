## Job Title
Mechanical Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Automotive Engineering Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Utilize SolidWorks or Autodesk Inventor for comprehensive 2D and 3D CAD modeling, focusing on complex assemblies and sheet metal design.
- Design and develop electronic housing and components specifically for automotive applications, including infotainment systems and electrical distribution units.
- Conduct thermal and structural analyses to ensure design integrity and performance under various conditions.
- Implement prototyping techniques and rapid manufacturing methods to expedite the development process and validate design concepts.
- Collaborate closely with manufacturing and assembly teams to ensure designs adhere to DFM (Design for Manufacturing) and DFA (Design for Assembly) principles.
- Prepare technical documentation, including design specifications, assembly instructions, and compliance reports, for internal and external stakeholders.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in SolidWorks or Autodesk Inventor with experience in 2D and 3D CAD modeling.
- Strong background in designing electronic components and housing for automotive applications.
- Familiarity with thermal and structural analysis tools.
- Understanding of prototyping techniques and rapid manufacturing processes.
- Knowledge of DFM and DFA principles to enhance manufacturability and assembly efficiency.
- Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering or a related discipline.
- Professional certifications related to CAD software and design best practices are advantageous.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with assembly process optimization will be a plus.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with a keen attention to detail.
- Strong collaboration and communication skills to work effectively in cross-functional teams.
