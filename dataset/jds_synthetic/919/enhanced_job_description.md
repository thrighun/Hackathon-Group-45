## Detailed Responsibilities
- Build and maintain full stack applications utilizing React for front-end and Node.js for server-side development.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather and analyze user requirements, translating them into technical features.
- Implement serverless architecture to enhance application scalability and reduce operational overhead.
- Optimize application performance through effective database management and efficient API design.
- Participate in Agile methodologies, contributing to sprint planning, daily stand-ups, and retrospectives.
- Conduct thorough testing of applications to ensure robustness, performance, and security.
- Create and maintain technical documentation for applications and development processes.
- Stay updated with industry trends and emerging technologies, integrating them when feasible.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or similar discipline.
- 3-5 years of experience in full stack web development with a strong focus on React and Node.js.
- Proficient in JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, and related frameworks.
- Experience with serverless technologies (AWS Lambda, Azure Functions, etc.).
- Familiarity with MongoDB or similar NoSQL databases.
- Understanding of RESTful services and integrating with third-party APIs.
- Knowledge of version control (Git) and CI/CD practices.
- Strong debugging skills and a proactive problem-solving mindset.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with design systems and component libraries.
- Experience with cloud computing services, preferably AWS or Google Cloud.
- Basic understanding of containerization (Docker or Kubernetes) is a plus.
- Exposure to modern testing frameworks (Jest, Mocha, or similar).
- Previous experience in an agile development environment, particularly in a startup setting.
