## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain robust data pipelines to facilitate the processing of large datasets.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather data requirements and deliver timely solutions.
- Monitor data quality and ensure that data governance practices are followed.
- Design and implement data models for efficient data retrieval and storage.
- Optimize data storage solutions using technologies such as Hadoop, Spark, or equivalent.
- Troubleshoot and resolve data-related issues in a timely manner.
- Document data architectures, workflows, and best practices for team reference.
- Stay abreast of industry trends and emerging tools to improve data engineering processes.
- Mentor junior data engineers and contribute to their professional growth.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of professional experience in data engineering or a similar role.
- Proficiency in Python and experience with data processing libraries such as Pandas or Dask.
- Strong knowledge of big data technologies like Hadoop, Spark, and cloud data warehouses (e.g., Redshift, BigQuery).
- Familiarity with SQL and NoSQL databases.
- Experience with data pipeline orchestration tools such as Apache Airflow or similar.
- Understanding of data warehousing concepts and ETL processes.
- Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.
- Excellent communication skills with the ability to work effectively in teams.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud data services is highly preferred.
- Knowledge of data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI) is a plus.
- Familiarity with containerization and orchestration tools (Docker, Kubernetes) is advantageous.
- Exposure to Agile methodologies is beneficial.
- Relevant certifications in data engineering or cloud technologies are desirable.
