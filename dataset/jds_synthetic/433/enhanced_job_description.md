- Lead the integration of complex automotive systems, ensuring seamless communication and functionality across Electrical, Control, Infotainment, and Safety systems.
- Utilize simulation software such as MATLAB/Simulink for system design and performance evaluation.
- Collaborate with hardware and software engineers to resolve integration issues and optimize system performance.
- Implement and adhere to automotive communication protocols, including CAN, LIN, and Ethernet, while maintaining compliance with industry regulations.
- Conduct testing and validation of integrated systems, documenting results and recommending improvements as necessary.
- Participate in project meetings and contribute to the New Product Development (NPD) process from an integration perspective.

- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
- Over 3 years of experience in automotive systems integration or a similar role.
- Proficient in automotive communication protocols such as CAN, LIN, and Ethernet.
- Experience with simulation tools like MATLAB/Simulink for system design and testing.
- Strong understanding of regulatory compliance standards in the automotive sector.
- Excellent problem-solving and critical-thinking skills.
