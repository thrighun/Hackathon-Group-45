## Location
Bengaluru

## Client Industry
Global Software Solutions Company

- Lead the design and architecture of innovative Java applications utilizing Microservices and GraphQL for data querying.
- Work closely with cross-functional teams including UX/UI designers and product owners to translate business needs into technical specifications.
- Develop and manage cloud-based integrations using platforms like AWS and Azure, ensuring seamless connectivity between services.

- Collaborate in agile team sprints, promoting continuous integration and deployment practices.

- Stay abreast of the latest industry advancements and integrate new technologies where applicable to enhance application capabilities.

- Experience with cloud services, particularly AWS or Azure, and familiarity with CI/CD pipelines.

- Excellent analytical skills with a strong focus on problem-solving and troubleshooting.
- Proven ability to work collaboratively in diverse teams and communicate effectively with all stakeholders.
