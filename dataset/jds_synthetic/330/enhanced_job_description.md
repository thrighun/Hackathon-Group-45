## Job Title
Senior Front-End Engineer (Angular)

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement high-quality user interfaces using Angular, focusing on responsive design and accessibility.
- Work closely with UX/UI designers to translate visual concepts into functional applications.
- Collaborate with backend developers to integrate RESTful APIs and enhance application functionality.
- Conduct user testing sessions to gather insights and iterate on design improvements.
- Create reusable components and libraries to streamline future development processes.
- Ensure the technical feasibility of UI/UX designs and optimize applications for performance and scalability.
- Participate in code reviews and mentor junior developers to foster knowledge sharing and best practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related discipline.
- Minimum of 6 years of experience in front-end development with a strong focus on Angular.
- Proficient in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a deep understanding of Angular's core concepts.
- Experience with responsive web design principles and mobile-first development.
- Familiarity with version control systems like Git and continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) workflows.
- Strong problem-solving abilities and a keen eye for detail.
- Excellent teamwork and communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with TypeScript and understanding of its benefits in Angular development.
- Knowledge of accessibility standards and best practices.
- Familiarity with state management libraries like NgRx.
- Experience with testing frameworks such as Jasmine and Protractor.
- Understanding of Agile methodologies and experience working in an Agile team environment.
