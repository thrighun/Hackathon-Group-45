- Monitor and support the performance of IT systems to ensure optimal uptime and reliability.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to maintain the integrity of databases and perform data analyses to inform operational decisions.
- Execute operational tasks such as accurate data entry and meticulous documentation to promote seamless workflows.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in Linux operating systems with practical knowledge of command-line tools.
- Familiarity with SQL for database querying and management.
- Strong analytical abilities with a keen eye for detail and data accuracy.

## Other Requirements
- A Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field is preferred but not mandatory.
- Prior internships or project experience in IT operations or system administration are advantageous.
- Detail-oriented mindset and eagerness to learn new technologies and best practices in IT and operations.
