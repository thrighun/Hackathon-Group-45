## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement server-side applications using Node.js and Express.js.
- Develop RESTful APIs to facilitate communication between the client and server.
- Ensure the responsiveness of applications and manage the interchange of data between the server and the users.
- Integrate user-facing elements developed by front-end developers with server-side logic.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including product managers, designers, and QA testers, to deliver high-quality software solutions.
- Conduct performance tuning and optimization to ensure applications run efficiently under load.
- Participate in code reviews and maintain documentation for codebase and processes.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in JavaScript and familiar with its asynchronous programming model.
- Extensive experience with Node.js and frameworks like Express.js.
- Understanding of RESTful architecture and experience in designing APIs.
- Knowledge of databases, both SQL (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and NoSQL (MongoDB).
- Familiarity with authentication and authorization mechanisms such as OAuth and JWT.
- Experience with version control systems, particularly Git.
- Understanding of containerization technologies, such as Docker, is a plus.
- Strong problem-solving abilities and a keen eye for detail.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- At least 3 years of experience in back-end development.
- Experience with testing frameworks like Mocha or Jest is a plus.
- Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to communicate effectively within a team.
- Commitment to staying updated with industry trends and best practices in application development.
