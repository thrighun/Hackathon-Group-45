## Detailed Responsibilities
- Oversee daily service desk operations to ensure timely support and resolution of customer issues.
- Communicate effectively with clients and technical teams to facilitate quick incident handling.
- Analyze service desk performance metrics to identify areas for process optimization and improvement.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to enhance workflow and implement best practices for service delivery.
- Create and maintain procedural documentation for the service desk to support operational consistency.
- Train service desk agents on protocols and tools to ensure high-quality customer engagement.
- Monitor compliance with SLAs and prepare reports to assess team performance and customer satisfaction.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong organizational and multitasking abilities.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills for effective customer interaction.
- Proficient in providing exceptional customer service in a fast-paced environment.
- Experience with ITIL frameworks and incident management processes is advantageous.
- Familiarity with support and ticketing platforms (e.g., Zendesk, Jira) preferred.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Business Administration, or a related field.
- 1-2 years of experience in a service desk or technical support role is desired.
- Availability to work in rotating shifts to accommodate service desk coverage needs.
