## Job Title
Senior Software Engineer - Backend Systems

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology Services / Technology Solutions

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the design and development of robust backend infrastructure to support large-scale applications.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including UX/UI designers and business analysts, to understand requirements and deliver solutions that meet user needs.
- Develop and optimize efficient data processing pipelines and ensure data integrity across systems.
- Conduct code reviews and provide constructive feedback to maintain high code quality standards and foster developer growth.
- Architect microservices to improve system scalability and resilience while facilitating deployment in cloud environments.
- Implement monitoring and logging solutions to proactively identify and resolve performance bottlenecks and system failures.
- Engage in system architecture discussions, offering insights based on industry best practices and emerging technologies.
- Mentor junior engineers, promoting best coding practices and agile development methodologies within the team.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or related discipline.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in backend software development with a focus on Python.
- Expertise in frameworks such as Django, Flask, or FastAPI, and proficiency in writing RESTful services.
- Extensive experience in database management, both SQL (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and NoSQL (MongoDB, Cassandra).
- Strong familiarity with Docker and orchestration tools like Kubernetes for deploying applications.
- Advanced knowledge of cloud services (AWS, Azure, GCP) and their respective backend offerings.
- Proven ability to troubleshoot complex issues and implement effective solutions quickly.
- Excellent teamwork, communication, and interpersonal skills, enabling effective collaboration in a multi-disciplinary setting.

## Other Requirements
- Experience working within an Agile development environment, utilizing tools like Jira and Confluence.
- Familiarity with continuous integration and deployment practices, including tools like Jenkins or CircleCI.
- Knowledge of application security practices and ability to integrate them into the development lifecycle.
- Relevant certifications in software development or cloud technologies are advantageous.
