## Job Title
AI/ML Product Manager

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology and Software Development

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Define and manage the product strategy and roadmap for AI-driven applications.
- Collaborate with engineering teams to oversee the development and maintenance of intelligent data analytics tools.
- Analyze market trends and customer feedback to prioritize features and enhancements for AI products.
- Ensure seamless integration of AI capabilities into existing product ecosystems, aligning with business objectives.
- Monitor compliance with industry regulations and standards throughout the product lifecycle.
- Facilitate communication between cross-functional teams, including engineering, marketing, and sales, to deliver cohesive AI solutions.
- Measure and report on the success of AI products, making data-driven decisions for future iterations.

## Skill Requirements
- Proven experience in product management with a strong focus on AI/ML technologies.
- Understanding of machine learning concepts and ability to translate technical requirements into product features.
- Familiarity with data analytics tools and methodologies.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to make data-driven decisions.
- Excellent communication skills, capable of conveying complex technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Business Administration, or a related field, with relevant certifications in product management (preferred).

## Other Requirements
- Experience with agile development methodologies.
- Knowledge of user experience (UX) design principles related to AI applications.
- Strong leadership abilities and experience managing cross-functional teams.
- A proactive approach to identifying opportunities for product improvement and innovation.
