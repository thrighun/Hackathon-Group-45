## Detailed Responsibilities
- Build, test, and maintain high-quality web applications using Angular framework.
- Work with UX/UI designers and back-end engineers to ensure the seamless integration of user-facing elements.
- Enhance application performance by implementing best practices in front-end development.
- Develop reusable components and libraries for future use while ensuring consistency across the application.
- Participate in code reviews and provide mentorship to junior team members to promote best practices.
- Collaborate with product owners to understand requirements and deliver features that meet business goals.
- Stay abreast of emerging technologies and incorporate them into the development process when beneficial.
- Address issues related to cross-browser compatibility and mobile responsiveness.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent experience.
- 3-6 years of professional experience in front-end development leveraging Angular.
- Proficiency in JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS3.
- Strong grasp of responsive design principles and web performance optimization techniques.
- Familiarity with state management tools like NgRx or services for managing application state.
- Experience with version control using Git and CI/CD practices.
- Strong analytical skills and attention to detail with an ability to solve complex problems.
- Excellent communication skills and a collaborative mindset.

## Other Requirements
- Understanding of RESTful APIs and experience integrating with back-end services.
- Familiarity with automated testing frameworks (e.g., Jasmine, Karma).
- Knowledge of accessibility standards and SEO best practices.
- Experience with agile methodologies and project management tools (e.g., Jira, Trello).
