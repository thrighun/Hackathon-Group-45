## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement comprehensive testing strategies for new features and enhancements.
- Conduct thorough manual and automated testing to ensure software integrity.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure testing aligns with overall product goals.
- Develop and maintain detailed documentation of testing processes and results.
- Actively participate in sprint planning and retrospective meetings to foster quality awareness.
- Mentor junior QA team members on best practices and quality methodologies.
- Research and recommend new testing tools and processes to enhance efficiency.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in a relevant field such as Information Technology, Computer Science, or Software Engineering.
- Minimum of 3 years’ experience as a Software Quality Analyst or similar position.
- Proficient in defect tracking and management tools (e.g., Jira, Bugzilla).
- Strong understanding of testing frameworks and methodologies.
- Experience with continuous integration tools (e.g., Jenkins, CircleCI).
- Effective problem-solving skills coupled with an analytical mindset.
- Exceptional communication and collaboration skills.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with performance testing tools (e.g., LoadRunner, JMeter).
- Basic understanding of scripting languages (e.g., JavaScript, Ruby).
- Quality Assurance certification (e.g., CSTE or similar).
