## Detailed Responsibilities
- Provide first-line technical support to customers, addressing inquiries and guiding them through troubleshooting processes.
- Diagnose and resolve software and hardware issues, documenting each interaction within the ticketing system for future reference.
- Work closely with product development teams to communicate client feedback and assist in the improvement of services and products.
- Stay informed about the latest technologies, updates, and industry standards to ensure reliable and accurate customer support.
- Engage in proactive customer outreach to ensure user satisfaction and identify potential areas for further assistance.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in Linux operating systems and command-line interfaces.
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills, both written and verbal.
- Previous experience in technical support or customer service roles.
- Ability to manage time effectively and prioritize tasks in a fast-paced environment.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with remote support tools and ticketing systems.
- A degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field (or equivalent experience).
- Capability to work with multicultural teams, demonstrating flexibility and adaptability in various situations.
