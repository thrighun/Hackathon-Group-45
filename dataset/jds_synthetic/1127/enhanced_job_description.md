## Job Title
Senior Data Analyst

## Location
New York, NY or Austin, TX

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Extract and analyze business data to identify actionable insights that enhance operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.
- Collaborate with product management and marketing teams to define key performance indicators (KPIs) and develop reporting tools for monitoring progress.
- Implement exploratory data analysis techniques to assess product performance and user engagement metrics.
- Develop dashboards and visual reports to summarize findings and present recommendations to executive leadership.
- Conduct thorough data validation and cleaning processes to ensure accuracy and reliability of reports.
- Mentor junior analysts and support their professional growth through knowledge sharing and guidance.
- Work with data engineers to optimize data collection processes and enhance data accessibility across the organization.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Analysis, Business Analytics, or a related field.
- Minimum of 8 years of experience in data analysis or a related role.
- Strong expertise in data visualization tools such as Tableau, Power BI, or similar platforms.
- Proficiency in SQL and experience with database management systems.
- Familiarity with statistical analysis software (e.g., SAS, SPSS) for conducting in-depth analyses.
- Excellent attention to detail with strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities.
- Ability to communicate complex analyses in a clear and concise manner to various stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with data warehousing solutions and ETL processes.
- Knowledge of programming languages like Python or R for data manipulation.
- Previous experience in a fast-paced technology or e-commerce environment.
