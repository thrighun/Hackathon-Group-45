## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and execute manual and automated test plans, cases, and scripts for e-commerce applications.
- Conduct thorough user acceptance testing (UAT) and performance testing to ensure product quality and reliability.
- Manage continuous integration processes using tools like Jenkins and collaborate with development teams to streamline deployment.
- Validate backend functionality by creating and executing SQL queries against databases.
- Utilize performance testing tools, such as JMeter or LoadRunner, to analyze application responsiveness under various loads.
- Document testing processes and results, and report defects using issue tracking tools like JIRA.
- Engage in Agile ceremonies and collaborate with cross-functional teams to enhance product development.

## Skill Requirements
- 6 to 8 years of experience in quality assurance and software testing, particularly in the e-commerce space.
- Strong proficiency in manual testing methodologies along with automated testing tools.
- Familiarity with CI/CD processes and tools, particularly Jenkins and GitHub.
- Experience with performance testing tools like JMeter or LoadRunner is a must.
- Solid understanding of databases, SQL, and back-end verification techniques.
- Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work collaboratively in an Agile environment.

## Other Requirements
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills to interact effectively with stakeholders.
- Experience in user experience testing and understanding of transaction flows in e-commerce is advantageous.
- Knowledge of Git for version control is preferred.
