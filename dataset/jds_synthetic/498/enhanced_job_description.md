## Job Title
Front-End Developer (Vue.js & Bootstrap) – Digital Marketing Solutions

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Information Technology / Digital Marketing Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain dynamic web applications and landing pages using Vue.js and Bootstrap to enhance user engagement and conversion rates.
- Collaborate closely with marketing teams, UX/UI designers, and backend developers to implement effective digital marketing strategies through web technologies.
- Write high-quality, scalable, and maintainable code that follows best practices in front-end development, performance, and accessibility.
- Create responsive and visually appealing user interfaces that are optimized for various devices and browser environments.
- Integrate various analytics and tracking tools to monitor and enhance user interaction and campaign performance.
- Participate in code reviews and provide constructive feedback to peers while addressing any technical challenges that arise.
- Stay abreast of industry trends and emerging technologies, suggesting improvements and innovations for web applications.
- Engage in Agile methodologies by contributing to sprint sessions and collaborating in cross-functional teams to drive project success.
- Assist in the onboarding and training of junior developers, promoting a culture of teamwork and professional growth.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Web Development, or a related field.
- Minimum 4 years of experience in developing web applications with Vue.js.
- Strong expertise in Bootstrap for responsive design and adaptive interfaces.
- Proficient in HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), and familiarity with RESTful services.
- Experience with version control systems such as Git and familiarity with collaborative tools.
- Understanding of SEO principles and how they relate to web development.
- Excellent analytical skills and attention to detail, with a knack for troubleshooting.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with digital marketing tools and platforms (e.g., Google Analytics, HubSpot) is a plus.
- Knowledge of TypeScript and unit testing frameworks (e.g., Mocha, Chai) is advantageous.
- Exposure to cloud hosting services (e.g., AWS, Heroku) and basic DevOps practices.
- Previous experience in a fast-paced digital marketing environment is preferred.
