## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the quality assurance strategy and planning, ensuring alignment with project timelines and deliverables.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to design and implement comprehensive test plans and test cases.
- Evaluate and enhance current testing methodologies and practices to improve efficiency and effectiveness.
- Mentor and train junior QA team members, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement.
- Conduct risk assessments and provide recommendations for corrective actions based on testing outcomes.

## Skill Requirements
- Primary Skills: Automated Testing Frameworks (e.g., Selenium, JUnit)
- Secondary Skills: Performance Testing (Beginner to Intermediate)
- Proven experience with software testing lifecycle and methodologies.
- Excellent leadership and team management skills.
- Strong documentation skills for creating detailed reports and test documentation.

## Other Requirements
- A minimum of 5 years of experience in quality assurance and software testing roles.
- Familiarity with Agile development methodologies.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field.
- Relevant certifications in software quality assurance (e.g., ISTQB).
