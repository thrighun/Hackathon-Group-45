## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement firmware for IoT devices, ensuring optimal performance and functionality.
- Collaborate with hardware and software teams to gather and analyze requirements for new features.
- Conduct thorough testing and validation of firmware to ensure compatibility with hardware specifications.
- Manage firmware version control and coordinate release schedules in alignment with project timelines.
- Troubleshoot and resolve firmware-related issues, enhancing the reliability of devices in the field.
- Document firmware designs, processes, and testing procedures to ensure clarity and maintainability.
- Mentor junior engineers and lead team workshops to improve coding practices and techniques.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 3 years of experience in firmware development for embedded systems or IoT devices.
- Strong proficiency in C/C++ and experience with low-level hardware programming.
- Familiarity with embedded development tools, such as JTAG debuggers and oscilloscopes.
- Understanding of communication protocols like MQTT, HTTP, and Bluetooth.
- Excellent analytical and troubleshooting skills with a keen attention to detail.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills for effective team collaboration.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with Python or shell scripting for automation tasks.
- Familiarity with Agile development methodologies and tools (e.g., Jira, Confluence).
- Knowledge of security practices in embedded systems is a plus.
- Relevant certifications in firmware development or embedded systems are desirable.
