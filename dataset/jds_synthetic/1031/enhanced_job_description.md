## Job Title
Front-End Architect

## Location
Remote Option Available

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the architecture and design of scalable front-end systems using React.js, ensuring high performance and responsiveness.
- Mentor and guide junior developers in best practices and code optimization techniques.
- Collaborate closely with UX/UI designers to translate designs and wireframes into high-quality code.
- Establish and enforce front-end coding standards, practices, and guidelines across the development team.
- Develop and maintain comprehensive documentation for architecture decisions and coding standards.
- Implement automated testing strategies to enhance the reliability and quality of the applications.
- Monitor application performance and troubleshoot front-end issues, providing timely solutions.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum 7 years of experience with front-end technologies, primarily React.js.
- Strong command of JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, and modern frameworks.
- Deep understanding of state management libraries like Redux or MobX.
- Experience with building responsive and adaptive web applications.
- Proficient in RESTful and GraphQL APIs, and their integration with web applications.
- Understanding of accessibility (a11y) standards and best practices.
- Excellent leadership and communication skills, with a focus on team collaboration.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with TypeScript is highly preferred.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices.
- Knowledge of server-side rendering techniques using Next.js or similar frameworks.
- Experience with performance optimization tools and techniques.
- Understanding of Agile methodologies and experience working in Agile teams.
