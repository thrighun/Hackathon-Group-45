- Analyze automated test results, identify critical issues, and provide actionable insights to enhance software quality.
- Work closely with cross-functional teams to establish testing requirements and ensure that quality is integrated throughout the development lifecycle.
- Provide mentorship and support to junior QA team members, fostering a collaborative environment and technical growth.
- Maintain clear documentation of testing procedures, results, and technical insights for stakeholders and continuous process improvement.
- Explore and implement innovative quality assurance tools and best practices to enhance testing methodologies.
