## Job Title
Senior Software Engineer - Full Stack Developer

## Location
Bangalore or Hyderabad

## Client Industry
International Software Development Firm

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement web applications using Node.js for backend services and React.js for dynamic front-end interfaces.
- Collaborate with UX/UI designers to create intuitive and visually appealing user experiences.
- Manage the software development lifecycle from concept through deployment, ensuring end-to-end functionality.
- Conduct regular code reviews and maintain high standards of software quality through best practices and peer feedback.
- Work closely with product management to gather requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
- Identify and address performance bottlenecks, ensuring optimal application performance and scalability.
- Stay informed about emerging technologies and industry trends, recommending tools and practices that enhance development processes.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- 6-8 years of experience in full stack development, with extensive knowledge of Node.js and React.js.
- Experience with RESTful API design and integration.
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies and the ability to work effectively in fast-paced environments.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills to troubleshoot and optimize applications.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills, capable of working with cross-functional teams.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with database technologies such as MongoDB, MySQL, or PostgreSQL.
- Knowledge of serverless architectures and cloud services (AWS or Azure).
- Familiarity with version control systems like Git and CI/CD pipelines.
- Certifications in Full Stack Development or relevant technologies are an advantage.
