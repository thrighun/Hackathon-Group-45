- Architect and develop state-of-the-art Progressive Web Applications (PWAs) using Next.js to enhance user experience and engagement.
- Collaborate with UX/UI designers to create intuitive and visually appealing interfaces tailored to retail customers.
- Optimize web applications for maximum speed and scalability, ensuring a seamless experience across various devices.
- Lead code reviews and provide constructive feedback to maintain coding standards and best practices within the team.
- Conduct performance testing and troubleshooting to identify and resolve any user experience bottlenecks.
- Stay current with industry trends and best practices, integrating new technologies as appropriate to improve the retail platform.
- Facilitate agile ceremonies and help drive the team towards successful project milestones.

- Strong experience with state management libraries (e.g., Redux, MobX) and RESTful APIs.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities, with a strong attention to detail.
- Proven track record in mentoring junior developers and fostering a collaborative team environment.
