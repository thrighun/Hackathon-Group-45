## Job Title
Cloud Backend Engineer (Node.js, Express, MongoDB, AWS)

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Information Technology & Services

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement high-performance backend systems using Node.js and Express in a cloud environment.
- Develop and manage scalable RESTful APIs that interact with MongoDB databases and meet business requirements.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams including front-end developers and product managers to deliver seamless user experiences.
- Architect and deploy serverless applications on AWS, optimizing for cost and performance.
- Ensure data consistency and high availability through effective database schema design and management.
- Write clean, maintainable, and well-documented code following modern best practices and coding standards.
- Monitor system performance and troubleshoot issues to ensure uninterrupted service and rapid issue resolution.
- Participate actively in code reviews and contribute to team knowledge sharing sessions.
- Implement CI/CD pipelines to automate testing and deployment processes.
- Keep abreast of industry trends and emerging technologies to foster continual improvement and innovation.

## Skill Requirements
- A minimum of 5 years of experience in backend development, specifically with Node.js and Express.
- Strong proficiency in MongoDB and experience with database design and management.
- Familiarity with AWS services, particularly Lambda, DynamoDB, and API Gateway.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline, with relevant certifications being advantageous.
- Proven track record of creating and maintaining scalable APIs and backend systems in cloud environments.
- Solid grasp of software development methodologies, including Agile and DevOps principles.
- Exceptional analytical and problem-solving capabilities, with attention to detail.
- Ability to work collaboratively within a team and communicate effectively.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with Docker and container orchestration tools like Kubernetes.
- Knowledge of monitoring and logging tools (e.g., CloudWatch, ELK Stack).
- Familiarity with GraphQL and its integration with existing APIs.
- Contributions to internal tools or open-source projects are highly valued.
