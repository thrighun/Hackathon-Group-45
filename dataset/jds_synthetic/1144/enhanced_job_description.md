## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and execute comprehensive test plans and test cases based on software requirements and specifications.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure clear understanding of project goals and timelines.
- Perform both manual and automated testing, focusing on functional, regression, and performance aspects of applications.
- Identify, document, and track defects, ensuring timely resolution and retesting.
- Contribute to process improvements in the quality assurance lifecycle, advocating for best practices and tools.

## Skill Requirements
- Primary Skills: Manual Testing
- Secondary Skills: Automated Testing (Intermediate)
- Familiarity with industry-standard testing tools (e.g., Selenium, JIRA).
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting capabilities.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills to convey findings and suggestions effectively.

## Other Requirements
- Minimum of 3 years of experience in a QA role, preferably within a technology or software development environment.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Relevant certifications in QA or Testing (e.g., ISTQB) are highly desirable.
