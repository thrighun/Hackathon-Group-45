## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement advanced statistical models to forecast trends and improve operational efficiency.
- Conduct exploratory data analysis and identify key patterns and insights from large datasets.
- Collaborate with business units to define goals and identify data needs for predictive modeling initiatives.
- Utilize machine learning techniques for creating classification and regression models to support business objectives.
- Create data visualizations to communicate findings and recommendations effectively to stakeholders.
- Assess model performance and iterate on designs based on evaluation metrics.
- Build and maintain dashboards for real-time data monitoring and predictive insight delivery.
- Stay informed about the latest trends and methods in data science and predictive analytics.
- Offer mentorship and guidance to junior data team members.

- Strong proficiency in Python or R, with experience in statistical modeling and analysis libraries (e.g., NumPy, SciPy, R's caret).
- Expertise in machine learning algorithms, including both supervised and unsupervised learning techniques.
- Familiarity with data visualization tools such as Tableau, Power BI, or similar platforms.
- Proficiency in data wrangling and preprocessing techniques to prepare data for analysis.
- Experience with big data technologies like Apache Spark or similar frameworks is a plus.
- Strong understanding of cloud computing services (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure) for deploying models and analytics solutions.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, along with a detail-oriented mindset.
- Strong communication skills to convey complex data insights to non-technical stakeholders.
- Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related field.
