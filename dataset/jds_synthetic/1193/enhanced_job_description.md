## Job Title
Front-End Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Retail

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and optimize highly engaging mobile applications that enhance user interaction and satisfaction.
- Collaborate with UX/UI designers to translate design concepts into functional, high-quality user interfaces.
- Implement performance tuning to ensure fast loading times and smooth user experiences across various devices.
- Work closely with product managers to gather requirements and define project milestones and deliverables.
- Assist in the development of reusable components and libraries for future use to streamline the development process.
- Participate in code reviews and contribute to team knowledge through sharing best practices and new ideas.
- Stay informed about industry trends, user interface best practices, and new technologies to continuously innovate and improve application quality.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Web Development, or a related field.
- Minimum of 4 years of experience in front-end development, focusing on mobile applications.
- Proficiency in JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, and frameworks such as React Native or Flutter.
- Solid understanding of responsive design principles and mobile-first development approaches.
- Strong analytical skills and a keen eye for detail when it comes to user experience and interface design.

## Other Requirements
- Experience working in an Agile environment with cross-functional teams.
- Familiarity with version control systems like Git.
- Knowledge of testing frameworks and tools such as Jest or Mocha is a plus.
- A strong portfolio demonstrating previous work and successful mobile application projects.
