## Job Title
Data Scientist - Conversational AI Specialist

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Artificial Intelligence Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- **Conversational Model Development**: Design and develop sophisticated conversational models for chatbots and virtual assistants to improve user experience and engagement.
- **Intent Recognition and Response Generation**: Implement and optimize models for accurate intent recognition and natural response generation in multi-turn conversations.
- **Dialogue Management**: Build dialogue management systems that enable fluid interactions and context-aware conversations.
- **Integration with Existing Systems**: Collaborate with product and engineering teams to integrate conversational AI solutions into existing platforms and applications.
- **Performance Monitoring and Improvement**: Continuously monitor the performance of deployed models and implement enhancements based on user feedback and analytics.

## Skill Requirements
- **Technical Skills**: Proficiency in Python, experience with NLP libraries (e.g., NLTK, spaCy, transformers), and familiarity with ML frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- **Experience**: 4-6 years of relevant experience in AI and machine learning, specifically in developing conversational agents or chatbots.
- **Educational Background**: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, or a related field.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with cloud-based solutions for deploying AI applications (e.g., AWS Lambda, Google Dialogflow).
- Strong communication skills for effective collaboration with cross-functional teams and stakeholders.
