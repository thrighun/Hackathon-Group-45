## Job Title
UI/UX Web Designer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design engaging user interfaces and experiences for web applications using wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups.
- Collaborate closely with front-end developers to implement designs effectively, ensuring alignment between design and code.
- Conduct user research and usability testing to gather feedback and iterate on design solutions.
- Create design systems and style guides to maintain consistency across all digital products.
- Analyze user data and behavior to drive design improvements and enhance user satisfaction.
- Stay informed on design trends and best practices to innovate and evolve the user experience.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in design tools such as Adobe XD, Sketch, or Figma.
- Strong knowledge of HTML and CSS to communicate design intent to developers.
- Experience with user-centered design principles and methodologies.
- Familiarity with accessibility standards and best practices.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with a focus on teamwork.
- Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design, Interaction Design, or a related field.
- Minimum of 3 years of experience in UI/UX design.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with creating responsive and adaptive design frameworks.
- Understanding of basic front-end development processes and constraints.
- Ability to present and explain design concepts to stakeholders.
- Knowledge of Agile/Scrum methodologies and collaborative design processes.
