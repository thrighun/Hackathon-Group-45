## Job Title
Data Engineer

## Location
Hyderabad/Gurgaon/Noida/Bengaluru

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Monitor and optimize data pipelines to ensure the integrity, quality, and availability of data across various environments.
- Collaborate with product managers and data analysts to transform business requirements into robust technical solutions.
- Drive initiatives for enhancing data quality and accuracy through innovative techniques and best practices.
- Engage in Agile methodologies, participating in sprints and contributing to project management efforts.
- Address data-related inquiries and provide timely solutions to enhance operational efficiency.
- Communicate effectively with stakeholders to understand their needs and deliver appropriate data solutions.
- Maintain a proactive stance towards problem identification and resolution in data operations.
- Be open to work flexible hours to support critical data processes outside of regular business hours.
- Successfully pass a technical interview conducted by the client.

## Skill Requirements
- 3-7 years of experience in Python and SQL programming.
- Solid understanding of relational databases and data warehousing concepts.
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies and project management frameworks.
- Proven problem-solving capabilities and analytical thinking skills.
- Strong communication skills, both verbal and written, to effectively liaise with teams and stakeholders.
- Ability to work independently and manage multiple data requests efficiently.
- Willingness to adapt to evolving technologies and processes in data engineering.

## Other Requirements
- Previous experience in a fast-paced technology or data-driven environment is an advantage but not mandatory.
