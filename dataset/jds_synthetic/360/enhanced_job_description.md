## Detailed Responsibilities
- Create and implement quality assurance strategies and testing methodologies tailored to our software products.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into effective test cases.
- Conduct exploratory testing to identify potential software issues and improve functionality.
- Work with automation engineers to design automated test scripts and frameworks.
- Report and prioritize defects, ensuring they are resolved in a timely manner.
- Facilitate quality assurance meetings, working closely with project management and development teams.
- Stay updated with industry trends and testing technologies to enhance current practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
- Experience in software testing and quality assurance practices.
- Proficient in manual testing and familiar with automation tools (e.g., Selenium, TestNG).
- Understanding of software development life cycle (SDLC) and agile methodologies.
- Strong attention to detail and exceptional analytical skills.
- Effective communication and interpersonal skills for cross-team collaboration.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in developing test automation frameworks.
- Familiarity with CI/CD processes.
- Relevant certifications in Software Testing (e.g., ISTQB, CSTE).
