## Job Title
Embedded Software Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain embedded software solutions for Internet of Things (IoT) devices.
- Work closely with hardware engineering teams to create and validate interfaces that ensure optimal integration.
- Write, test, and optimize code primarily using C and assembly languages tailored for performance in resource-limited settings.
- Conduct thorough validation and verification processes to ensure that all embedded systems comply with industry standards and specifications.
- Document software requirements and design specifications, and contribute to user manuals to aid user understanding.
- Provide technical support and troubleshooting services for deployed systems in various operational environments.
- Remain current on advancements and trends in the fields of embedded systems and IoT technology to inform development practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in embedded software development, particularly for IoT applications.
- Proficient in programming languages such as C and assembly, with a deep understanding of system-level programming.
- Strong knowledge of embedded system architectures and communication protocols (e.g., SPI, I2C, UART).
- Experience with debugging tools and methodologies specific to embedded environments.
- Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills, with a focus on detail.
- Strong communication and collaboration skills, able to work effectively within a team.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with additional programming languages such as Python or Java for scripting purposes.
- Experience in agile development practices and project management tools.
- Understanding of embedded hardware design and testing methodologies.
- Previous experience in the technology provider sector or IoT industry.
