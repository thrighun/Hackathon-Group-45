## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and maintain automated test scripts for API and mobile applications to ensure functionality, performance, and security.
- Collaborate with development teams to define test strategies and improve application performance through load and stress testing practices.
- Utilize various test automation frameworks and tools to enhance testing efficiency and maintainability.
- Analyze automation results and provide detailed feedback and reporting to stakeholders, facilitating informed decision-making.
- Participate in code reviews and contribute to improving the overall quality of the codebase through automated testing practices.
- Contribute to the ongoing refinement of automation testing processes and tools, fostering a culture of continuous improvement.
- Work closely with teams in an Agile/Scrum environment to meet project deadlines and ensure thorough coverage of test scenarios.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 3 years of experience in test automation, with a focus on API and mobile testing.
- Proficient in scripting languages such as Python, Java, or JavaScript used for automation frameworks.
- Strong understanding of performance testing tools and methodologies.
- Experience with CI/CD tools and processes, aiding in the seamless integration of testing within the development lifecycle.
- Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail, with a proactive approach to identifying and addressing issues.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with cloud-based testing environments and services.
- Experience working with version control systems, such as Git.
- ISTQB or equivalent testing certification is highly regarded.
- Knowledge of Agile methodologies with hands-on experience is a plus.
