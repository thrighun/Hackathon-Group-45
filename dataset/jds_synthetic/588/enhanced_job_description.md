## Job Title
Data Scientist

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology & Services

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze large and complex data sets to derive actionable insights and support decision-making processes.
- Build, test, and implement predictive models to forecast trends and enhance business performance.
- Create compelling data visualizations and dashboards to communicate findings to non-technical stakeholders.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams including product management and engineering to integrate data-driven features into software solutions.
- Conduct statistical analyses and experiments to evaluate model effectiveness and improve algorithm performance.
- Maintain and optimize database queries and data pipelines for efficiency in data retrieval and processing.
- Keep abreast of advancements in data science methodologies and big data technologies to innovate and improve current practices.
- Document analytical processes, model development, and results for reporting and knowledge sharing.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related field.
- Solid experience in statistical modeling, machine learning techniques, and data mining.
- Proficient in programming languages such as R, Python, or SQL, with familiarity in data visualization tools like Tableau or Power BI.
- Experience with big data technologies such as Hadoop, Spark, or Kafka for managing and processing large datasets.
- Strong understanding of statistical analysis, data wrangling, and data interpretation.
- Excellent communication skills for effectively collaborating with teams and presenting findings.
- Detail-oriented with strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with cloud computing platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP) for data storage and analysis.
- Knowledge of MLOps principles for ensuring model reliability and performance monitoring.
- Experience in working with unstructured data sources including text, images, or social media analytics.
- Contributions to data science competitions or open-source projects in the analytics domain.
