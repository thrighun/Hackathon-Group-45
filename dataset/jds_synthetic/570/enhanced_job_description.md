## Job Title
Cloud Infrastructure Engineer (Terraform, AWS, Docker)

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology & Services

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and manage scalable cloud infrastructure solutions using Terraform and AWS services.
- Collaborate with development teams to automate deployment processes and enhance system reliability.
- Configure and maintain container orchestration solutions with Docker and Kubernetes.
- Monitor and optimize cloud resources for performance, cost-efficiency, and security.
- Develop Infrastructure as Code (IaC) scripts to streamline infrastructure provisioning and management.
- Ensure compliance with security best practices and policies in the cloud environment.
- Troubleshoot and resolve infrastructure-related issues to ensure high availability.
- Participate in capacity planning and disaster recovery strategy formulation.
- Stay updated on industry trends and emerging technologies to continuously improve cloud infrastructure practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum 5 years of experience in cloud infrastructure engineering.
- Proficient in Terraform and AWS services (EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda, etc.).
- Strong experience with Docker and container orchestration tools like Kubernetes.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field. Relevant certifications are a plus.
- Solid understanding of networking concepts and cloud security practices.
- Excellent analytical skills and a proactive approach to problem-solving.
- Strong communication and collaboration abilities.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with CI/CD tools and pipeline automation.
- Experience with monitoring tools (CloudWatch, Prometheus, etc.) and logging solutions.
- Knowledge of scripting languages (Python, Bash) for automation tasks.
- Contributions to open-source projects or involvement in cloud communities.
