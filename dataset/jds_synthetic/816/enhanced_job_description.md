## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement AI-driven applications specifically for edge computing environments, focusing on low-latency processing and real-time analytics.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify and prioritize AI use cases, ensuring alignment with business objectives and technical feasibility.
- Develop, test, and maintain APIs to facilitate seamless integration of AI algorithms into edge devices and applications.
- Create and manage data ingestion pipelines to ensure efficient processing and analysis of data collected at the edge.
- Monitor and optimize the performance of edge AI solutions, applying best practices for reliability and scalability.
- Address security, compliance, and data governance considerations in edge deployments, ensuring adherence to industry standards.
- Participate in technical strategy sessions, contributing insights on emerging technologies and methodologies in edge AI.
- Provide technical guidance and mentorship to junior engineers and developers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related technical field.
- 7+ years of experience in software engineering and architecture with a strong emphasis on edge computing solutions.
- Proficient in developing AI/ML algorithms and models tailored for edge environments, including experience with frameworks such as TensorFlow Lite or ONNX.
- Strong programming skills in modern languages (Python, Java, C++) with a solid understanding of API design and development.
- Familiarity with IoT protocols and edge device management, including MQTT, CoAP, and device connectivity standards.
- Experience in deploying applications using containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes) and orchestration frameworks.
- Knowledge of security best practices concerning edge computing and AI solutions, including encryption, access control, and compliance.

## Other Requirements
- Relevant certifications in AI/ML, edge computing, or cloud technologies (AWS IoT, Azure IoT Developer, etc.).
- Active engagement in AI or edge computing communities, including participation in open-source projects.
- Experience working with hybrid cloud architectures and integrating with cloud-based AI services.
- Familiarity with regulatory frameworks pertaining to data privacy and security (GDPR, HIPAA).
- Strong aptitude for problem-solving and analytical thinking, with effective communication skills.
