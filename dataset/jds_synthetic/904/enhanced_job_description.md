## Detailed Responsibilities
- Define and manage the Product Backlog for ITOM and Cloud Management within ServiceNow.
- Collaborate with business stakeholders to translate requirements into user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Prioritize features and functionality based on business needs and impact.
- Facilitate Agile ceremonies including Sprint Planning, Backlog Refinement, and Sprint Review.
- Analyze and leverage data insights to inform product development and enhance user experience.
- Serve as the point of contact for product-related queries, providing transparency and updates to stakeholders.
- Work with development teams to ensure timely delivery and quality of features.
- Conduct user training sessions and create documentation to support users.
- Monitor industry trends to identify opportunities for new features and enhancements.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Business Administration, or related field.
- 8+ years of experience in product management or a similar role within IT service management.
- Strong understanding of ServiceNow platform capabilities, particularly in ITOM and Cloud Management.
- Experience working in an Agile environment with familiarity in Scrum methodologies.
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.
- Analytical mindset with the ability to interpret data and leverage insights.
- ServiceNow certifications in ITOM or Cloud Management are a plus.
- Proficiency in creating user stories, acceptance criteria, and maintaining a product backlog.
- Strong problem-solving abilities and attention to detail.

## Other Requirements
- Previous experience working in a multinational technology environment.
- Familiarity with ITIL frameworks and practices.
- Experience with user training and support documentation development.
