- Assess client requirements and translate them into actionable ServiceNow solutions for ITSM and CSM.
- Guide clients through the implementation process, facilitating workshops and training sessions as needed.
- Design and configure ServiceNow modules specific to customer service operations and IT service management.
- Execute data migrations and system integrations to ensure a seamless transition to the ServiceNow platform.
- Offer ongoing support and training to clients post-implementation, ensuring smooth operations.
- Identify opportunities for process improvements and propose innovative solutions to enhance client workflows.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather feedback and iterate on service offerings.
- Maintain documentation for system configurations and customizations.
- Stay updated on ServiceNow releases, features, and industry trends to provide informed recommendations.
- Foster a culture of collaboration and continuous improvement within the project team.

- Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Business Administration, or a related field.
- 8+ years of experience in ServiceNow implementations focusing on ITSM and CSM.
- Strong knowledge of ServiceNow modules relevant to IT service management and customer service.
- Proficiency in workflow design, configuration, and customization of ServiceNow applications.
- Excellent communication skills with the ability to convey complex information effectively to stakeholders.
- Experience with data migration and system integrations, particularly in a cloud-based environment.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a focus on customer needs and satisfaction.
- ServiceNow Certified Implementation Specialist (CIS) certifications preferred.
