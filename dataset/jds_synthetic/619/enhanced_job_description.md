## Job Title
Senior Software Engineer - Full Stack Developer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop scalable web applications using a variety of programming languages and frameworks.
- Lead projects from concept to production, working closely with stakeholders to understand requirements and deliver solutions.
- Architect and implement microservices-based applications to enhance system modularization and scalability.
- Build robust APIs and integrate with third-party services to enhance application functionality.
- Write high-quality, efficient, and reusable code, adhering to industry standards and best practices.
- Mentor junior engineers and foster a collaborative team environment.
- Conduct rigorous testing, debugging, and troubleshooting to maintain application integrity.
- Stay ahead of industry trends and emerging technologies, providing insights for continuous improvement and innovation.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 6 years of experience in software development, with a focus on full stack development.
- Strong proficiency in back-end technologies (Java, Node.js, etc.) and front-end frameworks (React, Angular, etc.).
- Expertise in microservices architecture and containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Experience in developing and consuming RESTful APIs and web services.
- Solid understanding of database technologies (SQL, NoSQL) and data modeling.
- Excellent debugging, problem-solving, and analytical skills.
- Strong leadership and interpersonal communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with version control systems, particularly Git.
- Experience with cloud computing platforms (AWS, Google Cloud).
- Understanding of DevOps principles and CI/CD processes.
- Certifications in full stack development or relevant technologies are a plus.
