## Job Title
AI Solutions Architect – Cloud & Machine Learning Integration

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the architectural design and deployment of AI solutions on cloud platforms, ensuring optimal performance and scalability.
- Develop and implement machine learning models and algorithms that integrate seamlessly with existing data infrastructures.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams including data engineers, business analysts, and project managers to understand business requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
- Design data pipelines for efficient data collection, storage, and processing, enabling real-time analytics and machine learning workflows.
- Conduct technical assessments of new AI technologies and frameworks to continuously enhance solutions and architecture.
- Provide technical leadership and mentorship to junior team members, fostering a culture of innovation and continuous improvement.
- Prepare and deliver presentations and demos of AI solutions to stakeholders, communicating technical concepts in a clear and engaging manner.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field.
- Strong experience in cloud services (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) with a focus on deploying machine learning solutions.
- Proficient in programming languages such as Python or R and experience with ML frameworks like TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Solid understanding of data architecture, data warehousing, and ETL processes.
- Knowledge of containerization and orchestration technologies, including Docker and Kubernetes.
- Experience with Agile methodologies and project management tools.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with MLOps practices and tools for deploying machine learning models.
- Experience in developing RESTful APIs for machine learning services is a plus.
- Prior experience in a technical leadership role.
- Contributions to AI research or participation in relevant industry forums and communities.
