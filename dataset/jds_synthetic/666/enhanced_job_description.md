## Job Title
Senior Front-End Engineer (Vue.js, eCommerce & Digital Content Solutions)

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
E-commerce and Digital Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain robust web applications using Vue.js, focusing on delivering top-notch eCommerce experiences.
- Collaborate with UX/UI designers to create interactive and visually stunning user interfaces with responsive designs using SCSS.
- Integrate various eCommerce platforms and digital asset management systems to streamline operational workflows.
- Work alongside product managers to gather requirements and prioritize features that drive customer engagement and satisfaction.
- Perform continuous performance tuning and implement security best practices to safeguard web applications.
- Mentor junior developers and share knowledge on front-end development standards and best practices.
- Conduct thorough testing and debugging of applications to ensure reliability and functionality across multiple devices and browsers.
- Stay informed on current trends and innovations within the eCommerce and front-end development landscape.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
- Extensive experience in developing applications using Vue.js with a solid understanding of component-based architecture.
- Proficiency in modern CSS methodologies, particularly SCSS, and responsive design principles.
- Experience in integrating eCommerce platforms like Shopify, Magento, or similar solutions.
- Knowledge of digital content management systems and their integration into web applications.
- Familiarity with RESTful APIs and asynchronous programming (e.g., Promises, async/await).
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with meticulous attention to detail.
- Excellent communication skills, capable of articulating complex ideas clearly.

## Other Requirements
- Proficiency in TypeScript and modern JavaScript (ES6+).
- Experience with cloud services, such as AWS or Azure, for hosting and deployment.
- Familiarity with Agile/Scrum development methodologies and their application in project management.
- Previous experience working on high-traffic eCommerce websites and implementing SEO best practices for optimization.
