## Detailed Responsibilities
- Build and maintain dynamic web applications using Vue.js and Nuxt.js frameworks.
- Collaborate with UX/UI designers to implement responsive and user-friendly interfaces.
- Optimize applications for maximum performance and efficiency, ensuring fast load times.
- Work closely with backend development teams to integrate RESTful APIs and manage data flow.
- Conduct code reviews to maintain high standards of code quality and reliability.
- Participate in Agile development processes, contributing to sprint planning and retrospectives.
- Mentor junior developers and provide technical support as needed.

## Skill Requirements
- 2-3 years of hands-on experience with Vue.js and Nuxt.js.
- Strong proficiency in JavaScript and experience with ES6+ features.
- Familiarity with state management libraries such as Vuex.
- Understanding of web performance optimization techniques.
- Experience with front-end testing frameworks like Jest or Mocha.
- Knowledge of version control systems, particularly Git.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, along with attention to detail.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with TypeScript is a plus.
- Familiarity with CSS preprocessors like SASS or LESS.
- Understanding of Progressive Web Applications (PWAs) and their benefits.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively in a team environment.
