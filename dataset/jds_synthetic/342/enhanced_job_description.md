## Job Title
Machine Learning Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and implement machine learning models to solve complex business problems and enhance product functionality.
- Collaborate with data engineering teams to collect, preprocess, and manage large datasets for model training and evaluation.
- Deploy machine learning solutions on cloud platforms, utilizing MLOps tools for streamlined workflow and monitoring.
- Conduct experiments to optimize model performance, including hyperparameter tuning and feature engineering.
- Work closely with software developers to integrate machine learning components into existing applications and systems.
- Document processes and methodologies for reproducibility and knowledge sharing within the team.
- Stay updated with the latest trends and advancements in machine learning and AI technologies to innovate and improve existing solutions.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field.
- 4 to 6 years of professional experience in machine learning model development and deployment.
- Proficiency in Python and experience with libraries such as Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch.
- Strong understanding of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP) and MLOps tools (e.g., MLflow, Kubeflow).
- Familiarity with data preprocessing, feature selection, and model evaluation techniques.
- Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with version control systems (e.g., Git) and agile development methodologies.
- Knowledge of containerization (Docker) and orchestration tools (Kubernetes).
- Proven track record of working in a collaborative team environment, particularly in large-scale projects.
