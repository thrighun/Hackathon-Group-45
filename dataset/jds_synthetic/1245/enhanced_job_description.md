## Job Title
AI Solutions Architect (Python & Intelligent Automation)

## Location
Remote / Hybrid

## Client Industry
Information Technology Services

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect and develop intelligent automation solutions utilizing Python and machine learning algorithms.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to analyze business operations and identify automation opportunities that enhance efficiency.
- Design scalable architectures for deploying machine learning models within enterprise environments.
- Guide cross-functional teams in implementing AI-driven enhancements to existing systems and processes.
- Evaluate and select appropriate AI tools, libraries, and technologies to support project requirements.
- Monitor and optimize machine learning model performance through continuous data-driven iterations.
- Create and maintain comprehensive documentation for systems architecture and implementation processes.
- Lead workshops and training sessions to educate teams on AI solutions and best practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or related discipline.
- Demonstrated experience in Python programming, particularly in developing machine learning applications.
- Familiarity with automation frameworks and tools such as UiPath, Automation Anywhere, or similar.
- Strong knowledge of machine learning libraries and frameworks (e.g., Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Keras).
- In-depth understanding of system integration and deployment of AI applications in real-world scenarios.
- Excellent analytical and troubleshooting skills with a talent for innovative problem-solving.
- Ability to communicate technical ideas clearly to diverse audiences.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with cloud-based AI services for automation (AWS Lambda, Azure Functions, or Google Cloud Functions).
- Knowledge of Agile methodologies and experience in collaborative development environments.
- Engagement in community-driven AI projects or participation in hackathons is a plus.
- Understanding of compliance and regulatory considerations relevant to automated systems.
