## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and optimize firmware for smart devices to enhance functionality and user experience.
- Collaborate closely with hardware engineers to ensure effective integration of firmware with electronic components.
- Identify, diagnose, and resolve firmware-related issues to improve product performance and reliability.
- Create and maintain comprehensive technical documentation regarding firmware architecture, APIs, and testing protocols.
- Research and evaluate new tools and technologies to streamline firmware development and boost productivity.
- Mentor junior developers, providing guidance and support in firmware development best practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Electronics, or a similar field.
- Proficient in C/C++ and Python programming languages for firmware development.
- Experience in developing firmware for microcontrollers and real-time systems.
- Familiarity with debugging tools and methodologies for embedded systems.
- Understanding of communication protocols such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and MQTT.
- Strong analytical skills and attention to detail in troubleshooting complex firmware issues.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with low-level programming and device drivers for embedded platforms.
- Knowledge of Agile development processes and version control systems.
- Understanding of IoT protocols and security measures in firmware design.
- Familiarity with Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) practices for firmware releases.
