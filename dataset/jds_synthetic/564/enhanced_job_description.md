## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the development of dynamic and intuitive web applications using Angular, focusing on user experience and performance.
- Collaborate effectively with designers and back-end developers to ensure seamless integration of user-facing elements with server-side logic.
- Convert user stories and requirements into practical, high-quality code that follows industry standards and best practices.
- Conduct regular code reviews and provide mentorship to junior team members, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement.
- Optimize web applications for maximum speed and efficiency, ensuring a responsive design across various devices and browsers.
- Identify and troubleshoot technical issues, working to enhance application performance and reliability.
- Maintain updated documentation for code, specifications, and workflows to ensure clarity and continuity in project development.
- Engage with stakeholders to gather feedback and iterate on features, enhancing overall product functionality and user satisfaction.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related domain (or equivalent practical experience).
- At least 3 years of experience in building web applications using Angular and related technologies.
- Strong knowledge of TypeScript, HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript frameworks.
- Familiarity with state management solutions like NgRx or equivalent.
- Experience with RESTful APIs, web services, and asynchronous programming practices.
- Understanding of accessibility standards and performance optimization techniques.
- Proficient in using version control systems, particularly Git, for collaborative development.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a keen eye for detail and a commitment to quality assurance.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with testing frameworks such as Jasmine or Protractor, and familiarity with unit testing practices.
- Exposure to cloud computing environments (e.g., Google Cloud Platform, AWS) and CI/CD methodologies.
- Familiarity with Agile development practices, including participation in Scrum ceremonies.
- Contributions to community projects or a demonstrated portfolio showcasing relevant work.
