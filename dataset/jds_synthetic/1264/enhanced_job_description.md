## Job Title
Full Stack JavaScript Developer with Node.js and Vue.js

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop scalable web applications using Node.js for server-side and Vue.js for client-side development.
- Collaborate with product managers and UI/UX designers to create user-friendly interfaces.
- Implement robust APIs and back-end services for data handling and storage.
- Ensure application responsiveness and cross-platform compatibility.
- Maintain version control and documentation for all codebases.
- Participate in agile development processes, including sprint planning and reviews.
- Monitor application performance and optimize components for maximum efficiency.

## Skill Requirements
- A minimum of 4 years of experience in full-stack JavaScript development.
- Strong proficiency in Node.js and Vue.js frameworks.
- In-depth knowledge of JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3.
- Experience with NoSQL databases (e.g., MongoDB) and SQL databases (e.g., PostgreSQL).
- Understanding of asynchronous programming and event-driven architecture.
- Familiarity with GraphQL and RESTful services.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with DevOps practices and deployment pipelines.
- Knowledge of unit testing frameworks and test-driven development.
- Strong analytical and debugging skills.
- Excellent interpersonal and communication skills, both written and verbal.
