## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement embedded software solutions for IoT devices using C and Python.
- Work with product managers to gather requirements and translate them into technical specifications.
- Design robust firmware architectures and conduct integration with hardware components.
- Identify and resolve software defects through debugging and performance optimization.
- Ensure that software components meet security and reliability standards in IoT environments.
- Provide technical guidance to junior engineers and participate in mentorship programs.
- Collaborate with QA teams to execute comprehensive testing strategies for embedded applications.
- Monitor industry trends and contribute innovative ideas to enhance product offerings.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- 4-6 years of experience in developing software for embedded systems, particularly in IoT applications.
- Strong programming skills in C and familiarity with Python for rapid prototyping.
- In-depth knowledge of communication protocols (e.g., MQTT, CoAP, HTTP).
- Experience in software testing methodologies and quality assurance processes.
- Strong analytical thinking and a commitment to producing high-quality software.
- Ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced, agile environment.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with cloud platforms and services for IoT, such as AWS IoT or Azure IoT.
- Understanding of low-power design considerations and energy-efficient coding practices.
- Familiarity with continuous integration and deployment tools (CI/CD).
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with a focus on technical documentation.
