## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and maintain interactive web applications utilizing Vue.js and Nuxt.js.
- Collaborate with product managers and designers to translate user needs into functional interfaces.
- Implement responsive design principles to ensure compatibility across various devices and platforms.
- Conduct thorough testing and debugging to enhance application reliability and user satisfaction.
- Participate actively in agile ceremonies and contribute to the continuous improvement of development processes.
- Mentor junior developers by sharing best practices and providing guidance on complex technical challenges.
- Keep abreast of emerging technologies and industry trends to drive innovation within the team.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related discipline.
- 3-6 years of experience in frontend development with a focus on Vue.js and Nuxt.js.
- Strong proficiency in JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, and modern frontend tooling.
- Familiarity with state management libraries like Vuex and experience with RESTful services.
- Knowledge of testing frameworks and methodologies (e.g., Jest, Cypress).
- Excellent analytical and troubleshooting skills with a keen attention to detail.
- Effective communication skills and a collaborative mindset.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with server-side rendering using Nuxt.js.
- Understanding of performance optimization techniques for web applications.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and tools like Jenkins or GitHub Actions.
- Basic understanding of SEO strategies and how they apply to web development.
