## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop user-centered design solutions by conducting thorough user research and leveraging CAD skills to enhance product interfaces.
- Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, including engineering and product management, to ensure design feasibility and alignment with project goals.
- Create low-fidelity and high-fidelity prototypes using CAD tools and implement usability testing methodologies to validate design concepts.
- Analyze user behavior and feedback, translating insights into actionable design enhancements that optimize user engagement.
- Document design processes and user research findings to support transparency and informed decision-making across teams.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in user experience design principles with hands-on experience in conducting usability testing and user research.
- Advanced CAD software skills to create technical schematics and design elements that enhance product usability.
- Solid knowledge of HTML/CSS for the development of interactive web pages and elements.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a focus on user-centered design principles.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills, enabling effective collaboration with a variety of stakeholders.
- A portfolio showcasing previous design projects and user-centered solutions.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with design software such as Adobe Creative Suite, Sketch, Figma, or equivalent.
- Knowledge of responsive design methodologies and accessibility standards.
- Background in working within agile environments or cross-disciplinary teams.
- Familiarity with front-end development practices is preferred.
