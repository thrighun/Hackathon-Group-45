## Job Title
Senior Front-End Engineer (Vue.js)

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology / Technology Solutions

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the design and development of dynamic web applications utilizing Vue.js, ensuring seamless user interactions and interface responsiveness.
- Collaborate closely with product managers, back-end engineers, and designers to define technical requirements and deliver high-quality web solutions.
- Implement reusable components and libraries for future use while maintaining a high standard of code quality.
- Optimize applications for maximum speed and scalability, ensuring compatibility across various devices and browsers.
- Perform code reviews and provide mentorship to junior developers, fostering a culture of continuous improvement and learning.
- Identify and debug issues, implementing solutions to enhance application performance and user experience.
- Keep abreast of the latest trends in front-end technologies and propose innovative enhancements to improve product offerings.
- Document all technical procedures, code changes, and system integrations for team reference.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience.
- Minimum of 4 years of experience in front-end development with a focus on Vue.js.
- Strong knowledge of JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, CSS3, and version control systems, particularly Git.
- Experience with state management tools like Vuex and familiarity with RESTful APIs.
- Proficient in performance optimization techniques and responsive web design principles.
- Solid understanding of testing frameworks such as Mocha, Chai, or Vue Test Utils.
- Exceptional troubleshooting skills and a strong attention to detail.
- Excellent communication and teamwork abilities, with a strong emphasis on collaboration in an Agile/Scrum environment.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with TypeScript and server-side rendering concepts in Vue.js.
- Experience with cloud services (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud) and Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) practices.
- Contributions to open-source projects or a portfolio showcasing previous projects and applications you have developed.
