## Detailed Responsibilities
- Manage day-to-day operations of the data center, ensuring optimal performance and uptime.
- Develop strategies for efficient power and cooling management to enhance energy efficiency.
- Oversee the installation, maintenance, and decommissioning of hardware systems.
- Implement and monitor security protocols to safeguard data center assets.
- Collaborate with IT teams to address infrastructure needs and scalability plans.
- Conduct regular audits and assessments of data center performance and compliance with industry standards.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in data center operations or facilities management.
- Strong understanding of data center infrastructure, including power distribution and cooling systems.
- Excellent leadership and project management skills.
- Effective communication skills to liaise with cross-functional teams and external vendors.
- Relevant certifications (e.g., Data Center Certified Associate (DCCA), Certified Data Center Management Professional (CDCMP)) are preferred.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with disaster recovery and business continuity planning.
- Experience with network infrastructure management and monitoring tools.
- Knowledge of regulatory compliance standards relevant to data centers.
