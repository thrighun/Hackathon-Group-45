## Job Title
Senior Software Engineer - Firmware Development for IoT Devices

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Internet of Things (IoT)

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and optimize firmware for IoT devices utilizing Python and C programming languages.
- Collaborate with hardware engineers to integrate software with physical components for enhanced functionality.
- Participate in agile development processes and work closely with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and deliver precise solutions.
- Implement security measures within firmware to protect devices from vulnerabilities.
- Conduct performance testing and troubleshooting on IoT firmware to ensure reliability and efficiency.
- Mentor junior engineers and provide peer code reviews to uphold quality standards.
- Keep abreast of emerging technologies and best practices in IoT and firmware development.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a relevant area.
- 5-7 years of experience in embedded firmware development, particularly for IoT devices.
- Proficient in C and Python programming languages.
- Solid understanding of wireless communication protocols (e.g., MQTT, CoAP).
- Experience with embedded systems testing and debugging tools.
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting abilities.
- Excellent collaboration and communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with cloud platforms and IoT ecosystems (e.g., AWS IoT, Azure IoT).
- Experience with Git or other version control systems.
- Knowledge of agile methodologies and project management tools.
- Certifications related to IoT or embedded systems are a plus.
