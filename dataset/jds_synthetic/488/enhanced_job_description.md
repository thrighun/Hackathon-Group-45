## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and execute comprehensive test plans and test cases for mobile applications on iOS and Android platforms, ensuring that all functional and non-functional requirements are met.
- Collaborate closely with product managers and developers to understand application features and establish effective testing strategies that align with project timelines.
- Conduct both manual and automated testing, utilizing tools such as XCTest for iOS and Espresso for Android to ensure application robustness and user satisfaction.
- Identify, document, and rigorously track defects using established bug tracking systems, while working with developers to ensure timely resolution of issues.
- Perform exploratory testing to uncover edge cases and enhance the application's overall user experience, providing constructive feedback for enhancements.
- Analyze and communicate test results effectively, reporting on application performance, usability, and reliability to stakeholders and making recommendations for improvements.
- Continuously research and implement new mobile testing tools and best practices, adapting testing processes to improve efficiency and effectiveness.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong experience in mobile application testing for iOS and Android platforms, with an emphasis on automation testing frameworks such as Appium or Selenium.
- Proficiency in application performance testing and familiarity with tools such as JMeter or LoadRunner.
- Understanding of CI/CD processes and tools to support automated testing pipelines.
- Proven ability to work with RESTful APIs and understand backend services integration for comprehensive testing.
- Excellent problem-solving skills and meticulous attention to detail when identifying and diagnosing software issues.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills to facilitate effective teamwork and stakeholder updates.
- Bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field, or equivalent hands-on experience in mobile application testing.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in Agile/Scrum development methodologies and participation in sprint planning and retrospectives.
- Familiarity with version control systems like Git and collaboration tools such as Jira or Trello.
- Knowledge of additional programming languages or frameworks related to mobile development and automation testing is a bonus.
