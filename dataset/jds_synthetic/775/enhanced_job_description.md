## Job Title
Senior Frontend Engineer

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Marketing Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the development of innovative web applications utilizing modern JavaScript frameworks with a focus on user engagement.
- Collaborate closely with marketing strategists, UX/UI designers, and backend teams to create interactive and visually appealing solutions.
- Design and implement scalable components and libraries for future use across multiple projects.
- Evaluate and improve application performance, optimizing for various devices and browsers.
- Contribute to the design and architecture of new features and enhancements based on business goals.
- Provide mentorship and guidance to junior developers through code reviews and knowledge sharing sessions.
- Stay abreast of emerging frontend technologies and best practices to continuously enhance the team’s development capabilities.
- Document application processes and architecture for knowledge sharing and onboarding new team members.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in frontend development with a focus on JavaScript frameworks.
- Proficient in frameworks such as Vue.js or Angular, in addition to React.
- Strong understanding of web standards and best practices in responsive design.
- Experience with state management libraries like Vuex or NgRx.
- Familiarity with RESTful services and integration with third-party APIs.
- Understanding of automated testing frameworks and experience in writing unit tests for frontend applications.
- Familiarity with modern CSS preprocessors like SASS or LESS.
- A degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with performance optimization techniques and tools.
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies and project management tools (e.g., JIRA, Trello).
- Strong analytical skills with a problem-solving mindset.
- Excellent collaboration and communication abilities with cross-functional teams.
