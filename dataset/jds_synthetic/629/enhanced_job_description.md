- Develop and maintain complex web applications, ensuring they are responsive, efficient, and user-friendly.
- Collaborate with UX/UI designers to translate wireframes and prototypes into functional web applications.
- Design and implement RESTful APIs to facilitate communication between front-end and back-end components.
- Manage databases, optimize queries, and ensure data integrity and security.
- Participate actively in code reviews, providing constructive feedback to team members to enhance overall code quality.
- Work with Agile/Scrum methodologies to ensure timely project deliverables and promote team collaboration.
- Identify, troubleshoot, and resolve application issues, ensuring a seamless user experience.
- Keep abreast of industry trends and best practices in web development to drive innovation.

- Familiarity with Agile/Scrum methodologies and the software development lifecycle.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail.
