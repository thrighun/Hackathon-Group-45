## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and maintain RESTful APIs and microservices utilizing Python and cloud technologies.
- Collaborate with product management, UX/UI, and DevOps teams to develop cohesive applications and deliver features on schedule.
- Conduct code reviews and provide constructive feedback to junior engineers to foster professional growth and ensure best practices.
- Optimize application performance and scalability by analyzing user needs and system performance metrics.
- Participate in Agile ceremonies and contribute to sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives to continuously enhance team collaboration and output.
- Provide troubleshooting support and resolve production issues, ensuring minimal downtime and optimal performance.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in Python programming and related frameworks (e.g., Flask, Django).
- Strong experience in cloud computing platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and their respective services.
- Hands-on experience in developing and integrating RESTful APIs and microservices.
- Familiarity with PostgreSQL or other relational databases.
- Understanding of software development lifecycle methodologies such as Agile and DevOps practices.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline.
- A minimum of 5 years of software development experience, with at least 2 years in a cloud computing environment.
- Knowledge of containerization technologies (e.g., Docker) and orchestration (e.g., Kubernetes) is a plus.
- Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to work both independently and collaboratively in a team setting.
- Strong communication skills to effectively interact with stakeholders across various levels.
