## Job Title
Senior Software Engineer - Backend Systems

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology / Advanced Software Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the design and implementation of high-performing backend systems using Node.js and Express.js frameworks.
- Drive the development of secure and scalable APIs and microservices in collaboration with product, design, and frontend teams.
- Optimize and manage database systems, primarily using MongoDB, focusing on performance and data availability.
- Utilize Amazon Web Services (AWS) for deployment, scaling, and maintaining backend architectures while applying best practices for cloud operations.
- Spearhead initiatives related to AI integration within applications to enhance user experiences and operational efficiency.
- Ensure code quality through rigorous testing and code reviews, adhering to software engineering standards and best practices.
- Mentor junior developers and participate in team-building activities to foster a collaborative engineering environment.
- Stay abreast of new technologies and industry trends, proposing innovative solutions and enhancements to existing systems.
- Collaborate closely with DevOps teams to establish streamlined CI/CD pipelines and improve operational workflows.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Extensive experience (5+ years) in backend development with Node.js and Express.js frameworks.
- Strong expertise in NoSQL databases, particularly MongoDB, and data modeling techniques.
- Proven experience deploying and managing applications on AWS with familiarity in serverless architectures.
- Knowledge of AI technologies and frameworks, coupled with experience in developing AI-driven applications.
- In-depth understanding of API design, microservices, and agile development methodologies.
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, with a strong emphasis on teamwork.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with container orchestration tools (Docker, Kubernetes) is desirable.
- Familiarity with monitoring and logging tools (e.g., ELK stack, Grafana) is a plus.
- Background in working with other cloud platforms (Azure, GCP) or hybrid cloud solutions.
- Strong leadership skills with the ability to influence and inspire a technical team.
