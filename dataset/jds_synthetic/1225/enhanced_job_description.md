## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement comprehensive security strategies for AWS cloud infrastructure.
- Collaborate with development and operations teams to ensure security is integrated into all stages of software development.
- Conduct regular security assessments and audits to identify vulnerabilities and compliance gaps.
- Lead incident response efforts and perform forensic analysis to determine the root cause of security breaches.
- Provide security training and awareness programs for technical and non-technical staff.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum 10 years of experience in IT security, with a focus on cloud security.
- Strong knowledge of AWS security services and best practices.
- Experience with automation and scripting for security operations.
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Cybersecurity, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Relevant security certifications (e.g., CISSP, AWS Certified Security Specialty) are required.

## Other Requirements
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Strong communication skills for collaborating with cross-functional teams.
- Familiarity with compliance standards (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA) and risk management frameworks is a plus.
