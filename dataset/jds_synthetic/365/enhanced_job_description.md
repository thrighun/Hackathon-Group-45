## Job Title
Senior Front-End Engineer (Vue.js)

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect and develop scalable web applications using Vue.js, ensuring a responsive and engaging user experience.
- Collaborate with UX/UI designers to create aesthetically pleasing and intuitive interfaces.
- Implement state management solutions like Vuex to handle complex application states efficiently.
- Work closely with back-end developers to integrate RESTful APIs and improve overall application performance.
- Conduct thorough code reviews and provide mentorship to junior developers to promote best practices.
- Debug and resolve performance bottlenecks and critical issues to enhance application stability.
- Research and integrate new technologies and tools to optimize development workflows.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field.
- Minimum of 7 years of experience in front-end development with a strong focus on Vue.js.
- Proficient in JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3 with a solid understanding of responsive design principles.
- Experience with Vue.js ecosystem tools like Vue Router and Vue CLI.
- Familiar with testing frameworks such as Mocha or Cypress for unit and end-to-end testing.
- Strong understanding of version control systems, particularly Git.
- Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to work in a fast-paced environment.
- Effective communication and collaboration skills, with experience working in cross-functional teams.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with TypeScript and its integration in Vue.js applications.
- Knowledge of Webpack and other modern front-end build tools.
- Familiarity with Progressive Web App (PWA) principles and implementations.
- Understanding of Agile/Scrum methodologies and participation in iterative development cycles.
