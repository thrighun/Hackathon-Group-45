## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement user-friendly mobile applications for iOS and Android platforms.
- Work with cross-functional teams to collect and refine user requirements into technical specifications.
- Ensure the performance, quality, and responsiveness of applications through continuous testing and debugging.
- Contribute to the development of mobile architecture and best practices to improve codebase quality.
- Mentor junior team members and foster a collaborative team environment.
- Research and integrate new mobile technologies and frameworks into existing workflows.

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in mobile application development.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Swift, Kotlin, and JavaScript.
- Experience with popular mobile frameworks like React Native and Flutter.
- Strong understanding of RESTful APIs and integration with back-end services.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, along with strong attention to detail.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with mobile application deployment processes and tools, including App Store and Google Play.
- Knowledge of CI/CD tools and practices for mobile development.
- Experience working in an Agile/Scrum environment is preferred.
