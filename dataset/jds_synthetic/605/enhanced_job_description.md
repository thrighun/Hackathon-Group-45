## Job Title
Frontend Engineer (Vue.js & Bootstrap)

## Location
Remote Available

## Client Industry
Information Technology / Web Development

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain dynamic web applications using Vue.js and Bootstrap frameworks.
- Collaborate closely with product managers and designers to create exceptional user experiences through intuitive interfaces.
- Ensure the implementation of responsive design principles to optimize web applications for various devices and resolutions.
- Conduct thorough testing and debugging of applications to ensure high standards of performance and reliability.
- Work in an Agile environment, participating in sprint planning and daily stand-ups to contribute to project goals.
- Keep abreast of industry trends and emerging technologies, recommending new solutions for enhanced user engagement.
- Document development processes, maintain records of changes, and participate in knowledge-sharing activities within the team.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience.
- Demonstrated expertise in Vue.js, Bootstrap, and modern JavaScript (ES6+).
- Familiarity with HTML5, CSS3, and responsive design methodologies.
- Experience integrating RESTful APIs and working with front-end state management tools (Vuex).
- Proficiency in version control systems, specifically Git, and familiarity with collaborative workflows.
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills with a focus on detail-oriented tasks.
- Effective communication skills and ability to work collaboratively within a team setting.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with TypeScript and front-end testing frameworks (Cypress, Mocha) is advantageous.
- Understanding of continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD) processes and associated tools.
- Exposure to Agile/Scrum practices and project management tools (JIRA, Trello).
- A strong portfolio showcasing relevant projects, contributions to development communities, or open-source involvement.
