## Job Title
Front-End Engineer specializing in Vue.js and Bootstrap

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Technology Services

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Create and maintain user-friendly web interfaces using Vue.js and Bootstrap frameworks.
- Collaborate with UX/UI designers to implement design specifications and enhance user experience.
- Optimize web applications for maximum speed and scalability.
- Write reusable, testable, and efficient code, adhering to best practices.
- Identify and fix bugs, ensuring smooth application functionality across different devices and browsers.
- Conduct code reviews and provide constructive feedback to peers to foster a culture of continuous improvement.
- Stay informed on the latest trends in front-end development and incorporate relevant advancements into projects.

## Skill Requirements
- At least 4 years of experience in front-end development, specifically with Vue.js.
- Strong proficiency in Bootstrap for responsive design.
- Solid understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Experience working with RESTful APIs and integrating backend services.
- Familiarity with modern front-end build tools and frameworks.
- Proficient with version control systems, particularly Git.
- Excellent analytical skills with a keen attention to detail.
- Strong communication skills and ability to work within a team environment.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with TypeScript is a plus.
- Experience with mobile-first design principles.
- Understanding of Progressive Web Apps (PWAs).
- Knowledge of accessibility standards and best practices in web development.
