## Job Title
Cloud Solutions Architect (Python/AWS)

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology / Cloud Computing

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement scalable cloud architectures using AWS services, ensuring adherence to best practices and security standards.
- Develop microservices using Python, integrating with existing systems and facilitating seamless data flows.
- Collaborate with DevOps teams to automate deployment processes and enhance system reliability through CI/CD pipelines.
- Analyze system performance metrics and conduct troubleshooting to resolve complex cloud-based issues.
- Engage with stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into technical specifications that guide system design.
- Mentor junior architects and developers, fostering a culture of continuous improvement and knowledge sharing.
- Stay current with industry trends and emerging technologies to recommend innovative cloud solutions.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- At least 5 years of experience in cloud architecture and backend development, particularly with Python.
- Strong proficiency in AWS services, including Lambda, EC2, S3, and RDS.
- Experience designing and implementing microservices and APIs with a focus on performance and scalability.
- Familiarity with infrastructure as code (IaC) tools such as Terraform or CloudFormation.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with a strong attention to detail.
- Effective communication skills, with the ability to work collaboratively across teams.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of container orchestration with Kubernetes or similar platforms.
- Understanding of security best practices in cloud environments.
- Prior experience in Agile methodologies and project management frameworks.
- Contributions to cloud computing forums or open-source cloud projects are a plus.
- AWS certifications (e.g., Solutions Architect) would be advantageous.
