## Job Title
Embedded Software Engineer - C++ & Real-Time Systems

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain firmware for embedded devices using C++ within real-time operating systems.
- Analyze system requirements and translate them into efficient software design and implementation.
- Work collaboratively with hardware engineers to ensure seamless integration of software and hardware components.
- Perform system-level testing and validation to guarantee the reliability and performance of embedded applications.
- Contribute to the development of technical documentation, including specifications and design documents.
- Participate in Agile sprints, providing regular updates on progress and collaborating in sprint planning sessions.
- Mentor junior developers and interns, fostering an environment of continuous learning and innovation.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
- At least 5 years of professional experience in embedded C++ development focused on real-time systems.
- Strong familiarity with RTOS concepts and application development in embedded environments.
- Proficient in using debugging tools and methodologies tailored for embedded software.
- Solid understanding of hardware interfaces such as SPI, I2C, and UART.
- Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills with a keen attention to detail.
- Strong interpersonal skills, capable of working in a collaborative team environment.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with additional programming languages such as C or assembly language.
- Familiarity with Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) practices.
- Knowledge of safety-critical software development standards such as MISRA or DO-178.
- Previous experience in the technology provider industry with a focus on embedded solutions.
