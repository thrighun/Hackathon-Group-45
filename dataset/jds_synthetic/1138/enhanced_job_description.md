## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead and manage a team of data engineers focusing on the design and implementation of efficient, scalable data pipelines and warehousing solutions using Spark, SQL, and Java.
- Collaborate closely with data scientists, product owners, and business analysts to understand data requirements and translate them into technical specifications for data solutions.
- Develop and oversee comprehensive ETL processes to ensure the seamless extraction, transformation, and loading of data from various sources, maintaining high data quality standards.
- Implement best practices for data governance, security compliance, and operational resilience throughout the data handling lifecycle.
- Proactively monitor system performance and reliability, identifying and resolving issues that affect data availability and integrity.
- Champion a culture of innovation and continuous improvement by promoting agile methodologies, conducting regular training sessions, and encouraging team collaboration.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong expertise in Apache Spark, SQL, and Java programming languages.
- In-depth knowledge of data engineering best practices, ETL processes, and data warehousing techniques.
- Proven experience in managing technical teams, with a demonstrated ability to mentor and develop engineering talent.
- Excellent analytical skills, problem-solving abilities, and attention to detail.
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the capacity to work collaboratively across diverse teams and stakeholders.
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Engineering, or a related field.

## Other Requirements
- Relevant certifications in Spark, SQL, or data engineering are preferred.
- Experience with cloud-based data platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and data visualization tools is advantageous.
