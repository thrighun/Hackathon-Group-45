## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead a team of testers to establish best practices in performance and usability testing.
- Collaborate closely with developers to understand product requirements and translate them into test scenarios.
- Develop comprehensive test plans, including test strategies and metrics for tracking quality.
- Analyze test results and provide actionable feedback to improve product performance.
- Mentor and train junior quality assurance staff in testing methodologies and tools.

## Skill Requirements
- Primary Skills: Performance Testing
- Secondary Skills: Usability Testing
- Proven expertise in automation testing tools and frameworks.
- Strong analytical skills and a methodical approach to problem-solving.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills for effective collaboration with cross-functional teams.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in software quality assurance in the IT sector.
- Relevant certifications in quality assurance or software testing (e.g., ISTQB).
