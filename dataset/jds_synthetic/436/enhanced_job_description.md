## Detailed Responsibilities
- Respond to incoming customer calls and emails, utilizing clear communication skills to understand and address needs efficiently.
- Troubleshoot basic technical issues and provide solutions or escalate as necessary to ensure swift problem resolution.
- Maintain accurate records of customer interactions and follow up as needed to guarantee ongoing satisfaction.
- Engage in training sessions for product knowledge enhancement and skill development, contributing to overall team success.
- Actively gather insights from customer interactions to recommend improvements in service delivery and operational processes.
- Uphold company policies and customer confidentiality standards, ensuring ethical conduct in all interactions.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong verbal and written communication skills with a focus on clarity and professionalism.
- Basic problem-solving skills and the ability to think critically under pressure.
- Ability to engage pleasantly with customers and maintain a positive demeanor.
- Willingness to learn and adapt quickly to new technologies and processes.

## Other Requirements
- High school diploma or equivalent; fresh graduates are welcome to apply.
- Interest in technology and a customer-focused approach are highly valued. 
- Experience in customer service or technical support is a plus but not mandatory.
