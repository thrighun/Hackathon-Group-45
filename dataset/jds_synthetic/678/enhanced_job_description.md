## Job Title
Senior Software Engineer - Node.js and Angular Developer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and maintain web applications using Node.js and Angular.
- Collaborate effectively with product owners, UX/UI designers, and other stakeholders to gather and define project requirements.
- Ensure that applications are scalable, resilient, and efficiently designed.
- Mentor junior developers and lead code reviews to enhance team performance and adherence to coding standards.
- Identify and troubleshoot complex application issues, ensuring quick resolutions.
- Participate actively in Agile ceremonies, contributing to sprint planning and retrospective meetings.
- Keep abreast of emerging technologies and recommend best practices to enhance product quality.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- 6-10 years of experience in software development, focusing on Node.js and Angular.
- Proficiency in JavaScript and TypeScript, as well as knowledge of frameworks and libraries related to Angular.
- Strong understanding of RESTful services and APIs for seamless integration.
- Experience with database technologies such as MongoDB or PostgreSQL.
- Familiarity with version control systems like Git and CI/CD pipelines.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities, with a focus on detail and quality.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of cloud services such as Google Cloud Platform or Microsoft Azure.
- Experience with containerization tools such as Docker.
- Familiarity with test-driven development and automated testing frameworks.
- Leadership experience and effective communication skills are highly desirable.
