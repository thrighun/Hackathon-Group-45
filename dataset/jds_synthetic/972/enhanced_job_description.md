## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement firmware for low-power IoT devices, ensuring efficient performance and connectivity.
- Collaborate with hardware and product design teams to define firmware specifications and requirements.
- Optimize firmware for energy efficiency and seamless integration with cloud services.
- Conduct debugging and testing of firmware to identify and resolve issues prior to product launch.
- Document firmware architecture, designs, and APIs for team reference and future development.
- Stay current with advancements in IoT technology to continuously enhance firmware solutions.
- Assist in the development of training materials and technical documentation for users and stakeholders.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as C, C++, and Python.
- Experience with embedded systems and firmware development for IoT applications.
- Familiarity with low-power wireless communication protocols such as MQTT, Zigbee, and Bluetooth.
- Strong analytical skills with a focus on problem-solving and troubleshooting.
- Ability to work in a dynamic team environment and communicate effectively across departments.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with microcontroller and sensor integration for IoT applications.
- Understanding of agile software development methodologies and practices.
- Knowledge of cloud platforms, such as AWS IoT or Microsoft Azure IoT.
- Familiarity with security protocols and best practices for IoT device firmware development.
