## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect and design robust and scalable systems using Python, ensuring alignment with business goals and technical capabilities.
- Lead the development of high-level system design documents and detailed architecture specifications.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including product management, QA, and DevOps, to establish best practices and drive project success.

- Strong interpersonal and communication skills to effectively collaborate with diverse teams.

## Other Requirements
