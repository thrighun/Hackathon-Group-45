## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement embedded software for Internet of Things (IoT) devices utilizing C and Python.
- Collaborate with hardware engineers to ensure seamless integration of software with new IoT products.
- Develop algorithms for data collection, processing, and transmission over various communication networks.
- Conduct thorough testing and validation of IoT solutions to ensure reliability and efficiency.
- Participate in agile development processes and contribute to sprint planning and reviews.
- Create comprehensive documentation of firmware architecture, design, and user manuals.
- Stay updated with IoT trends, cybersecurity measures, and best practices in device management.
- Provide support during the product lifecycle, including troubleshooting and product updates.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in C and Python programming languages.
- Comprehensive experience in developing software for IoT applications.
- Strong understanding of network communication protocols (e.g., MQTT, CoAP).
- Familiarity with embedded systems and microcontroller platforms.
- Experience with testing tools and methodologies for embedded software.
- Excellent analytical skills and attention to detail.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related field.
- Relevant certifications in IoT or embedded systems development (preferred).

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of cloud platforms and data analytics tools.
- Familiarity with version control systems like Git.
- Understanding of device security protocols and best practices.
- Strong communication and teamwork skills.
