- Design, develop, and maintain automated test scripts to validate software functionality and performance.
- Collaborate with developers, product managers, and other stakeholders to identify test requirements and ensure comprehensive test coverage.
- Execute automated and manual tests, analyze results, and report on software quality metrics.
- Continuously improve testing frameworks and processes to enhance efficiency and effectiveness.
- Participate in design reviews and provide feedback on application design for testability.

## Skill Requirements
- Primary Skills: Automation Testing (Selenium, Java/C#)
- Secondary Skills: API Testing and Performance Testing
- Strong analytical skills with a focus on problem-solving.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills to liaise with team members and management.
- Proven experience in creating and executing test plans and test cases.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field.
- Minimum of 3 years of experience in software testing, with a strong emphasis on automation.
- Knowledge of Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) processes.
- Relevant certifications in test automation or quality assurance are a plus.
