## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect and develop microservices solutions using Spring Cloud and related frameworks.
- Collaborate with business analysts and developers to define the system architecture and design.
- Mentor and lead developer teams in best practices for microservices development and deployment.
- Analyze and troubleshoot system performance and scalability issues, implementing necessary improvements.
- Create and maintain documentation for microservice architecture, including APIs and integration points.
- Engage with cloud technologies and tools to enhance application efficiency and performance.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a relevant field.
- 5-8 years of experience in designing and implementing microservices architectures.
- Proficient in Spring Cloud, RESTful APIs, and microservices frameworks.
- Strong knowledge of container orchestration with Kubernetes and continuous integration/deployment methods.
- Experience with databases (both SQL and NoSQL) and API gateway technologies.
- Excellent communication skills and proven ability to work in a collaborative environment.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with cloud service providers such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud is preferred.
- Understanding of serverless architecture principles is a bonus.
- Experience working with Agile methodologies and DevOps culture is an advantage.
