## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop cloud-native applications following microservices architecture principles to ensure high performance and scalability.
- Lead architectural strategy sessions, fostering collaboration among developers, product managers, and stakeholders to define effective application specifications.
- Oversee code quality through rigorous reviews, ensuring adherence to best practices in security, performance, and maintainability.
- Research and integrate emerging technologies into the software development lifecycle, driving innovation within the team and projects.
- Mentor and guide junior developers in architectural principles, Agile methodologies, and best practices in software design and implementation.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless integration and deployment of applications, utilizing CI/CD pipelines for efficient delivery.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in Python programming and framework experience (e.g., Django, Flask) for backend development.
- Strong expertise in frontend development using React, ensuring a seamless user experience while integrating with backend services.
- In-depth understanding of microservices architecture, including service design, API development, and service orchestration.
- Knowledge of cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud, including their services for deploying and managing cloud applications.
- Familiarity with relational databases such as PostgreSQL, including performance tuning and query optimization.
- Experience with Agile methodologies and best practices in continuous integration and development.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in software development with a solid track record in architectural roles.
- Relevant certifications in cloud technologies (AWS, Azure) are advantageous but not mandatory.
- Previous experience with DevOps practices and tools, containerization (e.g., Docker), and orchestration (e.g., Kubernetes) is a plus.
- Excellent communication and leadership skills, capable of fostering collaboration in a remote work environment.
