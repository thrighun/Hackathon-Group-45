## Detailed Responsibilities
- Leverage performance monitoring tools to proactively detect and analyze system performance issues, ensuring swift incident identification and resolution.
- Work closely with IT operations and engineering teams to form effective incident response protocols, thus improving system reliability and minimizing downtime.
- Compile and analyze performance data to generate comprehensive reports that provide actionable insights into system health and operational efficiency.
- Conduct thorough post-incident reviews to determine root causes, documenting findings and implementing measures to mitigate future incidents.
- Develop and update operational documentation, including training materials that promote best practices and encourage knowledge sharing within the team.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to establish continual improvement processes aimed at enhancing IT service delivery and client satisfaction.

## Skill Requirements
- Advanced proficiency in performance monitoring and incident management tools (e.g., Dynatrace, AppDynamics, New Relic).
- Strong understanding of IT operations management and system reliability engineering principles.
- Proficiency in data analysis and reporting methodologies to support performance management.
- Experience with troubleshooting methodologies and incident response frameworks.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills to effectively collaborate with diverse teams.

## Other Requirements
- ITIL Foundation Certification (preferred but not mandatory).
- Relevant technical certifications in performance monitoring or IT operations (optional but beneficial).
- Familiarity with cloud-based monitoring solutions and automation tools is advantageous.
