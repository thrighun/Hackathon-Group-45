## Job Title
Frontend Engineer with Vue.js and Nuxt.js skills

## Location
Remote/Hybrid Options Available

## Client Industry
Technology Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement dynamic web applications using Vue.js and Nuxt.js frameworks.
- Work closely with UX/UI designers and backend developers to create seamless user experiences.
- Write modular, reusable, and efficient code in accordance with modern development practices.
- Ensure application performance, security, and reliability through thorough testing and code optimization.
- Participate in agile development processes and contribute to sprint planning and retrospectives.
- Provide mentorship and support to junior developers, fostering a culture of learning within the team.
- Research and evaluate emerging technologies to incorporate into future web applications.

## Skill Requirements
- 2-6 years of experience in front-end development, specifically utilizing Vue.js and Nuxt.js frameworks.
- Proficient in JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3, with a strong understanding of ES6+ features.
- Experience with state management libraries such as Vuex or Pinia.
- Knowledge of RESTful services and GraphQL, including how to integrate them into applications.
- Familiarity with testing frameworks such as Jest or Cypress for improved code quality.
- Strong analytical skills and the ability to troubleshoot complex issues effectively.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills for collaborative teamwork.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with mobile-first design principles and progressive web apps (PWAs).
- Knowledge of TypeScript for enhanced code maintainability.
- Familiarity with automated build tools such as Webpack or Vite.
- Understanding of accessibility principles and best practices for inclusive design.
