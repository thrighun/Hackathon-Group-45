## Job Title
Cloud Microservices Developer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement cloud-based microservices architectures to enhance application scalability and resilience.
- Develop and optimize frontend applications using modern JavaScript frameworks to improve user experience.
- Collaborate with product managers and designers to translate business requirements into technical specifications.
- Build efficient APIs for seamless integration between services and third-party applications.
- Monitor and troubleshoot application performance, ensuring high availability and low latency.
- Participate in code reviews and ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices.
- Research and adopt emerging technologies that can enhance application functionality and performance.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in developing microservices with frameworks such as Spring Boot or Express.js.
- Strong experience with frontend technologies, specifically React, Angular, or Vue.js.
- Familiarity with API design principles, including REST and GraphQL.
- Experience with cloud platforms, particularly AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud, in deploying and managing services.
- Understanding of containerization tools like Docker and orchestration platforms such as Kubernetes.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Excellent analytical skills and the ability to solve complex technical problems.
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills for effective team collaboration.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD) practices.
- Knowledge of database technologies, including SQL and NoSQL solutions.
- Familiarity with agile development methodologies and project management tools.
- Certification in relevant cloud services or microservices architecture.
