## Job Title
Senior Front-end Engineer

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement user interfaces for SaaS applications using modern front-end technologies.
- Utilize TypeScript to enhance code quality and maintainability across front-end projects.
- Collaborate closely with UX/UI designers to ensure a seamless and visually appealing user experience.
- Integrate with RESTful APIs to fetch and display data dynamically within the application.
- Conduct thorough code reviews and mentor junior developers to foster a culture of learning and improvement.
- Optimize application for maximum speed and scalability while maintaining high standards of quality.
- Remain informed about emerging technologies and industry trends to continuously improve development practices.
- Contribute to Agile methodologies, participating in sprint planning and daily stand-ups.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in front-end development with a strong focus on TypeScript.
- Proficiency in building responsive applications and working with RESTful APIs.
- Excellent understanding of JavaScript, HTML, CSS, and modern front-end frameworks.
- Strong problem-solving abilities and attention to detail.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with version control systems like Git.
- Familiarity with testing frameworks and methodologies for front-end applications.
- Knowledge of CI/CD practices and experience deploying applications in cloud environments.
- Prior experience in the SaaS industry or similar technology-driven environments.
