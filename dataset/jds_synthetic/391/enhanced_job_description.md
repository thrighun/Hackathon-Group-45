## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement user-friendly web applications using Angular framework.
- Work closely with product managers and designers to translate UX/UI designs into high-quality code.
- Develop reusable components and libraries for future use.
- Ensure high performance of applications and provide support for both new and existing features.
- Conduct tests and debugging processes to ensure software meets quality standards.
- Collaborate in an agile environment with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality software solutions on time.
- Contribute to the continual improvement of development practices and methodologies.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- 3-6 years of experience in front-end development with a strong focus on Angular.
- Proficiency in JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS3.
- Solid understanding of RESTful APIs and asynchronous programming.
- Experience with version control systems, especially Git.
- Excellent problem-solving abilities and a strong attention to detail.
- Great verbal and written communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of state management libraries (such as NgRx or Redux).
- Familiarity with performance optimization techniques and best practices.
- Understanding of accessibility considerations in web applications.
- Experience with modern build tools and CI/CD processes.
