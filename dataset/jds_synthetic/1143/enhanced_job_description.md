## Job Title
Senior Frontend Engineer (Vue.js)

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Retail

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement robust and scalable web applications using Vue.js to enhance the online shopping experience.
- Collaborate closely with UX/UI designers and product stakeholders to translate visual designs into functional and engaging web applications.
- Conduct performance optimization and code refactoring of existing platforms to improve load times and overall user experience.
- Review code and provide constructive feedback to ensure adherence to best practices and coding standards within the team.
- Ensure cross-browser compatibility and responsiveness across devices to maintain a seamless user experience.
- Stay informed about industry trends, best practices, and emerging technologies to drive innovation within the team.
- Support the onboarding and ongoing development of junior team members, fostering a culture of growth and knowledge sharing.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum 6 years of experience in frontend development with a strong focus on Vue.js.
- Expertise in building eCommerce applications and a solid understanding of modern web development standards.
- Proficiency in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and related frameworks or libraries.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with an ability to navigate complex challenges.
- Excellent communication skills to effectively collaborate with team members and stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with version control systems, particularly Git.
- Understanding of backend APIs and experience working with RESTful services.
- Experience with agile methodologies and project management tools like JIRA or Trello.
- A strong portfolio showcasing innovative projects and contributions to frontend development.
