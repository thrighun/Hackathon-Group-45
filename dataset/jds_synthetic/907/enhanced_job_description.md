## Job Title
Application Architect

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the architectural design and technical direction for enterprise applications, ensuring they are robust, scalable, and maintainable.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and transform them into technical specifications and architectural designs.
- Ensure the integration of various components and services, adhering to security and performance best practices.
- Provide mentorship and guidance to development teams on architectural standards and coding best practices.
- Conduct regular code reviews and performance assessments to ensure high quality and optimization of applications.
- Stay abreast of emerging technologies and industry trends to continuously improve architectural practices.
- Create and maintain architectural documentation to facilitate ongoing development and scalability.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline.
- 7-12 years of experience in software architecture and design, with a strong focus on enterprise application design.
- Proficiency in architectural patterns such as microservices, event-driven architecture, and serverless design.
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and their respective services.
- Strong programming skills in languages such as Java, C#, or Go, with familiarity in modern frameworks and libraries.
- Knowledge of security principles and practices for application development and deployment.
- Excellent analytical skills and a solid understanding of system performance metrics.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with DevOps practices and CI/CD tools.
- Experience with containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Strong communication skills, with the ability to articulate complex concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Agile or Scrum certification is a plus.
