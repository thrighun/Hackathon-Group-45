## Job Title
Data Engineer – AI Solutions

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Global Technology Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain scalable data pipelines to support data ingestion and processing for AI applications.
- Collaborate with data scientists and machine learning engineers to prepare and optimize datasets for modeling and analysis.
- Implement data quality checks and validation processes to ensure accuracy and reliability of data across systems.
- Optimize ETL processes to enhance performance and minimize latency in data retrieval for real-time applications.
- Write efficient, maintainable, and well-documented code, complying with best practices in data engineering.
- Participate in architecture discussions and provide recommendations for data architecture improvements.
- Conduct performance tuning and troubleshooting to resolve issues in data processing workflows.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field.
- 4 to 6 years of experience in data engineering with strong proficiency in Python and SQL.
- Familiarity with big data technologies (e.g., Hadoop, Spark) and cloud data services (AWS Redshift, Google BigQuery).
- Experience with data modeling, data warehousing, and ETL tools.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to work effectively in a team environment.

## Other Requirements
- Prior experience integrating machine learning models into data pipelines is desirable.
- Knowledge of containerization and orchestration technologies (Docker, Kubernetes) is a plus.
- Experience working in an agile environment with cross-functional teams.
- Relevant certifications in data engineering or cloud data services are an advantage.
