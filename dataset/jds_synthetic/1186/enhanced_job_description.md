## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop, test, and maintain firmware for microcontroller-based applications in consumer electronics.
- Collaborate with hardware engineers to create and refine hardware specifications and firmware interfaces.
- Conduct rigorous testing, including unit tests and integration tests, to ensure firmware quality and reliability.
- Implement power optimization techniques to extend the battery life of embedded devices.
- Document firmware development processes and maintain detailed records of design changes and test results.
- Provide ongoing support and optimization for existing firmware products following launch, responding to customer feedback and issues.
- Participate in design reviews and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives within the firmware development team.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, or related field.
- 4-5 years of hands-on experience in firmware development using C and C++ specifically for embedded systems.
- Strong familiarity with hardware interfaces and protocols, such as I2C, SPI, and GPIO.
- Experience with embedded debugging tools and methodologies, including JTAG and oscilloscopes.
- Understanding of low-level programming concepts and real-time operating systems (RTOS).
- Proficient in version control systems and build automation tools.
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills as well as the ability to work independently and in a team.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in developing firmware for safety-critical applications is a plus.
- Familiarity with Agile development practices and methodologies.
- Knowledge of wireless communication protocols (e.g., Bluetooth, Wi-Fi) for embedded applications is advantageous.
- Certifications in embedded systems or related frameworks are beneficial.
