## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the integration and validation of automotive systems, ensuring seamless functionality between electrical, software, and mechanical components.
- Utilize simulation and modeling tools like MATLAB/Simulink for systems architecture and performance analysis.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop system requirements and specifications that align with automotive safety standards such as ISO 26262.
- Conduct thorough testing and diagnostics on integrated systems, identifying any issues and implementing effective solutions.
- Manage project timelines and deliverables while ensuring compliance with industry regulations and quality standards.
- Provide technical mentorship to junior engineers, fostering a culture of innovation and continuous improvement.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 3 years of experience in automotive systems integration and architecture.
- Proficient with simulation tools, specifically MATLAB/Simulink, and familiarity with embedded systems development.
- Strong understanding of automotive standards, particularly ISO 26262 for functional safety.
- Experience with system testing methodologies and troubleshooting automotive electronics.

## Other Requirements
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving capabilities.
- Strong communication skills, with the ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.
- Proven track record of managing multiple projects simultaneously while meeting deadlines effectively.
