## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and optimize ETL processes to streamline data ingestion and transformation across various platforms.
- Develop and maintain efficient data warehouse solutions using relational and non-relational databases to support analytical needs.
- Collaborate closely with data scientists and business analysts to ensure a seamless flow of clean, reliable data for reporting and analysis.
- Monitor and improve data quality metrics and troubleshoot data-related issues proactively.
- Conduct performance tuning and optimization for complex queries and data processing workflows.
- Provide ongoing maintenance and support for existing data systems, ensuring adherence to service level agreements.
- Stay informed about emerging technologies in data engineering and analytics to propose innovative solutions.

## Skill Requirements
- Proven experience as a Data Engineer or in a related data management role.
- Strong proficiency in SQL, Python, and data modeling best practices.
- Familiarity with big data technologies such as Hadoop, Spark, and data streaming tools like Kafka.
- Experience with data storage solutions in cloud environments, particularly AWS or Azure.
- Understanding of data warehousing methodologies and best practices.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with data visualization tools such as Tableau, Power BI, or similar platforms.
- Exposure to containerization technologies like Docker and orchestration platforms such as Kubernetes.
- Knowledge of machine learning principles and relevance in data architecture design.
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field is preferred.
