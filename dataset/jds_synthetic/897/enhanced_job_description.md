## Job Title
ServiceNow Technical Consultant

## Location
Remote/On-site options available

## Client Industry
Information Technology Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze client requirements and design solutions that leverage the ServiceNow platform effectively.
- Lead the deployment of ServiceNow modules, ensuring alignment with best practices and client business processes.
- Provide expert guidance on ServiceNow enhancements and functionality improvements based on industry trends.
- Collaborate with stakeholders to define technical specifications and deliver tailored ServiceNow solutions.
- Monitor and maintain system performance, ensuring high availability and optimal functionality.
- Conduct training sessions for end-users and administrators on ServiceNow features and applications.
- Drive continuous improvement initiatives and participate in user acceptance testing (UAT) to validate system changes.
- Develop and manage project timelines, deliverables, and status reports for ongoing service delivery.

## Skill Requirements
- A minimum of 3+ years of experience as a ServiceNow Technical Consultant or similar role.
- Advanced knowledge of ServiceNow applications including ITSM, ITBM, and Security Incident Management.
- Strong proficiency in JavaScript, Glide scripting, and ServiceNow development environments.
- Familiarity with Agile/Scrum methodologies and project management tools.
- Bachelor's degree in Information Systems, Computer Science, or a related field.
- ServiceNow Certified Implementation Specialist (CIS) or relevant certifications are mandatory.
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills with an emphasis on delivering customer satisfaction.
- Excellent communication and facilitation skills, capable of working with diverse teams.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in ITIL framework and ServiceNow best practices.
- Knowledge of data modeling, relational databases, and reporting tools.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple projects concurrently.
