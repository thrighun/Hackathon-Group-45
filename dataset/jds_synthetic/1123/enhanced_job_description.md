## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement effective test strategies tailored to specific software applications.
- Execute manual and automated test cases to uncover issues before software deployment.
- Collaborate extensively with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and refine testing approaches.
- Develop testing frameworks and contribute to the integration of testing tools within the development pipeline.
- Report testing outcomes and participate in retrospectives to help shape future quality assurance practices.
- Maintain comprehensive documentation of tests, defects, and improvements.
- Proactively engage in continuous learning to keep up with evolving technologies and testing methods.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline.
- Relevant experience in software testing or quality assurance roles.
- Proficiency in both manual and automated testing techniques.
- Familiarity with industry-standard testing tools (e.g., QTP, LoadRunner).
- Understanding of DevOps practices and CI/CD pipelines.
- Strong problem-solving abilities and a keen attention to detail.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with test management tools (e.g., JIRA, TestRail).
- Basic programming knowledge (e.g., JavaScript, Ruby) is a plus.
- Relevant certifications in Quality Assurance (e.g., CSTE, CSTP).
