## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement scalable cloud architectures using AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform, ensuring alignment with industry best practices.
- Lead cross-functional teams through the software development lifecycle, providing architectural guidance and ensuring adherence to project design standards.
- Evaluate, select, and integrate appropriate cloud services and tools for deployment and management, ensuring high availability and disaster recovery capabilities.
- Mentor and facilitate the development of architecture artifacts, including cloud solutions, system integrations, and implementation plans.
- Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to gather requirements and translate them into technical specifications for cloud-based applications.
- Stay updated on industry trends and emerging technologies in cloud computing, DevOps, and container orchestration to drive continuous improvement and innovation.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in designing cloud architectures and services using various cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).
- Strong knowledge of container orchestration technologies, specifically Kubernetes, for deploying and managing microservices.
- Experience with scripting languages such as Python or Bash for automation and integration purposes.
- Familiarity with relational and NoSQL databases, particularly PostgreSQL and their cloud implementations.
- Knowledge of CI/CD practices and tools to ensure efficient application lifecycle management.

## Other Requirements
- Relevant certifications in cloud architecture (e.g., AWS Certified Solutions Architect, Azure Solutions Architect Expert) are highly desirable.
- Experience in Agile methodologies and DevOps practices.
- Previous exposure to large-scale enterprise environments and cloud migration projects is beneficial.
- Excellent organizational and communication skills to facilitate collaboration among diverse teams and stakeholders.
