## Detailed Responsibilities
- Create, maintain, and execute comprehensive test plans and test cases based on project requirements.
- Report, track, and manage bugs and discrepancies in a timely and efficient manner.
- Work closely with the development team throughout the SDLC to ensure a quality-first approach.
- Conduct various types of testing including usability, integration, and regression testing.
- Provide detailed feedback and reports on testing outcomes to project stakeholders.
- Engage in early testing by participating in sprint planning sessions and requirement reviews.
- Drive improvements in automated testing frameworks and methodologies to enhance overall testing efficiency.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Proven experience as a Software Quality Analyst or similar position.
- In-depth knowledge of QA processes, methodologies, and best practices.
- Familiarity with automated testing frameworks (e.g., TestNG, Cucumber) and manual testing tools.
- Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments is highly desirable.
- Strong analytical and critical thinking abilities.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with mobile application testing.
- Basic knowledge of coding or scripting languages (e.g., JavaScript, Ruby).
- Quality Assurance certifications (e.g., CSTE, CSQA) are a plus.
