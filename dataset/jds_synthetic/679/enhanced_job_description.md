- Create scalable and maintainable front-end architecture by utilizing Vuetify for UI component development.
- Conduct performance testing and optimization to enhance application speed and responsiveness across devices.
- Collaborate closely with UX/UI designers to translate wireframes and prototypes into high-quality code.
- Ensure the application is SEO-friendly and follows best practices for web standards and accessibility.
- Contribute to team knowledge sharing through code reviews, documentation, and mentoring junior developers.
- Keep abreast of emerging technologies and propose innovations to improve development processes and product offerings.
