## Job Title
Front-End Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement user-friendly web interfaces using Vue.js and Vuex.
- Collaborate with product managers and backend engineers to define and refine project scopes.
- Ensure high performance of applications by optimizing application speed and scalability.
- Engage in code reviews and contribute to team knowledge sharing through discussions and documentation.
- Partner with graphic designers to seamlessly integrate visual elements into the user experience.
- Research and evaluate emerging front-end technologies to incorporate best practices into the development workflow.

## Skill Requirements
- At least 4 years of professional experience in front-end development with Vue.js.
- Strong understanding of HTML5, CSS3, and responsive design principles.
- Proficiency in state management and asynchronous programming within the Vue ecosystem.
- Familiarity with build tools like Webpack and task runners like Gulp.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of API integration, including RESTful and SOAP services.
- Experience with version control tools such as Git.
- Understanding of accessibility standards and best practices.
- Exposure to server-side frameworks like Node.js or Express is a plus.
