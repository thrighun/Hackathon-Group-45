## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the development and execution of comprehensive test plans and strategies for software projects.
- Mentor and support junior QA team members while fostering a culture of quality within the team.
- Collaborate with development teams to ensure that quality is embedded from the beginning of the software development lifecycle.
- Conduct rigorous testing of software applications to identify and resolve defects and ensure compliance with accessibility guidelines.
- Analyze testing results and prepare detailed reports to communicate findings and recommendations to stakeholders.

## Skill Requirements
- Primary Skills: Test Automation Frameworks (Selenium, Cypress)
- Secondary Skills: Manual Testing Techniques and Accessibility Standards
- Strong leadership abilities and a capacity to inspire and guide team members.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills for effective stakeholder interaction.
- Proven experience in using different testing tools and software lifecycle methodologies.

## Other Requirements
- Previous experience in a QA lead or senior role with a focus on software testing across multiple sectors.
- Relevant certifications in quality assurance or software testing methodologies (e.g., ISTQB).
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies and collaboration with DevOps teams.
