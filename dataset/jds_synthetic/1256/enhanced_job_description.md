## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and maintain test frameworks and infrastructures.
- Execute thorough functional, integration, and user acceptance testing.
- Document test findings and collaborate with the development team to resolve quality issues promptly.
- Utilize automated testing tools to streamline testing processes, enhancing overall efficiency.
- Provide feedback on usability and identify areas for improvement in software products.
- Engage in continuous learning about industry trends and best practices in quality assurance.
- Assist in the development of training materials for team members and new hires.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in a relevant field such as Computer Science or Information Technology.
- Previous experience in QA and a solid understanding of software development processes.
- Proficient in testing frameworks (e.g., TestNG, Cypress) and tools.
- Strong capabilities in debugging and problem-solving.
- Effective communication skills, both written and verbal, for reporting and discussing issues with stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with database tools and SQL for validating data.
- Experience in mobile application testing.
- Certification in Quality Assurance (e.g., CSTE, CSQA).
