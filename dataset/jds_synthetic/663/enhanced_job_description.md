## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement scalable data architectures for data ingestion, processing, and storage across various platforms.
- Develop ETL processes using tools such as Apache Airflow, Talend, or AWS Glue to ensure efficient data flow.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define data requirements and ensure data integrity across multiple sources.
- Optimize SQL queries and database performance for large datasets in relational and NoSQL databases.
- Implement data security measures, including encryption and access controls, to comply with regulatory standards in the pharmaceutical industry.
- Monitor and troubleshoot data pipeline performance issues, ensuring high availability and reliability.
- Provide mentorship to junior data engineers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement.
- Communicate effectively with stakeholders to gather insights and translate business requirements into technical solutions.
- Stay updated with industry trends in big data technologies and recommend appropriate solutions to enhance capabilities.

## Skill Requirements
- Extensive experience in data engineering with a strong understanding of data architecture principles.
- Proficiency in ETL tools such as Apache Airflow, Talend, or AWS Glue.
- Strong SQL skills with experience in optimizing queries on large datasets.
- Knowledge of both relational (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL) and NoSQL (e.g., MongoDB, Cassandra) databases.
- Familiarity with data security best practices and regulatory compliance, specifically in the pharma industry.
- Experience with cloud platforms like AWS or Azure, including their data services.
- Strong analytical problem-solving skills and the ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.

## Other Requirements
- Proven experience in leading and mentoring a team of data engineers.
- Good communication skills to liaise with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Familiarity with agile methodologies and data management frameworks.
