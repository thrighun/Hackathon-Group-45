- Develop and maintain RESTful APIs for seamless integration with various applications.
- Collaborate with UX/UI teams to create user-friendly interfaces and functionalities.
- Conduct performance optimization and troubleshooting for existing services.
- Perform comprehensive testing across different levels for quality assurance.
