## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze and interpret complex data sets related to business metrics and performance.
- Develop and implement statistical models to forecast and optimize business outcomes.
- Collaborate with machine learning engineers in creating the data pipelines necessary for model training and evaluation.
- Communicate findings and insights through compelling visualizations and presentations to non-technical stakeholders.
- Conduct exploratory data analysis and hypothesis testing to uncover trends and patterns in large datasets.
- Stay informed of the latest developments in data science methodologies and industry trends, integrating relevant techniques into projects.
- Automate data collection and analysis processes to enhance efficiency.
- Assist in the design and implementation of experiments to test and validate business hypotheses.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in Python or R, with a strong understanding of libraries such as Pandas, NumPy, and SciPy.
- Experience with statistical analysis techniques and proficiency in designing and interpreting A/B tests.
- Familiarity with machine learning principles and ability to work alongside machine learning engineers.
- Knowledge of data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI, Matplotlib) to present data insights clearly.
- Strong background in data manipulation and preprocessing techniques.
- Excellent problem-solving abilities and a critical thinking mindset.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills to convey data-driven insights effectively.
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related field.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with big data platforms such as Hadoop or Spark is an advantage.
- Understanding of SQL for querying relational databases.
- Knowledge of cloud computing tools like AWS or Google Cloud is beneficial.
- Familiarity with version control systems, preferably Git.
- Strong analytical skills and a passion for data-driven decision-making.
