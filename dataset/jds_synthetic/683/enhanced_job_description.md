## Job Title
ServiceNow Architect - Senior Level (ITSM, ITOM, SecOps)

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Global Information Technology Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect and lead the development of advanced ServiceNow solutions, focusing on ITSM, ITOM, and SecOps modules.
- Direct platform upgrades and propose enhancements to optimize performance and security across the ServiceNow environment.
- Spearhead the design and execution of integrations between ServiceNow and various enterprise applications, ensuring seamless data flow using APIs and web services.
- Oversee the implementation of Security Incident Response and Vulnerability Response processes within the ServiceNow framework.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand business needs, translating them into technical requirements and deliverables.
- Conduct thorough analyses and troubleshooting of technical issues, ensuring minimal disruption to services.
- Develop and maintain detailed technical documentation for architecture, integrations, and best practices.
- Mentor junior developers and provide guidance on ServiceNow best practices and methodologies.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- 8+ years of experience in ServiceNow architecture and development.
- Expertise in ITSM, ITOM, and SecOps modules within ServiceNow.
- Mastery of JavaScript, XML, HTML, and CSS relevant to ServiceNow.
- Extensive experience in guiding platform enhancements and strategic upgrades.
- Proven ability to design complex integrations using APIs and web services.
- In-depth knowledge of ServiceNow's Security Operations modules.
- Outstanding analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent communication skills with a strong focus on stakeholder engagement.
- ServiceNow Certified Application Developer (CAD) and ServiceNow Certified Implementation Specialist (CIS) certifications.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in ITIL framework and best practice methodologies.
- Familiarity with Agile/Scrum development practices.
- Knowledge of additional ServiceNow products and industry trends.
