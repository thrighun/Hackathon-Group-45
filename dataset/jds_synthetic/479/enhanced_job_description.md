## Job Title
Senior Backend Developer

## Location
Remote/On-site options available

## Client Industry
Innovative Software Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement dynamic server-side applications utilizing Node.js.
- Collaborate with product owners and stakeholders to define backend system requirements.
- Optimize applications for maximum speed and scalability.
- Manage database integration and ensure data integrity and security.
- Conduct code reviews and provide mentorship to junior developers.
- Document application processes and technical specifications.
- Proactively troubleshoot and resolve backend issues or bottlenecks.
- Stay abreast of new technologies and industry trends to enhance development practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Minimum of 3 years of experience in backend development with Node.js.
- Proficiency in database management systems like MongoDB or PostgreSQL.
- Strong grasp of RESTful APIs and microservices architecture.
- Familiarity with frontend frameworks (e.g., React or Angular) is a plus.
- Excellent analytical and debugging skills.
- Outstanding collaboration and communication skills within a team setting.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with containerization technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes).
- Knowledge of automated testing frameworks and CI/CD pipelines.
- Familiarity with Agile development methodologies.
