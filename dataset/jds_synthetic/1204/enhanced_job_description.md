## Detailed Responsibilities
- Serve as a primary liaison between project management teams and support services, ensuring seamless communication and resource alignment.
- Track and analyze resource utilization data to identify trends and areas for improvement, contributing to enhanced operational efficiency.
- Collaborate with the recruitment team to assess internal talent capabilities and address external hiring needs, ensuring timely fulfillment of project requirements.
- Maintain accurate workforce planning documentation and reporting tools, supporting strategic workforce management initiatives.
- Organize onboarding and training sessions for new team members, fostering a supportive environment for their integration.

- Technical Skills: Proficient in Unix-based operating systems and basic HR management software.
- Educational Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Human Resources, or related fields preferred.
- Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret data and generate actionable insights.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel for data analysis and PowerPoint for presentations.
- Excellent communication skills, with a focus on stakeholder engagement and team collaboration.

## Other Requirements
- Previous experience in resource coordination, HR operations, or workforce planning is advantageous.
- Familiarity with project management concepts and principles.
- Ability to thrive in a dynamic and fast-paced environment while managing multiple priorities.
