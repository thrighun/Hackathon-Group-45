## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and optimize firmware for embedded systems in consumer electronics.
- Collaborate with product designers and hardware engineers to translate specifications into functional firmware.
- Conduct code reviews, unit testing, and integration testing to ensure software quality and reliability.
- Diagnose, debug, and resolve hardware and firmware interaction issues to enhance product performance.
- Create and maintain comprehensive documentation for firmware design, interfaces, and user guidelines.
- Engage in research and continuous improvement to incorporate the latest technologies and methodologies in firmware development.
- Participate in project planning and estimation while ensuring adherence to timelines and deliverables.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- At least 3 years of experience in firmware development for consumer electronics or similar products.
- Proficient in C/C++ programming and familiar with low-level hardware interfaces.
- Strong understanding of microcontrollers and real-time firmware design principles.
- Experience with embedded software development tools and debugging techniques.
- Excellent analytical skills with a focus on problem-solving and troubleshooting.
- Strong communication skills and ability to work as part of a multidisciplinary team.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of wireless communication protocols (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Zigbee).
- Familiarity with software development practices such as Agile or Waterfall.
- Experience with automated testing strategies for embedded firmware.
- Certification in firmware or embedded system design is an advantage.
