## Detailed Responsibilities
- Utilize statistical methods to analyze and interpret complex datasets for business insights.
- Collaborate with product teams to improve data collection and processing methodologies.
- Develop predictive models and algorithms to anticipate trends and optimize outcomes.
- Create informative data visualizations to convey analytical findings to stakeholders.
- Communicate results and recommendations effectively to both technical and non-technical audiences.
- Conduct exploratory data analysis to identify patterns and anomalies in data.
- Monitor and validate model performance, making adjustments as needed to maintain accuracy.
- Research and implement new data science techniques and tools to enhance project efficiency.

- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, or a related field.
- 3-6 years of experience in data analysis and modeling.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as R, Python, or SQL.
- Solid understanding of statistical analysis techniques and machine learning algorithms.
- Experience with data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI) to present findings.
- Strong analytical and critical thinking skills with attention to detail.
- Effective communication skills for cross-functional collaboration.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with big data technologies (e.g., Hadoop, Spark).
- Experience with cloud computing platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure).
- Knowledge of natural language processing (NLP) or time series analysis is a plus.
