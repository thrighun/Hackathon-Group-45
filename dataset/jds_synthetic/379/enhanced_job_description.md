## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and oversee AWS Redshift data warehouse architecture to meet complex analytics and reporting requirements.
- Develop and maintain comprehensive data models that align with business objectives and optimize data storage strategies.
- Engineer ETL processes to ensure efficient and accurate data integration from diverse sources, utilizing Python and relevant tools.
- Use Terraform to establish and manage infrastructure as code, ensuring streamlined deployment and lifecycle management of data solutions.
- Collaborate with stakeholders, including data practitioners and business leaders, to define data architecture strategies and requirements.
- Regularly assess data security and compliance measures to ensure adherence to industry standards and regulations.
- Perform continuous tuning and optimization of data solutions to enhance performance and availability.
- Provide mentorship to junior staff, sharing knowledge and fostering a high-performance environment in data engineering practices.

## Skill Requirements
- In-depth knowledge of AWS Redshift for data warehousing solutions and analytics.
- Strong expertise in data modeling and ETL design, with hands-on experience in relevant tools.
- Proficiency in Python for data processing and automation of workflows.
- Familiarity with Terraform for infrastructure management and automation.
- Understanding of data security practices and governance frameworks for protecting sensitive information.
- Proven experience in collaborative environments, effectively interacting with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Strong analytical skills to evaluate data architecture performance and make necessary adjustments.

## Other Requirements
