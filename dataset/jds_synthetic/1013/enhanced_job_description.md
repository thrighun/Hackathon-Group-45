## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and maintain data models and analytical processes to support business decisions.
- Collaborate with business stakeholders to identify key data-driven opportunities and requirements.
- Conduct exploratory data analysis to discover trends and patterns in complex datasets.
- Create visualizations and reports that clearly communicate findings to non-technical audiences.
- Utilize statistical methods and tools to validate data and enhance model accuracy.
- Work closely with data engineers to ensure data integrity and optimize data pipelines.
- Stay abreast of emerging analytics technologies and trends to drive innovation within the team.
- Train and support team members in best practices for data analysis and visualization.
- Engage in cross-functional teams to contribute to enterprise-wide analytics initiatives.

## Skill Requirements
- Strong proficiency in data analysis programming languages such as SQL and Python.
- Experience with data visualization tools like Tableau, Power BI, or similar platforms.
- Familiarity with statistical analysis and modeling techniques.
- Understanding of data warehousing concepts and technologies.
- Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills to interact effectively with diverse teams.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, or a related field.
- Prior experience in analytics or business intelligence roles.
- Knowledge of cloud-based analytics solutions, such as AWS or Azure.
- Experience working with big data technologies (e.g., Hadoop, Spark).
- Familiarity with machine learning is a plus.
