## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and implement automated test scripts using industry-standard testing frameworks.
- Collaborate with software developers and product teams to understand application features and ensure robust test coverage.
- Conduct performance testing to identify bottlenecks and areas for optimization in software applications.
- Maintain and enhance existing automated test suites to accommodate application changes and improvements.
- Analyze test results, document defects, and work with development teams to facilitate quick resolutions.
- Participate in technical reviews and contribute to the continuous improvement of QA processes and practices.
- Provide mentorship and training to junior QA team members on automation best practices and tools.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in programming languages such as Java, Python, or C#.
- Strong experience with automation testing tools like Selenium, TestNG, or JUnit.
- Understanding of software development methodologies, including Agile and DevOps practices.
- Familiarity with version control systems such as Git or SVN.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Minimum 3 years of experience in QA automation testing.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with a keen eye for detail.
- Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, collaborative team environment.
