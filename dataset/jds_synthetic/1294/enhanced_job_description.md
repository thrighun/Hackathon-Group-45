## Job Title
Frontend Software Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop user-friendly web applications utilizing Angular for dynamic single-page applications.
- Collaborate with product managers and backend developers to integrate APIs and enhance application functionality.
- Implement responsive design principles to ensure applications function seamlessly on various devices and screen sizes.
- Conduct code reviews and provide constructive feedback to enhance team performance and code quality.
- Participate in iterative development processes, including Agile methodologies, to ensure timely project delivery.
- Actively engage in continuous learning and incorporate best practices into development processes.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum of 4 years of professional experience in frontend development using Angular.
- Strong understanding of TypeScript and modern JavaScript frameworks.
- Proficiency in state management libraries such as NgRx or Redux.
- Experience with front-end testing frameworks, such as Jasmine or Karma.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Software Development, or a related discipline.
- Exceptional analytical and debugging skills.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with RESTful services and APIs.
- Understanding of SEO principles and web performance optimization.
- Knowledge of version control systems, particularly Git.
- Experience with CI/CD processes and tools such as Jenkins or CircleCI.
