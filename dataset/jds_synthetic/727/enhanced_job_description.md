## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement firmware solutions for various embedded systems and devices.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams including product managers and hardware engineers to define firmware specifications and requirements.
- Perform extensive debugging and troubleshooting of firmware to enhance performance and ensure reliability.
- Optimize firmware code for power management and memory efficiency in embedded platforms.
- Conduct testing and validation of firmware solutions through unit tests and system-level testing.
- Stay updated on emerging technologies and trends in embedded systems to drive innovation and product enhancements.
- Create and maintain comprehensive documentation for firmware development processes and methodologies.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, or a related discipline.
- At least 3 years of experience in firmware development for embedded systems.
- Proficiency in C/C++ and an understanding of assembly languages for embedded programming.
- Knowledge of real-time operating systems (RTOS) and microcontroller architectures.
- Experience with hardware debugging tools and techniques (e.g., oscilloscopes, logic analyzers).
- Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.
- Capable of working effectively in a team-oriented environment.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with wireless communication protocols (e.g., Bluetooth, Zigbee, Wi-Fi).
- Experience with automated build systems and CI/CD pipelines.
- Knowledge of Agile methodology and participation in Agile teams is a plus.
- Relevant certifications in embedded firmware development or related technologies are advantageous.
