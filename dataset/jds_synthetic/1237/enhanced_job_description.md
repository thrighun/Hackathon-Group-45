## Job Title
Workforce Insights Analyst

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze employee engagement metrics to identify factors that influence workforce satisfaction and retention.
- Employ predictive analytics to forecast workforce needs and assess the impact of various HR initiatives.
- Develop workforce models to inform strategic planning and resource allocation in alignment with company goals.
- Collaborate with business leaders to define workforce strategies that enhance organizational performance and productivity.
- Create comprehensive reports and visualizations that communicate findings and recommendations to stakeholders.
- Monitor industry trends and best practices in workforce analytics to continuously improve HR processes and strategies.
- Conduct surveys and focus groups to gather qualitative insights and correlate them with quantitative data.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Human Resources, Analytics, Business, or a related field.
- Strong background in employee engagement analysis with demonstrated experience in HR metrics.
- Proficiency in predictive modeling and workforce analytics techniques.
- Experience in advanced data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Qlik).
- Solid understanding of statistical analysis and workforce modeling methodologies.
- Excellent problem-solving skills and the ability to derive actionable insights from complex data sets.
- Strong communication skills, capable of presenting data-driven recommendations to diverse audiences.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with HRIS systems and data management practices.
- Certifications in workforce analytics or related domains.
- Experience with programming languages for data analysis (e.g., R, Python, SQL).
