## Detailed Responsibilities
- Analyze client requirements and design tailored ServiceNow solutions for IT Asset Management and Incident Management.
- Configure and customize ServiceNow modules to enhance functionality and user experience.
- Lead the implementation of ServiceNow integrations with ITSM tools and asset management systems.
- Monitor and report on key performance metrics to ensure optimal operation of ITAM and incident processes.
- Collaborate with IT teams to gather feedback and continuously improve ServiceNow workflows.
- Provide training and support to users, enhancing their understanding and utilization of the ServiceNow platform.
- Conduct system audits and assessments to ensure compliance and data integrity.
- Stay updated with ServiceNow upgrades and new features to leverage the platform effectively for our clients.
- Foster a knowledge-sharing environment by mentoring junior consultants and engaging in community practices.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field.
- 8+ years of experience in ServiceNow configuration and implementation, with a focus on ITAM and incident management.
- Proficiency in ITSM best practices and experience in asset management methodologies.
- Strong analytical skills and the ability to translate business requirements into technical specifications.
- Experience with ServiceNow integrations involving third-party tools and systems.
- Excellent communication skills and the capability to effectively train and support end-users.
- ServiceNow Certified Implementation Specialist (CIS) or similar certifications in relevant areas.
- Knowledge of software development practices and tools would be an advantage.

## Other Requirements

- Experience in a consulting environment or working with multiple clients concurrently.
