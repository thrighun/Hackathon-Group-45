## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement cloud-based architectures tailored for enterprise-level applications.
- Collaborate with development teams to establish and enforce architecture standards and best practices.
- Optimize cloud infrastructures for performance, reliability, and cost efficiency.
- Integrate cloud services with existing on-premises systems, ensuring seamless operations.
- Assess and improve the security posture of cloud solutions alongside DevSecOps practices.
- Provide technical guidance and mentorship to development teams on cloud technologies and architectures.
- Evaluate new technologies and tools to ensure the company remains at the forefront of cloud innovation.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- 7-12 years of experience in cloud architecture and solutions design.
- Proficiency in cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
- Strong understanding of microservices architecture and container orchestration (Kubernetes, Docker).
- Knowledge of networking, security, and compliance in cloud environments.
- Familiarity with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools (Terraform, CloudFormation).
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and technical documentation skills.
- Strong communication and leadership abilities to effectively collaborate with cross-functional teams.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices.
- Relevant certifications in cloud architecture or related technologies (e.g., AWS Certified Solutions Architect, Google Cloud Professional Architect).
- Familiarity with agile and/or DevOps methodologies.
