## Job Title
QA Automation Specialist

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement automated test frameworks for mobile applications using Java and Appium.
- Develop and execute end-to-end test scenarios to guarantee quality and performance on various devices.
- Collaborate with product owners and developers to ensure alignment on testing strategies and coverage.
- Manage test documentation and results in Rally while maintaining effective communication with cross-functional teams using Azure DevOps.
- Troubleshoot issues identified during testing and propose viable solutions to development teams.
- Optimize MongoDB queries to support automated test data management.
- Contribute to the continuous improvement of testing processes and methodologies.
- Participate in sprint reviews and retrospectives to enhance the overall testing strategy.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- Proven experience in mobile application testing with Java and Appium.
- Familiarity with test management tools such as Rally and Azure DevOps.
- Strong knowledge of MongoDB and data management practices.
- Solid understanding of software testing life cycle, automation frameworks, and best practices.
- Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
- Ability to work in a collaborative and dynamic team environment.

## Other Requirements
- Industry certifications in mobile testing (e.g., Certified Agile Tester) are a plus.
- Experience in implementing CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices.
- Familiarity with cloud services and microservices architecture.
- Knowledge of performance testing and security testing tools is advantageous.
