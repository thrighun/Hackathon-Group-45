## Detailed Responsibilities
- Manage and optimize Windows Server environments, ensuring high availability and performance.
- Provide Level 2 and Level 3 technical support for server-related issues and configuration management.
- Collaborate with IT teams to assess, design, and implement robust systems architectures tailored to business needs.
- Assist in cloud migration projects and optimize cloud resource allocation as required.
- Identify system vulnerabilities, propose security measures, and oversee patch management processes.
- Engage in after-hours support to resolve critical incidents on a rotating basis.
- Develop scripts for task automation and system monitoring to enhance operational efficiency.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology or a related field.
- 4-6 years of experience in systems engineering and administration, particularly with Windows Server.
- Strong background in virtualization technologies like VMware and Hyper-V.
- Proficiency in Active Directory management and Group Policy scripting.
- Familiarity with networking concepts and protocols, including DNS and TCP/IP.
- Experience with Azure or AWS cloud services is beneficial.
- Knowledge of automation tools such as Ansible or Terraform is a plus.
- Solid understanding of backup, recovery, and disaster recovery practices.

## Other Requirements
- Previous experience in an ITIL environment is preferred.
- Relevant certifications such as MCSA or MCSE are advantageous.
- Understanding of containerization technologies like Docker is an added benefit.
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills with effective communication capabilities.
