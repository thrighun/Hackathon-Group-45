## Job Title
Senior Java Solution Architect

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the architecture and design of scalable, high-performance Java solutions tailored to client requirements.
- Collaborate closely with business analysts and product managers to translate business needs into technical specifications.
- Oversee the development of Java applications using J2EE and modern frameworks, ensuring adherence to architectural standards.
- Mentor development teams on best practices for coding, architecture, and systems design to foster a culture of excellence.
- Conduct system assessments and performance tuning to optimize application efficiency and responsiveness.
- Drive initiatives for continuous integration (CI) and continuous delivery (CD) within the development pipeline.
- Engage with stakeholders to define and refine application requirements, ensuring alignment with business objectives.
- Evaluate and recommend emerging technologies that can enhance product offerings and improve development processes.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Minimum of 7 years of experience in Java development with a focus on architecture and design.
- Deep knowledge of Spring Framework, including Spring Boot and Spring Cloud.
- Proficiency in microservices architecture and RESTful API development.
- Strong understanding of cloud computing platforms, specifically AWS or Azure.
- Familiarity with DevOps practices and tools, including Jenkins, Docker, and Kubernetes.
- Excellent analytical skills and a proven track record in problem-solving.
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills to work effectively with diverse teams.
- Experience with Agile methodologies and frameworks such as Scrum or Kanban.

## Other Requirements
- Proven experience with system integration challenges and solutions.
- Knowledge of security best practices for web applications.
- Certification in AWS, Java, or related technologies is highly desirable.
- Experience with NoSQL databases and caching solutions is a plus.
