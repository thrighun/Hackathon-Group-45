## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain full stack web applications using React, Node.js, and Tailwind CSS.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define project requirements and scope, ensuring alignment with business objectives.
- Implement user interface components and back-end services, ensuring optimal performance and responsiveness.
- Conduct code reviews and contribute to maintaining code quality through automated testing and continuous integration.
- Diagnose and resolve technical challenges in both front-end and back-end systems.
- Create and maintain detailed documentation of application architecture, code, and development processes.
- Engage in regular team meetings and Agile ceremonies to discuss project progress and identify potential improvements.
- Stay updated with industry trends and best practices in web development, integrating new techniques into our projects.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field.
- 4+ years of experience in full stack web development, specifically with React.js and Node.js.
- Solid understanding of Tailwind CSS and the utility-first CSS approach.
- Proficiency in JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, and RESTful API integration.
- Familiarity with databases (SQL or NoSQL) and server-side frameworks.
- Experience with version control tools, particularly Git.
- Strong problem-solving skills, with the ability to think critically and creatively.
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills for effective collaboration.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with cloud-based services (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud).
- Experience with Agile methodologies and familiarity with tools such as Jira or Trello.
- Knowledge of containerization technologies (Docker) is a plus.
- Previous experience working in a team-oriented environment, preferably in a startup or fast-paced organization.
