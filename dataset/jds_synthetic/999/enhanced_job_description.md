## Job Title
Automotive Systems Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Global Automotive Engineering Firm

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Leverage SolidWorks or Autodesk Inventor for 3D CAD modeling, assembly, and comprehensive design validation processes.
- Engage in the design and integration of electrical wiring systems for automotive applications, ensuring compliance with industry standards.
- Collaborate on the development of Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) and related infotainment products.
- Conduct thorough manufacturing process evaluations, including CNC machining, additive manufacturing, and injection molding techniques.
- Perform structural and thermal simulations using Finite Element Analysis (FEA) and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) to validate design performance.
- Work closely with other engineering disciplines to ensure seamless integration of systems within automotive products.
- Generate and review technical documentation to support design specifications and regulatory compliance.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in SolidWorks or Autodesk Inventor.
- Extensive experience in 3D CAD modeling, assembly, and design validation techniques.
- Familiarity with electrical wiring and integration design for automotive applications.
- Knowledge of Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) and infotainment product design.
- Strong grasp of manufacturing processes, including CNC machining and injection molding.
- Experience with design simulations using FEA and CFD tools.
- Bachelor's degree in Electrical or Mechanical Engineering or a related field.

## Other Requirements
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail.
- Excellent interpersonal and communication abilities for cross-disciplinary collaboration.
- Experience with project management tools and methodologies is a plus.
