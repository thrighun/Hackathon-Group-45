## Job Title
Electrical Systems Design Engineer - Automotive

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Innovative Automotive Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Utilize SolidWorks or Siemens NX for 3D CAD modeling, assembly design, and prototype development of automotive electrical systems.
- Design and develop wiring harnesses, control modules, and other electrical components to optimize vehicle performance and efficiency.
- Apply knowledge of thermal systems and fluid dynamics to ensure thermal management within electrical systems.
- Leverage familiarity with advanced manufacturing techniques such as additive manufacturing, electroplating, and stamping in product design.
- Implement Quality Assurance processes and adhere to automotive standards to ensure compliance and reliability in electrical systems.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate electrical systems with mechanical components and overall vehicle design.
- Drive continuous improvement in design methodologies to enhance system performance and reduce production costs.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in SolidWorks or Siemens NX.
- Strong background in 3D CAD modeling, assembly design, and prototype development.
- Knowledge of thermal systems and fluid dynamics is preferred.
- Experience in the design of automotive electrical systems, including wiring harness and control modules.
- Familiarity with advanced manufacturing techniques such as additive manufacturing and stamping.
- Understanding of Quality Assurance processes and automotive standards.
- Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering or a related field.
- Excellent analytical skills and a proactive approach to problem-solving.

## Other Requirements
- Relevant certifications in electrical design or automotive engineering.
- Experience with simulation software and tools related to electrical systems.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills for effective collaboration.
