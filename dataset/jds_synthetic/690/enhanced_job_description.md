## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect, implement, and optimize AWS cloud infrastructures that cater to the scalability and security needs of healthcare applications.
- Work closely with pharmaceutical and biotechnology partners to translate business requirements into technical specifications and design optimal cloud solutions.
- Develop strategies for data integration and management to enhance research capabilities while ensuring compliance with healthcare regulations, such as FDA and HIPAA.
- Lead cloud migration projects, leveraging AWS tools to ensure minimal disruption to ongoing operations and maximum performance efficiency.
- Produce comprehensive architectural documentation and provide training sessions for technical teams and stakeholders on best practices and cloud governance.

- Minimum of 8+ years of experience in AWS solutions architecture, with a focus on the healthcare domain.
- In-depth knowledge of AWS services, including EC2, RDS, IAM, CloudTrail, and VPC configuration.
- Demonstrated experience implementing cloud solutions that are compliant with healthcare regulations and industry standards.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field; AWS Solutions Architect certification is preferred.
- Strong analytical skills and the ability to communicate complex technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

## Other Requirements

- Experience in data analytics and reporting solutions within a healthcare context.
- Strong leadership qualities and the capability to mentor junior team members and lead cross-functional initiatives.
