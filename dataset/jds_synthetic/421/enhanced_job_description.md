## Detailed Responsibilities
- Evaluate cloud environments for compliance with regulatory frameworks such as GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2.
- Analyze risks associated with cloud services and develop effective mitigation strategies.
- Collaborate with IT and security teams to implement compliance controls and ensure policies are adhered to.
- Conduct regular compliance audits and assessments to identify gaps and provide actionable recommendations.
- Maintain documentation related to compliance processes, including control assessments and audit results.
- Deliver training sessions and resources to teams on compliance best practices and regulatory requirements.
- Stay informed of changes in regulations and standards affecting cloud compliance and adjust practices accordingly.

## Skill Requirements
- A minimum of 8 years of experience in compliance and risk management, particularly in cloud environments.
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Information Technology, Compliance, or a related field.
- Relevant certifications such as Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) or Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control (CRISC).
- Strong understanding of regulatory requirements and industry standards affecting cloud compliance.
- Excellent analytical skills to assess complex compliance risks and recommend solutions.
- Strong communication skills for effective collaboration with diverse teams and stakeholders.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with cloud platforms such as AWS or Google Cloud is advantageous.
- Experience with compliance automation tools and frameworks.
- Understanding of data privacy principles and their implications for cloud services.
