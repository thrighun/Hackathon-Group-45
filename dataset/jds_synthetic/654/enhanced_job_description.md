## Job Title
Application Security Specialist

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Retail

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and establish secure coding standards for all application development projects.
- Perform in-depth security assessments and code reviews to identify and remediate potential vulnerabilities.
- Work closely with product management and engineering teams to integrate security features into product design and development.
- Develop and maintain automated security testing tools to streamline the identification of security issues in applications.
- Investigate and respond to security incidents related to application vulnerabilities, ensuring timely resolution and reporting.
- Drive awareness and training programs for development teams on secure coding practices and emerging threats.
- Regularly report security findings and metrics to leadership, offering insights on risk management and improvement areas.

## Skill Requirements
- A minimum of 8 years of experience in application development with a strong emphasis on security practices.
- Expertise in secure coding languages and frameworks (e.g., Java, .NET, OWASP).
- Familiarity with application security testing tools (e.g., SAST, DAST) and methodologies.
- Strong grasp of regulatory compliance and security standards relevant to web applications.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline.
- Relevant certifications such as OWASP, CSSLP, or GIAC are preferred.
- Exceptional analytical skills with a focus on detail-oriented problem solving.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in the retail sector or familiarity with mobile commerce applications is a bonus.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with an ability to work effectively across multidisciplinary teams.
- Understanding of Agile methodologies and coordination with CI/CD pipeline security practices.
