## Job Title
Senior Data Engineer – AI Systems & Big Data Analytics

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to build and maintain scalable data pipelines that cater to AI and machine learning initiatives.
- Design and develop ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes for large datasets, utilizing technologies such as Apache Spark and Hadoop.
- Work closely with data scientists to ensure availability and accessibility of clean, organized data for training and deploying AI models.
- Optimize data architectures for performance and scalability, ensuring that data ingestion and processing workflows are efficient.
- Troubleshoot data-related issues and implement solutions promptly to maintain system integrity.
- Utilize Python and SQL extensively in building data models and data warehouses to support analysis and reporting.
- Participate in code reviews, advocating for best practices in data governance, security, and quality assurance.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Proven experience in data engineering with strong proficiency in Python and SQL.
- Hands-on experience with big data technologies such as Apache Spark, Hadoop, or similar frameworks.
- Familiarity with cloud-based data solutions (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and data warehousing solutions (e.g., Snowflake, Redshift).
- Strong understanding of data pipeline architecture and data modeling concepts.
- Proficient in version control systems (e.g., Git) and development workflows.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with machine learning frameworks or libraries (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch) is a plus.
- Knowledge of data governance methodologies and best practices in data quality.
- Familiarity with containerization technologies (e.g., Docker) and orchestration tools (e.g., Kubernetes).
- Previous experience working in agile development teams.
- Active involvement in data engineering or AI community contributions.
