## Detailed Responsibilities
- Build and maintain mobile-first web applications utilizing React, focusing on performance and user experience.
- Collaborate with product managers and designers to create visually engaging and intuitive interfaces.
- Implement state management solutions and optimize application performance.
- Integrate payment gateways and content management systems to enhance ecommerce functionality.
- Participate in agile development processes, including sprint planning and code reviews.
- Collaborate closely with backend development teams to create and maintain robust APIs.
- Stay abreast of emerging technologies and trends in the ecommerce landscape to foster innovative solutions.

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- At least 4 years of experience with front-end technologies, specifically React.
- Strong knowledge of mobile web development practices and responsive design.
- Proficient in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern web technologies.
- Experience with state management libraries such as Redux or MobX.
- Familiarity with RESTful APIs and asynchronous programming.
- Good understanding of version control systems, primarily Git.
- Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.

## Other Requirements
