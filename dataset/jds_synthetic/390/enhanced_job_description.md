## Job Title
Firmware Engineer for IoT Devices

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Internet of Things (IoT) Solutions Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and optimize firmware for a variety of IoT devices using C and Python programming languages.
- Implement software solutions on Linux-based platforms to enhance device functionality and connectivity.
- Collaborate with hardware engineers to ensure seamless integration of firmware with device hardware.
- Conduct rigorous testing and validation of firmware to guarantee reliability and performance in real-world applications.
- Mentor and support junior engineers in firmware development best practices and troubleshooting techniques.
- Research and incorporate new technologies and methodologies relevant to the IoT landscape.
- Create and maintain comprehensive technical documentation for firmware design and implementation.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a similar technical field.
- 4-6 years of experience in firmware development for embedded systems, particularly in IoT applications.
- Proficient in C and Python programming languages, with experience in Linux environments.
- Strong understanding of communication protocols used in IoT (e.g., MQTT, CoAP, HTTP).
- Excellent debugging skills and experience with hardware-software interaction.
- Strong analytical skills with a focus on problem-solving and attention to detail.
- Proven ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with cloud platforms for IoT device management and data collection.
- Familiarity with development tools such as Docker and CI/CD pipelines.
- Knowledge of security practices in IoT firmware development.
- Certifications in IoT or embedded systems are a plus.
