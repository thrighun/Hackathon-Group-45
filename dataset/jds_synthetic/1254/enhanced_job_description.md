## Job Title
Web Application Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology / Technology Solutions

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and maintain full-stack web applications leveraging modern frameworks like Angular or Vue.js in conjunction with Node.js or Django for backend services.
- Collaborate with product owners, designers, and QA engineers to define application requirements and translate them into functional features.
- Optimize applications for maximum speed and scalability while ensuring a high-quality user experience across various devices and platforms.
- Conduct thorough testing and debugging of applications to identify and resolve issues efficiently.
- Implement and manage database solutions, ensuring data integrity and performance through efficient query design.
- Keep abreast of web development trends and emerging technologies, integrating them into the development process to enhance products.
- Contribute to code quality and maintainability by performing regular code reviews and adhering to best practices.
- Document application architecture, workflows, and API specifications for team collaboration and future reference.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum 3 years of experience in web application development, with a focus on both frontend and backend technologies.
- Proficient in JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML5, and CSS3, along with experience in frameworks like Angular, Vue.js, or similar.
- Solid understanding of server-side languages such as Node.js, Python, or Ruby.
- Experience with database management systems (SQL or NoSQL) and knowledge of ORM frameworks.
- Familiarity with API development and integration, specifically RESTful services.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline (or equivalent experience).
- Strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and the ability to work independently and as part of a team.

## Other Requirements
- Knowledge of cloud services (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud) and containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes) is preferred.
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies and tools such as Jira or Trello.
- Previous experience in DevOps practices and CI/CD pipeline development is a plus.
- Strong communication skills, with a proven ability to collaborate effectively with diverse teams and stakeholders.
