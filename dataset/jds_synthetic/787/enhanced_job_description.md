## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement firmware for a range of embedded devices, focusing on optimizing performance and resource utilization.
- Coordinate with hardware engineers to ensure effective integration of firmware with physical components, addressing any compatibility issues.
- Perform comprehensive testing and debugging of firmware to ensure reliability and functionality in diverse environments.
- Actively contribute to team discussions on design modifications and technical challenges, offering innovative solutions to enhance product performance.
- Document all firmware development processes, specifications, and updates to facilitate knowledge sharing and compliance with industry standards.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- At least 3 years of experience in firmware development for embedded systems, with a strong focus on low-level programming.
- Proficient in programming languages such as C/C++ and Assembly, with a solid understanding of embedded software design principles.
- Experience with software debugging tools and methodologies relevant to firmware development.
- Knowledge of communication protocols (e.g., I2C, SPI, UART) and system-level interactions in embedded applications.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with real-time operating systems (RTOS) and their implementation in firmware solutions.
- Understanding of version control systems like Git and Agile methodologies for collaborative development.
- Experience with hardware design using tools such as Altium or similar PCB design software is an advantage.
- Relevant certifications in firmware development or embedded systems are highly desirable.
