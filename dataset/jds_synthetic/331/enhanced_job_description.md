## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and deploy custom applications and solutions on the Salesforce platform to meet organizational needs.
- Collaborate with business analysts and stakeholders to gather and document technical requirements and functional specifications.
- Create and manage Salesforce workflows, process builders, and custom objects to optimize user experience and operational workflows.
- Integrate Salesforce with third-party systems and APIs, ensuring data integrity and effective communication across platforms.
- Conduct thorough testing of Salesforce solutions and troubleshoot issues as they arise.
- Maintain comprehensive documentation for the developed solutions, including technical manuals and user guides.
- Adhere to best practices in Salesforce development and ensure compliance with organizational standards.
- Participate in code reviews and provide mentorship to junior developers on Salesforce best practices.
- Contribute to Agile development processes, including sprint planning, stand-ups, and retrospectives.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Proven experience in Salesforce development and administration, with a strong understanding of the Salesforce platform architecture.
- Proficiency in Apex, Visualforce, Lightning Components, and Salesforce APIs.
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies and collaborative development environments.
- Salesforce Certified Platform Developer I or II certifications are required.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, paired with excellent communication abilities.
- Experience with data management and data migration tools in Salesforce.

## Other Requirements
- Salesforce Certified Administrator or Advanced Administrator certifications preferred.
- Experience in implementing Salesforce integrations using middleware platforms like MuleSoft is a plus.
- Prior experience in large-scale enterprise environments and understanding of business process automation.
- Knowledge of additional programming languages (e.g., Java, JavaScript) is a plus.
- Strong attention to detail and ability to document technical processes effectively.
