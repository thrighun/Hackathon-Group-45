## Job Title
Embedded Software Engineer - IoT Focus

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
IoT Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain firmware for Internet of Things (IoT) applications across various devices.
- Collaborate with hardware engineers to ensure effective system integration and functionality.
- Implement security protocols and solutions to safeguard IoT devices against vulnerabilities.
- Perform thorough testing and validation of software to guarantee reliability and performance in real-world applications.
- Contribute to the full software development lifecycle, from requirement analysis to deployment.
- Optimize firmware for low-power consumption and efficiency in resource-constrained environments.
- Document processes, designs, and code for team use and future reference.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- 3-6 years of experience in embedded software development, particularly for IoT applications.
- Proficient in programming languages such as C/C++ and familiar with embedded C standards.
- Strong knowledge of wireless communication protocols (e.g., MQTT, Bluetooth, Zigbee).
- Experience with low-power design techniques and real-time constraints.
- Excellent analytical skills and a systematic approach to troubleshooting.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with cloud computing platforms and services (AWS, Azure) for IoT applications.
- Knowledge of version control systems like Git and continuous integration tools.
- Understanding of software security principles and best practices for IoT devices.
- Strong communication skills to collaborate with cross-functional teams effectively.
