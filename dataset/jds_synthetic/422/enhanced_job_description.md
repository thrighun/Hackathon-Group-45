## Job Title
Electrical Design Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Automotive Electronics Manufacturer

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Utilize Siemens NX or SolidWorks for 3D CAD modeling, assembly design, and surface modeling of automotive electrical systems.
- Design and develop innovative electrical and electronic systems for automobile applications, including infotainment units, battery management systems, lighting, and ADAS features.
- Implement cable management and harness design strategies to optimize layout and functionality.
- Ensure compliance with industry standards and regulations pertaining to automotive electronics and safety.
- Collaborate closely with hardware and software engineers to integrate electronic components and PCB designs into overall vehicle architecture.
- Prepare and maintain comprehensive technical documentation for design processes and specifications.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in Siemens NX or SolidWorks.
- Strong experience in 3D CAD modeling, assembly design, and surface modeling.
- Background in designing electrical and electronic systems in the automotive industry.
- Understanding of PCB design and electronic component integration.
- Familiarity with automotive industry standards and safety regulations.
- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering or a related field.
- Certifications in CAD software and electronic design tools are a plus.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with cable management and harness design is advantageous.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Strong communication skills and ability to work in a team-oriented environment.
