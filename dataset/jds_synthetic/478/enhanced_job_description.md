## Detailed Responsibilities
- Conduct thorough inspections and testing of software products to ensure conformity with quality standards.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to troubleshoot and resolve quality issues encountered during the testing process.
- Document test results, prepare inspection reports, and communicate findings to stakeholders.
- Develop and implement quality control processes and procedures to enhance our testing framework.
- Participate in user acceptance testing (UAT) sessions and gather feedback for continuous improvement.
- Assist in training team members on quality assurance best practices and methodologies.
- Monitor and analyze trends in defects to identify areas for product improvement.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- Proven experience as a QC Analyst or in a similar role.
- Strong understanding of quality control methodologies and best practices.
- Familiarity with software testing tools and tracking systems (e.g., JIRA, TestRail).
- Excellent analytical skills and attention to detail.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- Previous experience in an Agile or DevOps environment is a plus.
- Knowledge of scripting languages (e.g., JavaScript, Ruby) for automated testing.
- Relevant certifications in quality control or software testing (e.g., CSTE, ASTQB).
