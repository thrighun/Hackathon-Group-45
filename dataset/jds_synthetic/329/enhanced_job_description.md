## Job Title
Oracle SQL Database Administrator

## Location
Pune/Madurai/Bengaluru/Chennai/NOIDA

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Monitor and optimize database performance to ensure high availability and reliability of systems.
- Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams to gather requirements and translate them into actionable technical specifications.
- Participate in the design and implementation of new Oracle database solutions, while evaluating existing systems for areas of improvement.
- Adhere to industry best practices regarding database security and compliance to protect sensitive data.
- Lead troubleshooting efforts for database-related issues, ensuring timely and effective resolutions.
- Communicate regularly with stakeholders to provide updates on database health and gather feedback for improvements.
- Be proactive in identifying and implementing continuous improvement initiatives related to database management.
- Manage multiple projects simultaneously while effectively prioritizing tasks to meet deadlines.
- Be prepared to work flexible hours, including weekends and holidays, as necessary.

## Skill Requirements
- 3-7 years of proficiency in Oracle SQL and data analytics.
- Strong understanding of database management and performance tuning techniques.
- Familiarity with database security best practices and compliance regulations.
- Excellent problem-solving skills and analytical thinking.
- Demonstrated ability to communicate effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Experience working in a fast-paced, technology-driven environment is a plus.

## Other Requirements
- Prior experience in an IT service management environment is advantageous but not mandatory.
