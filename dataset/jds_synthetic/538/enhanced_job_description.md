## Job Title
AI Product Manager

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Define the product vision and strategy for AI-based products, ensuring alignment with business goals.
- Collaborate with engineering, design, and marketing teams to drive the product development lifecycle from ideation to launch.
- Conduct market research and competitive analysis to identify trends and opportunities in AI technology.
- Develop and prioritize product feature roadmaps based on customer feedback and business needs.
- Act as a liaison between technical teams and stakeholders, translating requirements into functional specifications.
- Monitor product performance and gather user feedback to continuously improve AI solutions.
- Lead cross-functional teams in Agile methodologies to deliver high-quality AI products on time.
- Ensure that all AI products comply with industry regulations and ethical standards.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related field.
- Minimum of 6 years of experience in product management, with at least 2 years focusing on AI/ML products.
- Strong understanding of product development processes and methodologies.
- Proficiency in data analysis tools and techniques to drive product decisions.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills to work with diverse teams and stakeholders.
- Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously in a fast-paced environment.
- Experience in user experience (UX) design principles related to AI solutions.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with AI technologies, machine learning frameworks, and data management practices.
- Knowledge of regulatory standards within the AI industry.
- Previous experience working in an Agile product management role.
- Understanding of customer needs and experience in user-centered design practices.
