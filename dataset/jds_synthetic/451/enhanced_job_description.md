## Job Title
AI Solutions Architect – Machine Learning & Data Engineering

## Location
Remote

## Client Industry
Technology

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead the design and architecture of AI solutions that leverage machine learning algorithms, ensuring alignment with business objectives and scalability.
- Collaborate with data engineering teams to create robust data pipelines that facilitate machine learning model training and deployment.
- Integrate cloud technologies to optimize the performance and accessibility of AI applications, utilizing platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather requirements, assess feasibility, and deliver tailored AI solutions that enhance user experience.
- Develop and maintain documentation for AI solution architectures, data workflows, and model deployment processes.
- Conduct performance tuning and optimization of models in production environments, ensuring high accuracy and efficiency.
- Mentor junior engineers and contribute to best practices in software development, machine learning, and data engineering.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field.
- Extensive experience in architecting AI solutions, with a strong background in machine learning and statistical modeling.
- Proficiency in Python and familiarity with machine learning libraries such as TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Strong understanding of data engineering principles, including ETL processes and data warehousing.
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and familiarity with serverless architecture.
- Knowledge of containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes) for deploying scalable applications.
- Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and communication skills.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with MLOps and deploying machine learning models in production settings.
- Familiarity with additional programming languages (e.g., R, Java) is advantageous.
- Understanding of agile methodologies and software development lifecycles.
- Contributions to relevant open-source projects or data science communities are a plus.
- Proven ability to manage multiple projects and collaborate with diverse teams.
