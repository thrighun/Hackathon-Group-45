- Analyze large datasets to identify trends, patterns, and insights that can drive business strategies.
- Collaborate with data engineers and software developers to ensure data quality and accessibility.
- Design and develop reports and dashboards that communicate findings effectively to stakeholders.
- Conduct data validation and cleaning to maintain high data integrity.
- Participate in cross-departmental projects to enhance data-driven decision-making across the organization.

- Bachelor’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or a related field.
- Proven experience in data analysis, statistical modeling, and data visualization tools.
- Proficiency in data manipulation and analysis using SQL, Python, or R.
- Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills, with a keen attention to detail.
- Excellent interpersonal and communication skills to effectively present findings.

## Other Requirements

- Familiarity with machine learning concepts and methodologies.
- Knowledge of data governance and data protection regulations.
