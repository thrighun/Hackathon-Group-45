## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and architect microservices-based solutions for enterprise-level applications, utilizing cloud platforms to enhance flexibility and scalability.
- Collaborate with development and DevOps teams to establish best practices for API development, deployment, and maintenance, ensuring high-quality standards are met.
- Conduct architectural reviews and provide expert guidance on integrating microservices with existing systems and third-party services.
- Lead the adoption of cloud-native technologies, including container orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes) and serverless computing, to optimize application performance.
- Stay abreast of industry trends and emerging technologies, sharing insights with teams and shaping the technical direction of projects.
- Mentor technical teams on microservices architecture principles, cloud technologies, and agile development methodologies to foster a culture of continuous improvement.
- Establish guidelines for CI/CD pipelines and automation practices to streamline application deployment and enhance operational efficiency.

## Skill Requirements
- Proven expertise in microservices architecture and design patterns, with hands-on experience in implementing cloud-based solutions.
- Strong knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and experience with containerization and orchestration technologies (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Familiarity with API management and service mesh tools to facilitate effective service communication.
- Comprehensive understanding of CI/CD processes and tools to support automated testing and deployment.
- Experience with agile methodologies and collaborative team environments.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities, with a strong focus on delivering high-quality, maintainable solutions.
- Strong communication skills to effectively convey technical concepts to diverse stakeholders and facilitate team collaboration.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Relevant certifications in cloud technologies, microservices, or architecture frameworks are considered an advantage.
- Previous experience in a solutions architect role, driving technical innovation and best practices in a fast-paced environment.
