- Collaborate with senior systems analysts to identify and document business requirements for system enhancements.
- Provide technical support to internal teams, troubleshooting issues in UNIX/Linux environments and ensuring timely resolutions.
- Conduct thorough analyses of existing data flows and procedures to propose and implement performance improvements.
- Engage in system testing and quality assurance processes to validate software updates and new functionalities.

- Solid understanding of UNIX/Linux command-line interfaces and system management.
- Proficiency in SQL for data manipulation and querying.
- Basic knowledge of software development life cycle (SDLC) and project management methodologies.

## Other Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field is preferred.
- Previous internship or experience in systems analysis, technical support, or IT operations is advantageous.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail and a methodical approach to tasks.
