## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain comprehensive data privacy policies and procedures to ensure compliance with international and local regulations, including GDPR and CCPA.
- Conduct routine audits and assessments to evaluate the effectiveness of data privacy practices, identifying gaps and proposing necessary improvements.
- Collaborate closely with legal and IT departments to ensure that data handling protocols are in full alignment with applicable regulations and organizational policies.
- Act as a liaison for all data privacy inquiries and concerns from internal teams and external partners, providing expert guidance and support.
- Design and deliver impactful training programs to educate employees about data privacy obligations, best practices, and the importance of compliance.
- Monitor and analyze emerging trends in data privacy legislation and industry standards, providing management with actionable insights and updates.
- Engage in cross-functional projects to incorporate data privacy requirements into new products, services, and technologies, ensuring privacy by design.

## Skill Requirements
- In-depth knowledge of data protection laws and regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills

## Other Requirements
- Proven ability to work collaboratively in a multi-disciplinary team.
- Experience in conducting compliance assessments and audits in a corporate setting.
- Commitment to ongoing professional development in the fields of data protection and compliance.
