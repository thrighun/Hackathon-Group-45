## Detailed Responsibilities
- Architect scalable and resilient cloud solutions tailored to business needs.
- Lead the development of cloud strategy and roadmap in collaboration with stakeholders.
- Evaluate and select appropriate cloud service providers and technologies.
- Conduct risk assessments and enforce cloud governance policies.
- Mentor and guide development teams in best practices for cloud architecture.
- Implement monitoring tools and metrics to ensure service reliability and availability.
- Research and apply emerging technologies to improve cloud services.

## Skill Requirements
- Extensive experience designing cloud-based architectures on platforms like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
- In-depth knowledge of cloud-native application design, microservices, and APIs.
- Familiarity with infrastructure as code (IaC) tools such as Terraform or CloudFormation.
- Strong programming skills in languages such as JavaScript, Ruby, or Go.
- Understanding of data storage solutions and their implementation in the cloud.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with compliance frameworks relevant to cloud deployments.
- Understanding of cloud cost management and optimization strategies.
- Ability to translate business requirements into technical specifications.
