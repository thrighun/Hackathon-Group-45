## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and develop innovative AI-driven applications that prioritize user experience, leveraging cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP).
- Work closely with product designers, UX researchers, and business analysts to ensure AI integrations seamlessly align with customer needs and behaviors.
- Manage deployment and optimization of AI models within cloud environments, ensuring scalability and efficiency of applications.
- Collaborate with front-end and back-end developers to create cohesive full-stack solutions that incorporate machine learning features.
- Implement best practices for user interface design, ensuring accessibility and responsiveness across multiple devices.
- Lead workshops and training sessions for internal teams on integrating AI functionalities into existing applications.
- Stay informed about the latest developments in AI and UX/UI best practices, advocating for continuous improvement within the team.
- Participate in project estimation, planning, and scoping meetings, providing expert input on the feasibility of AI components.

- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Human-Computer Interaction, or a related field.
- 5+ years of experience in software development, with a focus on AI applications and user interface design.

- Familiarity with machine learning libraries (TensorFlow, PyTorch) and deployment practices.
- Knowledge of UI/UX principles, wireframing tools (Figma, Sketch), and user testing methodologies.
- Experience working in Agile environments, collaborating with cross-functional teams.
- Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to communicate complex concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
