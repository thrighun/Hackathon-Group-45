## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop, enhance, and maintain user-friendly web applications using React.js and related technologies.
- Work collaboratively with UX/UI designers to implement visually appealing and intuitive interfaces.
- Optimize application performance and ensure high levels of responsiveness and accessibility.
- Debug and resolve issues proactively, ensuring a seamless user experience.
- Write reusable, testable, and efficient code while adhering to best practices.
- Participate in code discussions and reviews, offering constructive feedback and learning opportunities for team members.
- Keep abreast of emerging trends in front-end development and suggest potential improvements.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline.
- 3-6 years of experience in front-end development, particularly with React.js.
- Strong skills in JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, and familiarity with modern JavaScript frameworks.
- Experience integrating with RESTful APIs and understanding of state management tools like Redux or Context API.
- Solid grasp of responsive design principles and cross-browser compatibility.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities with keen attention to detail.
- Proven ability to work well in a dynamic, team-oriented environment.

## Other Requirements
- Familiarity with Agile/Scrum development methodologies.
- Experience with version control systems, particularly Git.
- Knowledge of build tools like Webpack or Babel.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills for effective collaboration with cross-functional teams.
