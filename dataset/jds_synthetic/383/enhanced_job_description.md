## Job Title
Visual Content Creator

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and produce high-quality video content for social media, websites, and internal communications that captivates and informs the target audience.
- Collaborate with marketing and product teams to create compelling narratives that highlight the brand's offerings through visual storytelling.
- Conceptualize and design infographics and animations to enhance engagement and convey complex information clearly.
- Edit and refine footage to ensure a polished final product that meets project standards and deadlines.
- Remain current with industry trends to bring fresh ideas and innovative techniques to the visual content strategy.
- Participate in brainstorming sessions and contribute creative solutions to marketing campaigns and initiatives.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficiency in video editing software (e.g., Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro).
- Strong creative and storytelling abilities with a keen eye for visuals.
- Excellent communication skills and a collaborative mindset.
- Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously while adhering to timelines.
- Bachelor’s degree in Film Production, Multimedia Arts, or a related field.
- A portfolio showcasing video projects and visual content work.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with graphic design software (e.g., Adobe Photoshop, After Effects) is a plus.
- Knowledge of social media platforms and their specific content requirements.
- Familiarity with lighting and sound equipment for video production.
