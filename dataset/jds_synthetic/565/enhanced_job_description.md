- Design and implement server-side applications using Node.js and Express in a microservices architecture.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand and translate business requirements into technical designs.
- Develop and maintain GraphQL APIs for efficient data retrieval and integration with frontend applications.
- Ensure high performance, security, and responsiveness of applications through code optimization and best practices.
- Conduct code reviews and provide constructive feedback to enhance code quality and performance.
- Mentor and guide junior software engineers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement.
- Manage and maintain CI/CD pipelines to streamline deployment processes.
- Participate in Agile ceremonies, contributing to sprint planning and retrospective meetings.

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- 5-7 years of professional experience in Node.js development.
- Strong experience in building microservices and developing GraphQL APIs.
- Familiarity with frontend technologies such as React or Vue.js is a plus.
- Experience with database management systems, including SQL and NoSQL options.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities.
- Strong communication skills, both verbal and written, and ability to work in a team-oriented environment.

## Other Requirements

- Understanding of Agile/Scrum methodologies.
