## Detailed Responsibilities
- Design and implement user-friendly web interfaces using Vue.js, ensuring high performance and responsiveness.
- Collaborate closely with product designers, back-end engineers, and stakeholders to understand requirements and develop effective technical solutions.
- Write clean, efficient, and maintainable code by adhering to coding standards and best practices.
- Integrate with backend services through RESTful APIs to provide a cohesive experience for end-users.
- Participate in regular code reviews, and provide constructive feedback to peers to promote knowledge sharing and continuous improvement.
- Optimize applications for scalability and speed, ensuring a smooth user experience across various devices and browsers.
- Stay up-to-date with the latest developments in Vue.js and front-end technologies, and share insights with the team.
- Contribute to technical documentation covering software architecture, components, and new features.
- Ensure best practices in security and data protection are followed during the development process.

## Skill Requirements
- Minimum 2 years of professional experience in front-end development with a focus on Vue.js.
- Proficiency in JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, and CSS3, with a solid understanding of Vue.js frameworks and methodologies.
- Experience using state management libraries (such as Vuex) and Vue Router.
- Familiarity with RESTful APIs and modern front-end build tools (such as Webpack, Babel, etc.).
- Understanding of responsive design principles and cross-browser compatibility challenges.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or equivalent practical experience.
- Strong knowledge of version control systems, preferably Git.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, paired with effective communication abilities.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with TypeScript is advantageous.
- Knowledge of testing libraries specific to Vue.js, like Vue Test Utils or Jest.
- Familiarity with Agile/Scrum development methodologies.
- Exposure to cloud services (AWS, Azure, or GCP) and familiarity with CI/CD best practices is a plus.
- Previous experience in a collaborative, fast-paced development environment is beneficial.
