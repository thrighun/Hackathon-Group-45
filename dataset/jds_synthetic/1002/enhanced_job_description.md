## Detailed Responsibilities
- Lead a team of design professionals in crafting user-friendly product interfaces utilizing UX/UI practices combined with CAD design methodologies.
- Collaborate with diverse teams, including engineering and product development, to gather insights and define product requirements that enhance user experience.
- Conduct thorough user research and usability testing to inform design decisions and implement improvements based on user feedback.
- Provide training and mentorship to junior designers, fostering skill development and creative thinking within the team.
- Research and incorporate current design trends, tools, and technologies to elevate design outcomes and processes.
- Organize and facilitate design sprints and brainstorming sessions to foster collaborative innovation and rapid prototyping.
- Document design workflows, decisions, and project milestones to ensure transparency and alignment with business objectives.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in UX/UI design principles with a portfolio showcasing impactful user-centered designs.
- Strong CAD design skills to translate technical requirements into visual representations.
- Solid understanding of HTML/CSS for effective design implementation on digital platforms.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with a focus on continuous improvement.
- Outstanding communication and interpersonal abilities to work effectively within cross-functional teams.
- Strong attention to detail and a passion for aesthetic quality in design.
- Bachelor’s degree in Design, Human-Computer Interaction, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience).

## Other Requirements
- Experience with agile methodologies and iterative design processes.
- Familiarity with design prototyping and collaboration tools such as Figma, Sketch, or Adobe XD.
- Previous experience in leading design teams and managing multiple projects.
- Strong organizational skills to manage timelines and project deliverables effectively.
