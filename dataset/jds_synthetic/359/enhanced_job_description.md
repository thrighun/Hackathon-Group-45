## Detailed Responsibilities
- Serve as the primary business liaison to gather and document business requirements for SAP implementations.
- Conduct workshops with stakeholders to articulate business needs and gather feedback for ongoing projects.
- Collaborate with technical teams to translate functional specifications into detailed SAP solutions across various modules.
- Facilitate user acceptance testing (UAT) to ensure that developed solutions meet business requirements.
- Develop and deliver training sessions and materials to support end-user adoption of SAP functionalities.
- Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and provide analysis to assist in strategic decision-making.
- Stay current with SAP trends and emerging technologies to provide innovative solutions to business challenges.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Computer Science, or a related field.
- 3+ years of experience in business analysis or a related role within the SAP ecosystem.
- Familiarity with SAP HCM, CRM, and SCM modules and their functionalities.
- Excellent analytical skills with experience in creating process maps and documentation.
- Strong communication skills, both verbal and written, enabling effective stakeholder interaction.
- Proficiency in data analysis and reporting tools.

## Other Requirements
- Experience with Agile project management methodologies is preferred.
- Knowledge of SAP Fiori and its applications in improving user experience is a plus.
- Relevant SAP certifications are advantageous but not required.
- Ability to manage multiple projects and stakeholders simultaneously while maintaining attention to detail.
