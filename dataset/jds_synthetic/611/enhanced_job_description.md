## Job Title
Firmware Engineer

## Location
N/A

## Client Industry
Information Technology Services / Multinational Technology Provider

## Detailed Responsibilities
- Develop and implement firmware solutions for a variety of embedded devices, ensuring optimal performance and reliability.
- Collaborate closely with hardware engineers to design and test firmware in a variety of environments.
- Conduct thorough debugging and profiling of firmware code to enhance performance and address issues.
- Create and maintain comprehensive documentation related to firmware functionality, design specifications, and user manuals.
- Participate in design reviews and contribute to system architecture discussions to ensure firmware solutions align with product goals.
- Engage in unit and integration testing to validate firmware integrations within larger systems.
- Stay informed about industry trends and emerging technologies to continually improve firmware development practices and tools.

## Skill Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
- A minimum of 3 years of experience in firmware development for embedded systems.
- Proficient in C/C++ programming, with a solid understanding of low-level programming and hardware interfacing.
- Experience with debugging tools and methodologies to diagnose and resolve firmware issues.
- Familiarity with various communication protocols (e.g., CAN, Ethernet, Bluetooth) and microcontroller/microprocessor architectures.
- Strong analytical skills, with a passion for problem-solving and a detail-oriented approach to coding.

## Other Requirements
- Experience in developing firmware for IoT devices is a significant advantage.
- Knowledge of software development lifecycle methodologies, including Agile or Scrum.
- Understanding of security practices in embedded firmware development is beneficial.
- Excellent teamwork and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively in a cross-disciplinary environment.
