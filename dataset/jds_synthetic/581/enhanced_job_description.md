## Detailed Responsibilities
- Collaborate with project managers and department leaders to assess and forecast resource needs based on project timelines and requirements.
- Contribute to the design and maintenance of resource tracking systems that provide visibility into employee skills, availability, and project engagements.
- Analyze resource utilization data to identify areas for optimization, generating detailed reports to support decision-making by management.
- Communicate effectively between various teams to ensure alignment on resource availability and facilitate necessary transitions or upskilling initiatives.
- Participate in recruitment strategy sessions, supplying data-driven insights to enhance the effectiveness of talent acquisition efforts for skill gaps identified.

## Skill Requirements
- Proficient in resource planning techniques and workforce management software.
- Strong analytical skills with experience in data visualization tools or software like Microsoft Excel and Power BI.
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills to foster collaboration across departments.
- Detail-oriented with a knack for problem-solving and organizational planning.

## Other Requirements
- A degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related discipline is preferred.
- Internship or project experience in HR analytics, resource planning, or operations management is a plus.
- Understanding of workforce dynamics and employee development practices.
