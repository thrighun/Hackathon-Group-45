- Define and communicate architectural vision and design strategies for software applications.
- Translate business requirements into technical specifications and establish architecture patterns.
- Guide teams in the implementation of best practices in software development and architecture.
- Conduct architecture reviews and provide constructive feedback to ensure adherence to established guidelines.
- Collaborate with product managers, developers, and other stakeholders to develop and refine project specifications.
- Evaluate new technologies and tools, presenting recommendations based on business needs and technical feasibility.
- Oversee performance tuning, scaling, and security aspects of software applications.
- Mentor and support development teams to enhance their architectural and engineering skills.
- Facilitate communication and collaboration among cross-functional teams to ensure project alignment and success.
- Contribute to the continuous improvement of internal processes, tools, and methodologies for software development.
