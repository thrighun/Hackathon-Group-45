- Develop and maintain data warehousing solutions, ensuring efficient data storage, retrieval, and processing capabilities.
- Perform data quality checks and validation to ensure data accuracy and completeness across various data sources.
- Work closely with cross-functional teams to gather requirements and deliver data solutions that meet business needs.
- Optimize data processing workflows for performance, scalability, and cost efficiency in cloud environments.
